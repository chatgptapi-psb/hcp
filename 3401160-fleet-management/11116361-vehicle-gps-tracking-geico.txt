How to Check if You Qualify for a Commercial Auto Insurance Discount through Geico

Housecall Pro customers may qualify for a discounted rate on commercial auto insurance policies through Geico.

---

How much can you save?

This depends on a number of factors, including the number of vehicles and the location. Qualifying customers can save up to 10% on their commercial auto policy every year!

---

Here’s how to determine your eligibility:

- First, sign up for Vehicle Tracking and/or Dashcams through Housecall Pro. - After 30 days of driving with either Vehicle Tracking or Dashcams, you will qualify for a discounted commercial auto insurance policy quote. To get started with Vehicle Tracking or Dashcams, navigate to the app store in your Housecall Pro account.

- Ensure your account meets the eligibility criteria. - You must have no more than 10 vehicles that are active in Vehicle Tracking and/or Dashcams.
- Your business is located in one of the following 26 states:
 - AL, AR, AZ, CO, CT, FL, GA, ID, IL, IN, MA, MD, ME, MN, MO, NH, NM, OH, OK, OR, SC, TN, TX, VA, VT, WI
 - More states will be added soon.

- Complete enrollment for your new GEICO commercial policy. - After 30 days of using VGPS or Dashcams, you'll get an in-app banner on the Force Fleet Tracking web app outlining next steps to get your discounted commercial auto insurance policy quote from GEICO.

---

Here's how to enroll through the Force Web app:

- Navigate to the Force Web app.

- Click "Learn More" on the blue banner at the top of the Force Fleet Tracking web app.

FAQs

What if I meet the qualifying criteria but don't see the in-app banner?

- Ensure you check that you meet the qualifying criteria above. If you don't see the banner, feel free to reach out to our support team at Housecall Pro.

What if I’ve been a Vehicle Tracking customer at Housecall Pro for over 30 days, and I want to switch my auto insurance policy?

- You can still switch over to your new auto policy and cancel your previous one.
