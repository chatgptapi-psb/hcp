What is Coach AI?

Being a business owner comes with challenges, and that’s where Coach AI steps in. We’re here to provide business guidance, hiring advice, and problem-solving support to help you succeed. With Coach AI, you can access expert insights and practical solutions tailored to your unique business needs.

Here are just a few examples of the great questions you can ask our Coach AI team:

- How can I generate more jobs for my business?
- When is the right time to hire?
- What can I do to increase my profitability?
- How can I improve my online presence?
- What strategies can I use to get the phone to ring?
- How can I increase my average ticket size?

Coach AI on Business Insights

Coach AI can also be found on the Business Insights tab by proactively providing you with suggestions and updates.

These suggestion prompts are specific to your business goals, revenue, metrics, and more, which help provide you with personalized business strategies to help you better achieve your objectives.

This can be found in the upper right, purple section with the button "Tell me more."

Coach AI will provide you with a proactive prompt about your business.

Click "Tell me more" to initiate a chat with Coach AI. This will open a chat in the right rail where Coach AI will respond to the proactive prompt with a response that includes tactics uniquely tailored to your business.
