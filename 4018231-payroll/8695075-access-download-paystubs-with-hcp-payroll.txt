Summary: Learn how to access and download paystubs with HCP Payroll.

How to Give Employees & Contractors Access to Paystubs

To provide employees and contractors access to their paystubs:

- Click on the 'Payroll' tab in the navigation bar at the top of your Housecall Pro account. - Depending on the size of your browser window, you may need to click 'More' and then select 'Payroll' from the drop-down.

- Select 'Employees & contractors' from the menu on the left.

How to Download Paystubs from the HCP Mobile App

Employees and contractors who have an active HCP user login can access and download their paystubs directly from the HCP mobile app by following the steps below:

- Tap the 3 horizontal lines icon in the top left corner of your HCP mobile app, then select 'Payroll' from the menu options.
