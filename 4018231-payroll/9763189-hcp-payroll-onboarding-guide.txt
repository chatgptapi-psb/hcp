HCP Payroll Overview

Managing payroll doesn't have to be complicated. With HCP Payroll, you can run payroll directly from the same platform where you manage your business—eliminating the need to switch between multiple apps.

Streamline your process with integrated Time Tracking and give employees easy, self-service access to their pay stubs. Plus, enjoy seamless onboarding and ongoing support from a dedicated Housecall Pro specialist to ensure your payroll experience is smooth and efficient.

Housecall Pro manages all payroll functions internally, offering greater transparency, control, and accuracy when processing employee compensation.

HCP Payroll has everything you need to pay and manage your team, and we've got you covered every step of the way!

HCP Payroll Features

- Full-service payroll (including W-2s & 1099s)
- Flexible earnings (bonuses, commissions, PTO)
- Free payroll tax filings
- Downloadable payroll reports
- Quickbooks integration
- Employee Benefits (Workers Compensation, Health Insurance)
- Integrated Time Tracking
- Compliance Alerts*
- HR tools and templates*

- Year to Date Payroll Historical Report (sometimes referred to as a Payroll Journal Report). - This should include a breakdown of the following information for each employee and contractor for each pay period in the current calendar year:
 - Employee Names
 - Pay Periods
 - Payday
 - Taxes
 - Deductions

How to Downgrade or Cancel Your HCP Payroll Plan

If you need to transition to a lower-tiered subscription plan for your HCP account, please reach out to the HCP Payroll team directly through the Blue Chat Bubble in your Housecall Pro account.

Should you need to cancel your HCP payroll subscription, please inform us accordingly and prepare the responses to the following inquiries:

- Contact name

- Business name

- Effective date to cancel service

- Address for future correspondence

- Reason for cancellation

- Do you need us to file your Q3, year-end, or W-2 payroll returns, or will you be switching to a new payroll provider who will handle these filings on your behalf?

- Do you need any historical payroll reports of what has been processed with HCP?

- Do you need to process any more payroll through HCP before your payroll is terminated?

---

Optional One-Time Tax Filing Service for Canceled Payroll Accounts

If you decide to cancel your HCP Payroll subscription, you can still have Housecall Pro handle your tax filings for a one-time service fee. This option is available for former Pros who need assistance completing their annual, quarterly, or per-employee payroll tax filings after cancellation.

Service Details

- Flat Fee: $150 per filing (annual, quarterly, or per-employee)

- Eligibility: Available to Pros who have canceled their HCP Payroll plan but need Housecall Pro to file outstanding or end-of-year payroll taxes.

- Included Services: - Federal, state, and local payroll tax filings.
- W-2, 1099, and related form submissions (if applicable)
- Compliance verification and record updates.

How to Request One-Time Filing

- Contact the HCP Payroll team through the Blue Chat Bubble in your Housecall Pro account or by emailing payrollsupport@housecallpro.com.

- Provide the following details: - Business name and contact information
- Filing period(s) you need assistance with
- Number of employees or contractors (if applicable)

- A member of our team will confirm your request, collect the required data, and provide payment instructions for the one-time filing service.
