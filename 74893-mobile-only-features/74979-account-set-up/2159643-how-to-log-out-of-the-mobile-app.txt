Summary: logging out of the mobile app

Log out of the Android App

You will want to click on the 3 bars in the top left corner of your app.

Once you get there you should see your email right below your picture. You can click on the email and it will drop down the option to log out of the app.

Log out of the iPhone App

To log out of the Housecall Pro app on your iPhone, follow the steps below.

Click 'More' in the bottom right of your Dashboard

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/108426762/8da02514bb9aa5429816665d/Screen_Shot_2019-03-12_at_12_21_57_PM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=ca38770a47ceda762f89c148fcc3425a9f860b993d7a752039f4f61844a9a662&req=dSAvEst4moddFb4f3HP0gLFGtp0FD3SaQG8z7SgNB3JpIPgIQkioHYaSwheW%0AqVQntjinecjKB0jPeA%3D%3D%0A

Scroll down to the bottom and click 'Log Out'
