Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/9014725-job-inbox-integration-with-trane
Author: Mathew H
Last updated: Updated over a month ago

Summary: Directly integrate Trane leads into your Housecall Pro account.

Trane Integration Overview

Trane is a trusted HVAC brand that connects homeowners with qualified service providers through its leads and marketing program. With the Trane integration in Job inbox, new leads from Trane’s webforms automatically appear in your account, complete with contact details and service requests.

---

---

Enable the Trane Integration in Job Inbox

- Click the My Apps icon (3x3 square) in the top right corner of your HCP account on web, then select the Job inbox app to view your Job inbox settings.

Getting Trane Leads in Job Inbox

Once the integration is set up, all new leads from Trane will be automatically created as Leads, and you'll be notified of each one by a message in your Job inbox.
