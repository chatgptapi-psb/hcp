Summary: How to best handle customers with multiple locations

Adding Multiple Addresses

There are two ways to set up customers who have multiple service locations in Housecall Pro: multiple addresses (under one customer) and individual sub-customers.
​

Adding multiple addresses to a customer will keep all of the addresses for that customer under one profile, so any text or email notifications, invoices, estimates, and receipts will all go to the same person.

This option works best for customers with multiple service locations, but where you only communicate with a single point of contact for all of them. For example, a commercial company that schedules services at various sites but coordinates everything through the main office.

- If you are communicating and invoicing everything to one customer for multiple locations, follow the steps below.

- If you are communicating with one customer, but invoicing another (i.e. property management companies and tenants), you will need to set up a sub-customer (parent-child relationship) instead.

---

How to Add Multiple Addresses (Web Portal)

To add multiple addresses to a customer's profile:

- Click the Customers tab in your navigation bar and open the Customer Profile.

- Ensure that you're on the Profile tab and click the '+' icon under the map on the right side of the page.

- Type the new address into the Add Address window.

- Check the box next to Set as billing if you'd like to save this as the billing address for the customer.

- Click Add in the bottom right corner to save, and that's it!

- From here, you can click the More icon (3 vertical dots) to the right of the address to: - Create a new job
- Create a new estimate
- Add a new property item
- Edit the address (includes address notes)
- Delete the address

Creating Jobs and Estimates for Customers with Multiple Addresses

To create a job or estimate for a customer with multiple addresses added to their profile:

- Click the Customers tab in your navigation bar and open the Customer Profile.

- In the top right-hand corner of the customer profile, select the + Job or + Estimate button to create a new Job or Estimate.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1720898680/e201ac6b676200c40e4f08a545c3/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=ef8088c850a142d6df3b022132081ee3a48a9283194aebb38b9f3d638d8a7d1a&req=dSclFsF3lYdXWfMW1HO4zU%2FCkm7uo8f7xpxKUL6p%2BhXG5XZ9%2FmsLFvPo8fd0%0AJPnrCCJWQZktSGDs7EU%3D%0A

- In the next window, choose either the Job address or the Estimate address from the list of addresses saved in the customer’s profile.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1720905662/e6815187563a6ce1bb5b288768c5/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=0b1a5fc3c29cd695b7706e2f1fafd95c2dea6469cb09be91294563f8a158bca2&req=dSclFsB%2BmIdZW%2FMW1HO4zQAY9z76wphdFvLPw7%2BDSn00%2BomINtqgSMwZJbhr%0ANnI7wi5tIV%2FFAO8yNDE%3D%0A

- The address you select will automatically be set as the Service Address for the Job or Estimate and will also appear when the Invoice or Estimate is sent to the customer.

How to Add Multiple Addresses (iOS)

To add multiple addresses to a customer profile from the HCP mobile app on iOS (iPhone/iPad), follow the steps below:

- Tap the 'Customers' icon at the bottom of the mobile app and select a customer from the list.
