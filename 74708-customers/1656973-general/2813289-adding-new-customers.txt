Add a New Customer (HCP Web Portal)

To add a new customer through the HCP web portal:

- Click the New button in the top navigation bar of your HCP account.

- Select Customer from the drop-down, and the "Add new customer" window will appear.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1364971936/7060d9bc61e211da408e85cd738f/Screenshot+2025-02-04+at+11_51_07%E2%80%AFAM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=84fc0178ce260b597fe00644727154265df40b6b5fb2280a4a3a48eaafa8d541&req=dSMhEsB5nIhcX%2FMW1HO4zZDYdRWfJXJy1Dg9KvDZmAlQFOYh6P0ByNMi4txv%0Ab%2FCyyG0CW9MhBNJ03aY%3D%0A

- In the Contact Info section, enter the following information: - First and Last Name (Contact)
- Display Name (shown on invoices)
- Email address
 - Additional emails can be entered by clicking the '+ Email' button
- Mobile/Home/Work Phone
 - Additional phone numbers can be entered by clicking the '+ Phone' button (you can also add a note related to the additional number)
- Company & Role (if applicable)
- Select whether this customer is a Homeowner or a Business
- Mark as 'Do not service' (if applicable)

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1365068093/fce603697296a832be6426abeece/Screenshot+2025-02-04+at+11_54_23%E2%80%AFAM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=45b1b64d2667bf89911328d8f3122337d33563a46f25e327e7aa4abd4819fe75&req=dSMhE8l4lYFWWvMW1HO4zQdIoULvERhiuRawsJlVJo3sIHwBt5s7oES3x1XB%0A8LSiSmMZh9pdaUxnosE%3D%0A

- In the Address section, start typing the address in the 'Street' field, and select the correct address from the drop-down list. - Click '+Address' in the grey bar to add multiple addresses.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1365278880/fe52e211fba9ebbe6cea726e8fe0/NewCustomerAddress1.gif?expires=1763568000&signature=acf34879de86a9d9acc457fb3606ce1e4d17d640742f4314783fd9b50a2f6efa&req=dSMhE8t5lYlXWfMW1HO4zVsAmezC45zbXhMdC2Pa7splq3qThh5rI8MfEsAl%0AmIPV8HkTEtnSpV9BZR8%3D%0A

- The Notes section consists of the following fields: - Customer Notes 
 - These are for internal use only and act as a reminder or notice to yourself and/or your employees.
- Customer Tags
 - Tags are internal identifiers that remain attached to the customer until they are edited/deleted.
- 'This customer bills to' 
 - This is where you will set up parent-child/sub-customer relationships. 
 - You will first need to create a customer profile for both parties. 
 - Then, in the sub-customer/child's profile, you will type in the name of the party being invoiced in this section and select from the drop-down.
 - The sub-customer will receive the notifications, but the parent will be sent the invoice 
- Lead Source
 - This field allows you to track where your customers found your business without having to use tags.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1365284596/89c96af0bc9cee79bcda4bb02ae4/Screenshot+2025-02-04+at+11_55_58%E2%80%AFAM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=e7e6fbc5e342f9a6715bc6bb1d1fdf571ca06b090b5910b8114a6361d2aa09e5&req=dSMhE8t2mYRWX%2FMW1HO4zY53ogDB56HNPD174Y9JSXjpARS0hCrnlTvaSqnB%0A55Nv09YotJMbzmS0r4E%3D%0A

- "Referred by" section: - If applicable, you can enter a referring customer's name in the "Referred by" field and select their profile from the drop-down menu.

- Check the "Send notifications" option in the bottom left corner of the window if you'd like this customer to receive notifications when: - A job or estimate is marked as 'Scheduled,' 'On My Way,' or 'Finished.'
- A payment has been made on a job/invoice.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1365290377/a49428b1b43a4a810ccb729e2244/Screenshot+2025-02-04+at+3_17_44%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=5dbddc884ac5b21e354f066ecf9f307092a19d1f37331e6ffc80bdf7ebe51e2a&req=dSMhE8t3nYJYXvMW1HO4zf4iXYlLEX4CLVsFsFzBadn53Jh438o9VFDUsI44%0Ak994O3a%2FregDIYFTx4g%3D%0A

- When you're ready, click "Create Customer" in the bottom right corner of the window, and your new customer will be added to your Customer List.

Selecting a Customer Type

When adding a new customer, you can select what type of customer they are. You can choose whether the customer is a Homeowner or a Business to help you differentiate between residential and commercial jobs.

We will default the customer to Homeowner unless otherwise indicated during customer creation. You can also change this at any time by editing your customer profile.

How to Add Multiple Emails for an Existing Customer

You can store multiple email addresses for a single customer if the customer has more than one contact email. This will allow you to send both invoices and estimates to each of those emails automatically.

To add multiple email addresses for the same customer:

- Click on the Customers tab in your navigation bar at the top of the page.

- Navigate to the Customer Profile that you need to add the email to, and ensure that the Profile tab is selected.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1355206276/6804bff59c858c6e53b12705dfb3/Housecall_Pro.jpg?expires=1763568000&signature=358f22a0574e1e1c278004410a9253cad7e7eb3ed980b53ce122450f3ca4abd1&req=dSMiE8t%2Bm4NYX%2FMW1HO4zZqpTUYD4JM6CqUZhLg0GEVvLONvf%2BevPN%2FyydCr%0A6uwDP05NJHKqDghDvCs%3D%0A

- Click the pencil icon next to Contact Info. Click the blue '+ EMAIL' button to add another email address. Repeat this process for any additional email addresses.

- Click the 'Update Contact Info' button in the bottom right to save the information.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1355206277/44b3745595f308879fc170154d1e/Housecall_Pro.jpg?expires=1763568000&signature=ad004ec2442d1e261406c1faebd44cc1872de6cb5fbf1439686d6096461b7f8a&req=dSMiE8t%2Bm4NYXvMW1HO4zcB1%2BArUB1pGbGqbVf5ZZD0s3URZLTUPcyw99L1V%0Ao4%2BvdPbvhNJ%2F6j1bsUw%3D%0A

- Log into the app and click the 'Customer' icon in the bottom right corner of your screen.

- Click the '+' button located in the upper right-hand corner of the screen.

- Select 'Import Contact(s)' OR '+ New Customer.'

Add Customers from Your Google Contacts

If your contacts are saved on your computer, you can import all of them from Google Contacts. If you do not use Gmail, you can still add your customer list from contacts on your computer by first importing them into Google Contacts. You will need a Gmail account to do this.

- Click on the 'Customers' tab located in your navigation bar
- Select the 'Customers' tab located in the left sidebar menu
- On the Customers page, select 'Actions' then select 'Import' from the drop-down menu

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1144652295/3bb61914463418fcdfb3e2ea/Screenshot+2024-08-13+at+3_24_00%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=d78227ef221a991878bc98cb9480311043ac01b395fcfe463fcab0110c61cebf&req=dSEjEs97n4NWXPMW1HO4zTuxZH%2FXRZzVyalatlYihxmB5jiVGL1xsqGUlfsV%0AiJ8lZFRnERrXVtUZV78%3D%0A

- Click on 'Google Contacts' and select 'Next' which is located in the bottom right-hand corner of the page.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1144652388/c010014fbeef4dd688db2de0/Screenshot+2024-08-13+at+3_24_37%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=6a59560568d2f6766025d403c8969e8514706824b0349a7aba0451b4ac7cbb16&req=dSEjEs97n4JXUfMW1HO4zYL%2Fp3mpSDZWQmn4zpCKOLHlKvTflFlBvE6Gq6MS%0AYFmGh58lwDqrK3svS68%3D%0A

- You will be asked to choose the Google account to connect and give access to Housecall Pro to see and import contacts. We do not save your Google account information or have any access other than importing your contacts.

- You will then see a drawer on the bottom of the screen showing all of your contacts. You can either select them individually or all at once.

- Click the "Add Customers" button in the bottom right-hand corner to add your contacts and your contacts will be imported immediately.
