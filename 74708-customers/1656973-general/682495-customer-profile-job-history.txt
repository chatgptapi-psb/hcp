Summary: How to view a customer's job history from the Customer Profile.

View a Customer's Job History

To view a customer's job history in the HCP web portal:

- Open a Customer Profile.

- Select the Jobs tab.

- Here, you'll see the list of all jobs linked to that customer.

Optional: Customize job history columns

- Click the Select columns to view button (3 bar icon) above the list on the right to edit which columns are displayed: - # (job number)
- Job created date
- Job scheduled for
- Job completed date
- Customer (display name)
- Address 
- Description
- Lead source
- Amount (total price of the job)
- Status (of the job)
- Payment (status)
- Notes
- Employees
- Tags

Optional: Filter job history

- Click the Filters icon to narrow the list by job scheduled date or by lead source.
