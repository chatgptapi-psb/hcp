Set Up Sub-Customers (Parent/Child Billing)

Author: Angel A
Last updated: Updated over 9 months ago

Summary: Complete work at a service location, but bill to a different party or location

Sub-Customers Overview

Anyone set as a sub-customer is an individual customer in the database. They have their own contact information on file and are simply linked to the appropriate "parent" customer.

Parent/child billing works best in situations where you're communicating with the person at the service location, but the actual payment will come from a different party. For example, a tenant might schedule a job, but the homeowner is responsible for the payment.

Automated notifications (job scheduled, "on my way," and job completed) are sent to the sub-customer, while invoices, estimates, and receipts are sent to the parent customer to be billed.

---

How to Set Up a Sub-Customer

You can easily set up sub-customers (parent/child relationship) when creating your customer database.

- If you are communicating with one customer, but invoicing another (i.e. property management companies and tenants), follow the steps below.

- If you are communicating and invoicing everything to one customer for multiple locations, you will need to set up multiple addresses instead.

To set up a sub-customer in your HCP account:

- When creating a new child customer, scroll to the Notes section of the 'Add new customer' window.

- In the "This customer bills to" field, type in the existing parent/billing party's name, email, address, or phone number and select their profile from the drop-down list.
- When you click 'Create Customer,' this setting will be saved.

If you happen to create the sub-customer first, you can still update this setting within their customer profile once you have added the parent account:

- Click on the pencil icon next to 'Contact Info', and edit the same "This customer bills to" section.
- Make sure to click 'Update' to save this change for future billing purposes.
- If you would like to remove or replace a parent customer, you can simply click the 'X' next to the customer name and either enter a new parent customer or leave the field blank.
