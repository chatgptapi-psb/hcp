How many minutes have I used?

You can check your usage in the Billing section of your settings or in the top right corner of your HCP Assist Call Log.

---

When can I upgrade/downgrade my HCP Assist plan?

For Upgrades:

- You can change your plan at any time during the billing cycle.

For Downgrades:

- You can schedule a downgrade to start on the first day of your next billing cycle. Downgrades cannot be processed mid-cycle.

---

How do I change my HCP Assist plan?

Our team is more than happy to discuss your usage and recommend the best plan that fits your usage! To do this, you can contact us in a few ways:

- Send a reply message via the HCP Assist call log.

- Go to the Blue Chat Bubble > General Support > HCPA Answering Service.

- Call us at 858-842-5746 M-F 8AM-8PM EST, Sat-Sun 11AM-7PM EST.
