Summary: Make sure your Housecall Pro account is running as smoothly as possible by using our recommended browser, Google Chrome.

Why We Recommend Google Chrome

Google Chrome is the most widely used browser worldwide, and for good reason. With its powerful feature set, extensive extension library, and reliable mobile apps, Chrome has become the foundation on which most modern websites are built.

At Housecall Pro, Chrome is our preferred browser because it consistently leads the way in adopting the latest web technologies. It is also the only browser that fully supports the advanced features our platform uses.

While our engineering team works hard to provide a seamless experience across all browsers, we have encountered a few issues with alternatives that are difficult to resolve. Because our engineers test and optimize primarily in Chrome, switching to Chrome is often the best first step when troubleshooting unexpected behavior.

That said, we don't require our Pros to use Chrome exclusively. However, if certain new features in Housecall Pro aren't supported by other browsers, we may recommend using Chrome to ensure you get the complete experience.
