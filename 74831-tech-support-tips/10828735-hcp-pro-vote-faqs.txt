Summary: A guide on how Housecall Pro uses your submitted feedback.

Pro Vote Overview

The Pro Vote system gives users the chance to influence Housecall Pro’s feature development. It’s a feedback and suggestion box where users can upvote features they want prioritized. The most supported ideas are more likely to be built.

---

Pro Vote FAQs

How do I access the Product Roadmap to submit feedback in the form of a Pro Vote?

- Click the Blue Chat Bubble in the bottom right corner of your HCP account.

- Then, select Customer Support to open the Blue Chat Bubble window.

- On the Home tab in the BCB window, click on the Check out our latest product updates section to open and access the Product Roadmap.

How can I submit my feedback in the form of a Pro Vote?

- Go to the Public Feedback Tab on the Product Roadmap - Enter a title and description for your suggestion
- Submit it to appear publicly for upvotes

- Message our support team through the Blue Chat Bubble

- Post in our Facebook community, and a community manager can submit it for you.

Why are Pro Votes important?

- They influence what our team works on, allowing us to prioritize high-demand features and build what matters most to Pros. Voting fosters community discussion and helps shape the Product Roadmap.

How long does it take for a Pro Voted feature to be built?

- Timing varies based on complexity, resources, and demand. We don’t guarantee specific release dates, but encourage voting to help prioritize development.

How do features move from Pro Votes to development?

- Once a feature gains support, product managers evaluate it based on feasibility, complexity, and resources before moving it into development.

Why don’t all Pro Votes get built?

- Some suggestions can’t be implemented due to: - Technical limitations – Major infrastructure changes required.
- Resource allocation – Competing priorities in development.
- Regulatory constraints – Compliance issues with security or privacy.

How can I track my Pro Vote?

- Check the Product Roadmap for updates on Pro Voted features.

How does Housecall Pro announce completed Pro Votes?

- Pro Vote Spotlights
- Product release updates
- Email newsletters
- Facebook posts
- Community lives with the Product Managers

What makes a good Pro Vote?

- Write a clear, actionable request that benefits many home service businesses. - Example: “Allow payments on estimates upon approval without converting to a job.”

What makes a challenging Pro Vote?

- Vague, overly niche, or unrealistic requests. - Example: “Make everything easier.”

What is the Product Roadmap?

- This is a public page where you can track features in development, recently released updates, and submit feedback.

How do I use the Product Roadmap?

- Building List – Features currently in development
- Shipped List – Recently released updates
- Click a feature card for more details, votes, and the option to comment

How do I create an account to submit a pro-vote?

- Sign in using Google for easy access without creating a separate account.

How do I get more votes on my Pro Vote?

- After submitting, copy your Pro Vote’s URL link and share it in the community by making a post and encouraging others to upvote.
