Pro Power Hour: Product Training Webinars
Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/9721968-pro-power-hour-product-training-webinars
Author: Lauren G.
Last updated: Updated today

Summary: A feature-focused solution for your company's training needs

Welcome to Pro Power Hour!

What: Add some power to your account in just one hour! Join us for Pro Power Hour: A new feature-focused training series in the HCP Facebook group to help Pros with training. Exclusive call with onboarding specialists, community managers, and on occasion Product managers who will discuss the week's feature, take feedback, and answer questions (ideally on the week’s topic but we will take any product questions). It will be a refresher for older pros and an opportunity for new Pros to come and learn.

Who: Community manager Chris Weldon is joined by our expert partner Phill Spignola from the Housecall Pro Onboarding Specialist team.

Why: You asked for more training, so we developed this weekly webinar to dive into features one at a time!

When: Thursdays at 2 pm PST/5 pm EST

Where: On Zoom & In Our Housecall PROs Facebook Group.

Sign Up for Zoom Webinar (Sign up once and be automatically registered for all future sessions): https://housecallpro.zoom.us/webinar/register/WN_4IM-wAyzQNyT1MqE0Q7jSw#/registration

Join the 25,000+ Member Facebook Group: https://www.facebook.com/groups/HousecallPros
