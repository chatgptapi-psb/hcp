Navigating the Blue Chat Bubble

If you have a question while working in your account, the Blue Chat Bubble is the quickest way to reach our Support team.

You'll find the Blue Chat Bubble in the bottom-right corner of most pages within your account.

---

How to Access Support

To access Support:

- Click the Blue Chat Bubble in the bottom right corner of your HCP account on web.

Blue Chat Bubble Options

After opening the Blue Chat Bubble, you'll have the following options:

- Send us a message - Start a new Support chat

- Search for help - Search for answers in our Help Center

- Check out our latest product updates

- View your past conversations to refer to similar questions, or pick up where you left off with a representative.

How to Get Back to the Main Menu

- If you are in a conversation and need to get back to the main menu, click the back arrow in the top left corner of the chat.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1411798830/b164ffbad3e83045180506ee8a73/Screenshot+2025-03-07+at+12_25_33%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=9ecac2a96b941ae1d1eac3fe2b576bf029b8443fc728983a8f917f3fd6d49380&req=dSQmF853lYlcWfMW1HO4zSG6LbBNgIoU1YZtS1krZuQOfWY5%2F9swayyA1V2B%0AjiO5MNBhG7lGMBhL5bI%3D%0A

- If you are ever in a chat and need to close the Blue Chat Bubble window completely, you can click the X icon in the top right corner.

- If you receive a message after closing the Blue Chat Bubble window, the message will pop up in the lower right-hand corner of your screen.

How to Download Chat Transcripts (HCP Web Portal)

To download a chat transcript:

- Open the Blue Chat Bubble in your Housecall Pro account.

- Select Messages in the menu at the bottom to view your previous chats.

- Select a chat from your history.

- Click the More icon (3 dots) in the top right corner of the chat window.

- Select Download transcript and the chat transcript will be saved to your computer as a text file (.txt).

Chat Support on iOS

Chat with our Support team through the mobile app to get your questions answered and any other help you may need.

As long as you have Admin-level permissions, you can chat in for Support help via the mobile app (as well as the web portal).

To chat with Support through your iPhone, follow the steps below:

- Click More on the bottom right of your mobile app Dashboard.
