Employee Training Checklists

Use these training checklists to ensure that your Office Staff and Field Tech employees know how to use Housecall Pro for their roles in your business.

You can copy and paste each list to your own document, or click on the images below to save them to your computer and print them out.

---

Field Tech Training Checklist

- Download the Housecall Pro mobile app
- Login using credentials provided by employer
- Watch the Field Tech Training Video
- Learn how to clock in/out (if applicable)
- Be assigned a practice/test job
- Press On My Way
- Press Start
- Take a photo for a job
- Add a note for a job
- Press Finish
- Send an invoice to yourself
- Remember to press On My Way, Start, & Finish on EVERY Job/Estimate

Office Staff Training Checklist

- Access https://www.housecallpro.com/
- Log in using credentials provided by employer
- Watch the Office Staff Training Video
- Download the Housecall Pro mobile app & log in
- Create and schedule a fake/test job
- Press On My Way, Start, and then Finish
- Send yourself an invoice
- Mark the job as paid
- Find a customer from your customer list
- Explore a customer profile and learn to update their contact info or address
- Check out the Help Center at https://www.help.housecallpro.com/
