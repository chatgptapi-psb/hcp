Summary: Discover effective methods to adjust service prices using the service price adjuster tool within your Price Book.

How to Make Service Price Adjustments

To make service price adjustments to your Price Book:

- Click the gear icon in the top right corner of your HCP account to go to Settings.

- Select Price Book from the Settings menu on the left.

- Click the Services tab on the Price Book settings page.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1643566653/691e5f67ff4362351ba2d0f704fc/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=4c6b58ce1aebe50b71f3a4180d39bac331fe6ea1c6e546a704a64cbef30cee4e&req=dSYjFcx4m4daWvMW1HO4zUg0Oyn9C%2Fpip%2Boz%2BGt8nGUPd4T5Lt5D9jhod0rf%0ALN4BpkbNRjeQd62uQ9I%3D%0A

- Select Increase or Decrease under the Adjustment section to modify prices.

- Enter the percentage for the change in the Amount field.

- If you want to round prices to the nearest dollar, check the box next to Round to Nearest Dollar

- Under Apply To, you can choose Apply to All Services to apply changes to all services, or select specific price book industries and/or categories by clicking the > icon and checking the relevant options.

- Once you have set your service price adjustment and you have set whether you want to round to the nearest dollar, press Save.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1647616711/5d2d5740faf101eb28db335df1c8/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=672534cabd0c6876f112bbdf5e356e77751e68057352c7d498fcf7f5eb3d2c1d&req=dSYjEc9%2Fm4ZeWPMW1HO4zTSGtT8e%2FQKNyBHrOvYnTg6w7A%2FXGB28unkfXnHB%0ACSdaxETgE7CnHvE95NE%3D%0A

- Next, review the price adjustment changes you plan to make to your price book services. A pop-up will appear with the message: Adjust pricing? This will increase pricing for the selected services.

- Click Apply Changes to save your adjustments.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1643607306/ba7b3cf3d1af2ab229f354caadfd/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=73865ac2efdcc0dbc70b360b86b9a35d0313236b0497d53640d0c19dcdf42914&req=dSYjFc9%2BmoJfX%2FMW1HO4zZYPHb5Ne48VkiD81THFDXqjRcWgZFNwu3tVdxio%0AzGT7dEv0GgNj10dDRfg%3D%0A

- Depending on how many services you are looking to adjust, this can take some time.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1643611451/a7b3a00e777426e75096ec10b9ac/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=63140f510d0bd9b37be80f11f60b675f55a91844d3ba90a25a94678e3b368094&req=dSYjFc9%2FnIVaWPMW1HO4zaj5hIRKhUo4tCZ19zldJVuiBpQ8FanB9lbW7vVQ%0AIHePNryCrrrLRNINQ3E%3D%0A

- Finally, you will receive an email from notifications@housecallpro.com with the subject Price Book Adjustment Complete once the changes have been made. Click View Price Book in the email to review your updated Price Book.
