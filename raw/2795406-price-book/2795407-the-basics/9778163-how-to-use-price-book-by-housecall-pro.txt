What is the Price Book by Housecall Pro?

The Price Book by Housecall Pro is a free collection of common services tailored for your industry. These services come with detailed descriptions aimed at communicating the value you offer to homeowners.

---

How to Access Your Price Book

To access the Price book by Housecall Pro:

- Go to Price book in the top navigation bar of your Housecall Pro account on web.

Navigating the Price Book by Housecall Pro

When you access the Price book by Housecall Pro:

- Industries: Categories are organized into a pre-determined industry. (e.g., Plumbing, HVAC, Electrical)
- Categories: Services are organized into relevant categories. (e.g., Condenser, Filters, Refrigerant) Click on any category to view the available services.
- Services: Each service includes a homeowner-friendly description designed to showcase the value of your work.
- Book Online Today Category: We’ve pre-enabled these services for online booking. To make them available, just complete the online booking setup when you're ready. Customizing Your Price Book

The Price book by Housecall Pro allows you to customize your services:

- Edit Service Descriptions: Click on any service to modify the description to better fit your offerings

- Enter Your Pricing: Since pricing is not included in the Free Price Book, you’ll need to enter your own pricing for each service.

- Add New Services: If you provide a service not listed in the Free Price Book, you can add it by selecting “Add Service” and filling out the relevant details.

- Add image: Click on any service and add a customized photo.

---

Using the Price book in Estimates and Jobs

You can use the Price book by Housecall Pro to streamline your estimates and jobs:

- Select services from the Price book when creating an estimate to auto-populate descriptions.

- Adjust pricing as needed for each job based on your own pricing structure.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Can I add my custom services to the Free Price book?

- Yes, you can add, edit, or remove services as needed to fit your business.

How do I update pricing for services?

- You will need to manually enter your pricing for each service in your Price Book.
