Adding, Editing, and Removing Tax Rates

Create and manage tax rates for your service items and invoices. You can set up multiple tax rates, each with a visible description for your customers. These tax rates can be easily edited or deleted as needed.

After creating your tax rates, you can apply them directly to job invoices to ensure accurate and transparent billing.

---

How to Add, Edit, or Remove a Tax Rate

For new users:

- Click the gear icon in the top right corner of your HCP account to go to Settings.

- Select Price Book from the Settings menu on the left.

- Click the Tax Rates tab on the Price Book settings page.

Apply Tax Rates to Services

Once you've created your tax rates, you can:

- Make a service item always taxable (through price book)
- Apply tax on individual invoices

This will default the service item to be taxable whenever added to an invoice

- Click into your Price Book

- Select either Services or Materials.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/740832456/a440be11d08db24ca20dad71/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=7299705942a7fe47d01fda5978e35695ed95af7f3340c1724a310f5ca9d090b4&req=cyQnHsp8mYRZFb4f3HP0gJ5YvAKJaQOS1251mKr9cHchtI6CKsuGQnCAgd4U%0AI0knOrae3OQxVjeTIg%3D%3D%0A

- Then continue until you are in your desired service or material.
- Toggle on Taxable. - Blue = On
- Grey = Off

- Click the blue save button in the top right

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/740833854/56fd22355677bc8a257e3281/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=32fc113edded62620d4fee72390c887b4828cc025b03d5daa40e5d0e5b148514&req=cyQnHsp9lYRbFb4f3HP0gEkSlVaekbuKJb20Uc5lX2eIWWyr3bsG0KBoDaD%2B%0ACfl%2B6gASmG6OEhB7eQ%3D%3D%0A

The service item will now be pulled with the Tax box checked any time you add it to an invoice.

- You can turn this off for individual invoices by unchecking the box

ADD TAX TO INDIVIDUAL SERVICE ITEM ⬆

To add tax on an as-needed basis:

- Check the tax box next to the service item once in the invoice.
- Select the tax rate you'd like applied from the drop-down underneath the Subtotal line. ⬆ (see photo above) ⬆

---

Callouts

- The system does not allow you to designate a default tax rate; it defaults to the first tax rate in alphabetical order. To work around this, you may prefix a tax rate with 'A-', such as 'A-Florida Tax,' to ensure it appears first.
- Please note that only one tax rate can be applied to an invoice at a time.
