Price Book Mobile - Key Features
Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/6467989-price-book-mobile-key-features
Author: Michael Storer
Last updated: Updated over a month ago

Summary: Learn about the key features of Price Book on mobile, including access to viewing and editing materials right from your mobile app.

Key features of Price Book on Mobile

- Ability to view and create your materials on jobs and in Price Book settings
- Ability to enter the cost for your services and materials within Price Book settings
- Ability to add a task code to your services
- Ability to add a part number to your material
- Ability to add images to your services and materials items saved to your Price Book. Images can be leveraged for Visual Price Book or Online Booking services.

Price Book on Mobile Considerations

Only employees with Admin and Field Techs status with the permission 'Edit Company Info' enabled can navigate to the Price Book in the mobile settings. Please refer to our Price Book Mobile Permissions guide to understand what your employees will have access to depending on their user permissions for more in-depth information.

If you have flat rate-enabled services or materials you will want to continue managing these on the web version of Housecall Pro, to have a full view of your labor hours and markups, etc. Please refer to our Flat Rate Services on Mobile article for more information.

Price Book Settings View on Mobile

To access your Price Book on your mobile app, tap the three horizontal dots icon labeled 'More' located on the right-hand side of your app's bottom navigation bar, and then choose Price Book from the top of the menu.

- Search across your services and materials using the search bar next to the magnifying glass at the top of the screen
- Clicking into your listed categories in your Price Book will pull up further lists of sub-categories, if you have them
- Services and materials can be created within selected categories by clicking on the plus icon in the upper right-hand corner of your screen
- Services and materials can be viewed and updated by clicking into them from Price Book on mobile
- Images can be included in your pre-existing services and materials, as well as new ones

How to use Price Book on Mobile

When creating a job or estimate on the mobile app, your employees can easily search for and add services and materials from the Price Book, as well as create and customize brand-new items.

To search for an item when creating a new job, select the gray Service box under Line Items on the New Job form. From here, select + Add Services or + Add Materials to search through the Price book, navigating through categories if you have them.

How do I save a line item to the Price Book from the mobile app?

To save a line item to the price book, you need to navigate to the invoice/estimates details page.
​
From the mobile app's dashboard, there are a few ways to get to the invoice/estimates details page. You can go to the dashboard by selecting it from the navigation bar at the bottom of your screen. (Usually located on the far left).
​
You can click the schedule icon on the navigation bar at the bottom of your mobile app. (calendar icon). From there you can go to the date your estimate or job is booked for and click on it.
​
Alternatively, on the mobile app's dashboard, you can scroll down and click View My Invoices, or View My Jobs. Invoices bring you to a list of your current invoices, and the jobs bring you to the schedule.
​

For estimates, you can scroll down and click on View My Estimates. 
​

Scroll down until you see the 'Line Items' card. Click on the pencil icon in the top right to edit the line items. 
​
​

Once you are on the job/estimates detail page, scroll down until you see Line Items. Click the pencil icon. This will bring you into edit mode for your line items. On this page, you'll see your existing line items. Below the existing line items, you'll see a + button with Add Services next to it, you'll also see a + button with Add Materials next to it. 
​
Click Add Services or Add Materials
​

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1079886639/4d48b8d3e1c7008184befbd8/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=655480ed5fca8040ac2d3830a289e77014869ad631c09b0c493b6c46e043f865&req=dSAgH8F2m4dcUPMW1HO4zWgSX6J72tDkBbfdJomKb9WmHFHCwJDoANQ1%2FhK7%0A0bWvopnZ8%2BeWu11yMeE%3D%0A

After you click Add Services or Add Materials, you'll be taken to your Price Book. While on this page, to create a new line item, click the + icon in the upper right.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1079890443/68eccee1059ca8f24adede70/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=02e8d758212802c68e571775a62e2eccaeda26faa5891c843870b3164733f836&req=dSAgH8F3nYVbWvMW1HO4zeQ80JOnjmvJCegrxsMSKPLy2oXTZ9SoB9jqpbgr%0Abf1%2FupD21x4bhzX9AtU%3D%0A

A screen titled New Service line item or New Material line item will appear based on the option you chose.
​
From there you can input the service or material name, description, unit price, unit quantity, mark taxable or not, and finally at the bottom of the list there is a toggle to Save to the price book. 
​

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1079898420/32491a86692327d278a6d171/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=d30e59a5ceb00882551a096300abd9a93d2c9d2f670398d6c3c3bfa64f3ff13a&req=dSAgH8F3lYVdWfMW1HO4zRWp3YbCRQ3hygT9UV4liHE3ieEfDaxA%2Fn%2FJm0EP%0ATfsrc%2BAmld6Yq%2BRnIDg%3D%0A

Once you click save to price book the button will be highlighted to indicate you want to save the item to the price book. 
​
You'll also notice two new options below that.
​
A line named Category will appear, along with the ability for you to attach an image to this Service or Material item.
​
​

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1079904595/ca98b89252c7e54e3d7d2973/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=0518f0d7b5568c2349fc351295c552281ecfdf8e984a5f77fa12ef181b294038&req=dSAgH8B%2BmYRWXPMW1HO4zVi0oiffH9hk%2BwMQ3oytTVVpfFo0N0vuGqFY3vUy%0ANlEj8HuqpA%2BRZ5Xc6Yc%3D%0A

A list of existing categories will appear once you click Category, at the bottom of that list you'll see the option to add a New Category. 
​
If you opt to use an existing category, simply click on it, then click Done in the upper right. From there you'll be taken back to the line item edit page where you can click Save in the upper right to save all your changes to the line items.

You'll be brought back to the 'New Line Item' page with all of your information entered. To save the item, click the 'add' button in the top right.
​
​

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1079921694/eb50ac09818b20a6b072fa44/New+line+item.gif?expires=1763568000&signature=f231bed3a42d0714fc633fcfe71e5b326cbc4c3d3dcbf736df361b19bb2e9a8a&req=dSAgH8B8nIdWXfMW1HO4zSwgOpuZpu1jDamZCCrDci96sj1RLs4bsBNDzBhj%0AJStXHkztsTvoHGoDV4Y%3D%0A

Alternatively, you can create a new category by clicking the New Category button. From there, you'll be brought to a screen to type input the category's name, then you can press Save in the upper right.
​
After that, you can select the category you just created from your list of existing categories.

Use this guide to understand what permissions impact the ability to access settings related to Price Book or view the prices you have set for your services and materials.

Who has access to Price Book on mobile:

- Owner/Admins
- Office Staff
- Field Techs
