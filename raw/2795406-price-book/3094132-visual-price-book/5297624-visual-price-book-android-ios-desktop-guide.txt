Summary: Learn how to use Visual Price Book with an Estimate or Job

Accessing the Visual Price Book

Note: If you need to still set up your Price Book, you can get started in our collection of Price Book articles.

With the Visual Price Book, you can enhance your sales process by showcasing both services and materials in a clear and engaging manner. This feature includes two distinct Visual Price Books: one for services and one for materials. Each Price Book is designed to build trust with customers and streamline the job process for your employees.

Additionally, both the Service Price Book and the Material Price Book include a search bar, allowing you to quickly find specific services or materials, making it easier to add items to your estimate or job.

Accessing the Visual Service Price Book on Desktop

To add items from the Visual Price Book to your estimate or job, start by creating a new job or estimate, or by accessing the existing job or estimate you are currently working on.

In the 'Line Items' section, you will find two distinct Visual Price Books: the Service Price Book and the Material Price Book.

To add services from the Visual Service Price Book, click the blue 'Service Price Book' icon in the top right corner of the 'Services' section within 'Line Items.' This will open the Visual Service Price Book interface.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/344601592/d1e33d4f4d52f7a5082e303a/Screen+Shot+2021-06-01+at+12.34.23+PM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=522a3089907121b06905e1e9d45dddaab838ed4f55f6d014f4661e7740beff77&req=dyQjEMl%2FmIhdFb4f3HP0gIAmSrSYSLZG5KQTpsmqiYIdm2ifL6xlBeLR1VHD%0AfWvL9fWojkVB3b2xzw%3D%3D%0A

Next, select your industry from the list of available options.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/344602540/b7afc9dae1d2c215e79bda26/Screen+Shot+2021-06-01+at+12.34.45+PM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=e96d3103139c66efaa368ea6bd0ee5dbf8c32123f68bf4da310e77ce261d412e&req=dyQjEMl8mIVfFb4f3HP0gL7ER%2F24351f6xDKjxr%2FImuc42jg0oQ7zob3uqfC%0AChLFi8nJRZh5iI3%2BRw%3D%3D%0A

After choosing your industry, proceed to select the appropriate category for the service you want to add.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1126112287/b21db80edfeadb3398b8d5c3/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=67fb97c3153af29bd0317701f434c16fd006f2cc1c0848098ae74ad208d0d8bc&req=dSElEMh%2Fn4NXXvMW1HO4zazVJvs2J05YCIXeGb8X4yNCiC7K013%2FsZsAakUS%0AzjlJ5rEWqSup8DNvXBk%3D%0A

Browse through the available services in the chosen category and select the one you wish to include.

Click 'Add Item' to add multiple items at once. To track how many unique services or materials have been added, refer to the counter indicated by a wrench icon in the top right-hand corner. You can adjust the quantity of each item by typing the number manually or using the '+' and '-' signs.

Once you’ve added all your items, click the 'x' in the top right-hand corner to return to viewing your job or estimate.

​Important notes:

- The multi-add view of this feature is only available on web at the current moment.
- To remove an item you added, exit out of the Visual Price Book and click the 'x' on the line item on the invoice or estimate
