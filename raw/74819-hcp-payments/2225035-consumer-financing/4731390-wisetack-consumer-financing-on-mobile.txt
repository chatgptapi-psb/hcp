Summary: Learn about the fully integrated Wisetack consumer financing on the mobile app

Overview

Housecall Pro is proud to announce the full launch of our natively integrated consumer financing option, with Wisetack. Your customers will now have the flexibility to break the total cost of a job into smaller, more manageable payments over time, and you get paid in full as soon as the job is finished. Sell bigger ticket jobs, maintain cash flows, and give your customers the flexibility they need when it comes to payments.

In the video below, we go through the workflow on the mobile app of sending the financing application to your customer, your customer filling out the application and choosing their financing terms, and how you'll get paid for the job.

Important Reminders

- Jobs/Estimates must be within $500-$25,000 range
- You must first sign up for Wisetack and be approved before you will be able to offer financing to your customers
- When a customer fills out the application, it is only a soft pull. Their credit will not receive a ding for simply filling out the application.
