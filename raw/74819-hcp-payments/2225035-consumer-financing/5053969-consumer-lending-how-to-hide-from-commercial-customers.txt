How to Only Show Lending Options to Non-Commercial Customers

If you'd like to have lending options presented for non-commercial customers only:

- Navigate to the Customer Profile for a commercial customer.

- Click on the pencil icon in the Contact info section to edit their information.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1226861883/29a1f3b74373bc951ecbda0243dd/Customers-HCP-2B-2823-29.png?expires=1763568000&signature=e6ad7b00858ea9896e339a0b973d871a0ec4bd1e54dcb29dfa5f26802066a258&req=dSIlEMF4nIlXWvMW1HO4zSe0eH2OetwL1Hn2cfbl9Q%2F9TTNByQFoNeT%2Fq3wg%0A0FVy9YVi8V1EvjraRMg%3D%0A

- In the Edit contact info window, click the radio button next to Business.

- Click Update in the bottom right corner to save the changes.

- All future invoices sent to that customer will not have lending options presented.
