Setting Up Your M2 Card Reader

Using our card reader is fast and simple! There are many benefits, including an enhanced customer experience and ease of mind knowing that both your and their account information is secure. You will also be protected from disputes reported as fraudulent activity.

When you first receive your Card Reader:

- Fully charge it using the charging cable provided.
- Verify that the Card Reader's firmware is updated.

---

Updating Your M2 Card Reader's Firmware

You'll update your M2 card reader's firmware by going through the steps of taking a payment on your mobile device (without actually processing the payment).

- Turn on the card reader by pressing the power button. The first LED will turn on, indicating that the card reader is ready for payment.

- Select a job on your HCP mobile app. - You can create and select a test job rather than a job for a customer.

- Tap the 'Pay' icon at the top of your screen, then tap 'Next' on the Checkout screen and select 'Credit Card' as the payment type.

- Tap the 'Card Reader' button, and your device will automatically begin searching for the card reader.

- Tap 'Pair' when prompted. - For Android devices, tap 'Pair, then 'Pair & connect.'

- Once your card reader connects, the firmware update will start automatically, if needed. This should take between 2 and 10 minutes to complete.

- Once your M2 card reader has been connected and updated, you'll be prompted to run the card.

- You can then cancel the transaction.

M2 Card Reader LED Indicators

When your M2 Card Reader is turned on, the LED lights located on the top of the card reader indicate its current status.

Status Indicators:

Table:
Row 1: Lights | Meaning
Row 2: None | The reader is off
Row 3: First LED ON | Ready for payment
Row 4: LEDs ON in consecutive order | Reading card information
Row 5: 4 LEDs ON + “BEEP” | Card read successful
Row 6: First LED flashing | Reader is in standby mode

Battery Indicators:

Table:
Row 1: Lights | Meaning
Row 2: 4 steady lights | Fully charged
Row 3: 3 steady lights | 75% charged
Row 4: 2 steady lights | 50% charged
Row 5: 1 steady light | 25% charged
Row 6: 1 steady light + 1 blinking light | Charging
