How to Order Your Card Reader

To order your own Housecall Pro card reader, follow the steps below:

- Click on 'My Money' in the navigation bar at the top of your Housecall Pro account. - Depending on your screen size, you may need to click the 'More' drop-down.

- Next, select 'Card reader' from the left menu, under the Payments drop-down.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1333153164/dceabda3cf6252038e000551bcff/MyMoney-Payments-CardReader.png?expires=1763568000&signature=120b1ed078c4a82ff5fbc206079b2d66215e57dd7735c5a86a3db06b9b7f8424&req=dSMkFch7noBZXfMW1HO4zTNaicOxf8O80z1rlSCO%2B3NqT6IN4r%2BzqhMbkS1a%0AvACVatcnyzj9P98957c%3D%0A

- From here, you'll see the Card Reader screen, where you'll have the option to purchase your card reader(s). 
​
- You can change the quantity by clicking on the plus and minus icons at the top of the page.
- When you're ready, click the blue 'Buy Now' button.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1234587107/426a8eca3ed578cc9e12c7cfd1f5/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=71dfe93542e94ad195131b7633b5cd315607f92e6758543f9f6c8119e0af36b5&req=dSIkEsx2moBfXvMW1HO4zbxLEOR8Dg9gLve3udAnxGPFuPs4lv2%2B2RcvMRNp%0Auo%2BINNqoUTOwO5Tck8w%3D%0A

- From there, confirm your shipping address and billing information and click the 'Review Order' button.

- If everything looks correct on the Order Summary screen, click 'Place Order' and you're all set!

Card Reader FAQs

---

---

What payment methods work with the card reader?

- The following payment methods can be used with our card reader: - Apple Pay 
- Android Pay
- Samsung Pay
- VISA Paywave
- MasterCard PayPass
- AMEX ExpressPay
- Discover D-PAS
​

---

Does the card reader work with iPads?

- Yes! The card readers are iOS-compatible.
​

---

Can I use a reader with my Android or Android Tablet?

- Yes! Card readers can be used with Android devices. 
​

---

​

Can I use one card reader with my iPhone/iPad and Android device?

- Yep! The card reader will work the exact same way with iOS and Android devices. 
​

---

I live in Canada, will I be able to use the card reader?

- Card readers are not currently available in Canada.
​

---

​How long will the card reader battery last?

- Out of the box, we recommend fully charging your card reader before you use it in the field. When fully charged, the card reader should last at least a thousand swipes/chip reads.

- The reader automatically shuts itself off after a few idle minutes. That being said, we do still suggest charging them often to ensure the battery is full and good to go. Better safe than sorry! 
​

---

I purchased a BBPOS card reader from a different company, can I use it with Housecall Pro?

- Currently, only card readers purchased through Housecall Pro can be used with the HCP mobile app.
​

---

​Where is the Card Reader button?

- The Card Reader button is located in the HCP mobile app.

- You'll see the Card Reader button after tapping the 'Pay' icon on the Job Details page and selecting the Credit Card payment method when going through the checkout process.

How can I view the payment afterward?

- Navigate to the My Money page in your account and select 'Payouts' from the menu on the left. 
​
- From here, you can toggle between Standard Payouts, Instant Payouts, and Check Deposits. 
​
- Click on the dropdown arrow to the right of each transaction to expand the details and view card reader payments.
