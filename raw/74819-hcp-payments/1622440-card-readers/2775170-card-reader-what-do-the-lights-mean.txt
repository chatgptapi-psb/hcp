Summary: Know what the different lights on your card reader mean.

BBPOS Chipper Card Reader LED Indicators

When the BBPOS Chipper 2X BT (Black) Card Reader is turned on, the LED indicators located beside the power button show the card reader's current status.

Table:
Row 1: Status Light | Meaning
Row 2: None | The reader is powered off
Row 3: Flashing blue every second | The reader is on & ready to connect. (Will turn off after 5 minutes).
Row 4: Multi-colored flashing | The reader has been discovered using Bluetooth Proximity and is ready to connect.
Row 5: Steady blue | The reader is connected to a device.
Row 6: Flashing blue every 5 seconds | The reader is in Standby mode. (Will remain in Standby indefinitely).
Row 7: Alternating red and magenta | The reader is charging.
Row 8: Flashing red | The reader's battery is low.
Row 9: Rapidly flashing blue and orange | The reader has finished installing a software update. If the reader is unresponsive after the update completes, restart the reader by turning it off and on.
