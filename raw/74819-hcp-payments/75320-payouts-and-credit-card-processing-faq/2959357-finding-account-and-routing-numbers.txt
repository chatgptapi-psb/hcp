Where to Find Your Account and Routing Numbers

There are a couple of places you can find your account and routing numbers:

- Your Bank's Online Banking Portal
​ - You should be able to find your account and routing number by logging into your online banking account (that you should have set up through your bank).
​ 
- If you're having trouble locating it within your bank's online portal, try searching "routing number" in the search bar within the portal.

- Checks
​ - If you have a checkbook, you can find your account and routing numbers at the bottom of a check.
