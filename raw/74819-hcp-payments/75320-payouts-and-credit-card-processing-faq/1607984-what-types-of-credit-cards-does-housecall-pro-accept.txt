What types of credit cards does Housecall Pro accept?

Housecall Pro wants to make accepting payments quick and easy for both you and your customer!

- Stripe accepts all major credit and debit cards, and the rates remain the same whether your customer is paying with Visa, MasterCard, or American Express.

---

Does the rate change based on the type of card?

- Whichever rate you held when you signed up with Housecall Pro will continue to be your rate for the lifetime of your uninterrupted plan.
