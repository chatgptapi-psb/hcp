How to Set Up Instapay

Setting up Instapay is a breeze! In just a few quick steps, you'll be able to get your account set up so you can start getting paid faster.

Depending on whether your bank supports Real-time Payments, follow the setup instructions below.

---

Setting up Instapay with a Bank that Supports Real-time Payments

To set up Instapay with a bank that supports Real-time Payments:

- Click on the 'My Money' tab in your Housecall Pro navigation bar.

- Select 'Settings' > 'Payout settings' from the menu on the left. This will pull up your Payout Settings panel.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1323691847/2fc6a7f8b3cee49445a3f140b83f/Screenshot+2025-01-06+at+6_00_06%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=d6e69b63157527ec63240fae8262330cb7672ecd16b6048ff12da62bfd44695a&req=dSMlFc93nIlbXvMW1HO4zZU6SH%2BzL7rZJdyMwqSU%2F2DVeFZtO9Cl%2BrenNrcI%0ARWWlEs69qmWlygiEz0M%3D%0A

- In the Instant Payouts section, click the 'Enable instant payouts' toggle to turn Instapay on. The toggle should move to the right, indicating that it is now on.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1351013746/8ece74e6a222d804f48119ee5264/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=04b4cde2a5d1ea75bd19748f515778d7dfe3fdcd4150ff475f49f32de64269b8&req=dSMiF8l%2FnoZbX%2FMW1HO4za0uc7%2FXR%2FQHLMY449FgGM%2BppEmGqCOqd2tw%2BhUv%0AYpPdv0pvgvvv%2ByUDd%2F8%3D%0A

- All of your future card and bank (ACH) transactions will be paid out with Instapay. Moments after taking a card or bank (ACH) payment, you'll be able to see your instant payouts in the 'My payouts' section of the My Money page.

---

Setting up Instapay with a Bank that Doesn’t Support Real-time Payments

To set up Instapay with a bank that doesn't support Real-time Payments:

- Click on the 'My Money' tab in your Housecall Pro navigation bar.

- Select 'Settings' > 'Payout settings' from the menu on the left. This will pull up your Payout Settings panel.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1323692795/c0fe9cdc4f831b90889cb6404426/Screenshot%2B2025-01-06%2Bat%2B6_00_06-E2-80-AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=4477719e1f3143c3391616e4964d1be6ef2820822970130e79fba70512d5196b&req=dSMlFc93n4ZWXPMW1HO4zQCjjSkdCvXqq1Wum22fxWGhH5XKTIdjYwGjMX7t%0AJsosoAhfkbAsAl93qM8%3D%0A

- In the Instant Payouts section, click 'ADD DEBIT CARD.'

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1320743500/54315f2f84784d8ec2328e6e26a6/AddDebitCard.png?expires=1763568000&signature=543adcc8480f77c3c89169fb1c08b3abad31657228253bb3e46a6dc01aca388f&req=dSMlFs56noRfWfMW1HO4zX6iTC%2Ffn6Mj8eAPCLxPBx8mxY76JGwkbHODI4uo%0AtDvBiPSTbdSkucojRaQ%3D%0A

- Next, input your debit card information, then click 'ADD CARD' to save the card to your Housecall Pro account.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1320744193/79ba56d2a39551efcb5b1c08ae43/Screen%2BShot%2B2018-04-13%2Bat%2B3_18_26%2BPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=28ee8222b6e377a6ec7aa9d31214519dbeec00219536ec1a0178ca0bdebb39ca&req=dSMlFs56mYBWWvMW1HO4zYQq9EQtYJUxUhncRunrm5UVJFjbr7u0iHxrpByz%0Aw6KK1JAx37iEKx49lno%3D%0A

- Afterward, instant payouts will automatically be enabled for you, and all of your future card and bank (ACH) transactions will be paid out with Instapay. - You can verify this via the 'Enable instant payouts' toggle which is moved to the right, indicating that Instapay is now on.

- After taking a card or bank (ACH) payment, you'll be able to see your instant payouts in the ‘My payouts’ section of the My Money page.
​

How to Disable Instapay

To disable Instapay and switch to Standard Payouts:

- Click the My Money tab in your Housecall Pro navigation bar.

- Select 'Settings' > 'Payout settings' from the menu on the left. This will pull up your Payout Settings panel.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1324983334/c1b43e4a57f613c6142d1d70a2da/Screenshot-2B2025-01-06-2Bat-2B6_00_06-E2-80-AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=c03837fdbbf4a0d7dad2956b98621e48c8bbd121d4d2ee1da39f3fce8de1a42e&req=dSMlEsB2noJcXfMW1HO4zeNBeRrMMXBEO9kdR%2Fd8u1FjHOzg1vOMEQuCnrbB%0ACGDP3sCoQqXG0%2FP4YJc%3D%0A

- In the "Instapay payouts" section, click the toggle next to the 'Enable instant payouts' option to disable it. The toggle should move to the left, indicating that it is now off.

- Card or bank (ACH) payments taken after disabling Instapay will be paid out with the Standard Payout method.

- You'll see these transactions in the "Pending transactions" section of the My Money page until they are deposited into your bank account (minimum transit time of 2 business days).
