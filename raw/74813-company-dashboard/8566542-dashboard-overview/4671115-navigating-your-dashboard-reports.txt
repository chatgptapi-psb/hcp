Export a Jobs/Estimates Dashboard Report

Learn how to export your jobs or estimates report from your Dashboard to a CSV file.

- Step 1: Click on the eye icon in the top right of your Jobs or Estimates Dashboard report.

Reorder Your Dashboard

To move a report on the Dashboard:

- Select the 'Dash' tab in the navigation bar at the top of your HCP account.

- Scroll down to the bottom of the Dashboard.

- Click the "Reorder dashboard reports" toggle in the bottom left corner of the page.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1355348688/797d7c30bb9703e04a94e81ec10f/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=088f6fd3395fdf9b40dda92112591af57648e892124bfedff5dae000c9575b98&req=dSMiE8p6lYdXUfMW1HO4zdZfYhwQkgGstyonKaOwVXMP0g%2FKJeovbQJm9BFD%0A4q2FCbEp9oYB%2F9iLp8w%3D%0A

- Click and drag your Dashboard reports as needed.

- Click the toggle again to save your changes, and that's it!

How to Use Filters on Dashboard Reports

- In the light blue bar at the top of the report, you can select the date range that the report will show as well as the columns that will appear.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1355455361/f347af4b49d8fe48ca31043c26ea/HouseCall.png?expires=1763568000&signature=ba3b78027c68caf65d9a7a9b6126819e393ce6d59804b534e3271e805f4e63b8&req=dSMiE817mIJZWPMW1HO4zTyVx2zn0GMDXUV6Qj6irK2%2BkN%2BUtRXYB8w4gYg%2F%0ARkv4wtBqs4TrLJfprZk%3D%0A

- Once the correct date and table columns have been selected, you can filter each of the columns by clicking the blue funnel icon in the top right of the column header. If the funnel is red, it means that a filter is already set on that column.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1355455359/05cb3f7e51e4816ae64a1d7ed122/HouseCall.png?expires=1763568000&signature=0bacae9222957731761db1af2dcd3dac06a1e59a6907ffb3098180a4c524b11a&req=dSMiE817mIJaUPMW1HO4zdzclPgs82wM0o1GA3oC%2Fe7GqztiI5FITXDMZBsN%0AC%2Bt2zhMO2ZOmUuXesyY%3D%0A

- Once you have clicked the funnel and entered the information you'd like to filter by, click the filter button and the report will filter to show the new report boundaries.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1355455358/3502b9d2f68052ad7889c817862c/HouseCall.png?expires=1763568000&signature=d2c1195f7e291c139fe968afca19bdf5e78c133e5f2a9faa2d5ed82f3dcc2c7d&req=dSMiE817mIJaUfMW1HO4zWT%2BQ5d%2FJjAMHcDfGNY3IwTjZBm9X6cNLZ51rNCt%0A7n4GjlFZnO2HOvHG4Qw%3D%0A

Remember, that you can set multiple filters at once by repeating this process on other columns to create whichever reports you need!

How to Filter by Unpaid Invoices on the Dashboard

To filter for all unpaid Invoices:

- Make sure you are in your Invoices dashboard. (Click on the right-most panel from your dashboard view).

- Find the DUE column header, and select the funnel icon to the right of it. This will allow you to filter by the amount due.

- Choose the option "Greater than or equal to" from the first drop-down.

- Click the empty box below it and enter the number 1. - Drop-downs will need to be on "Greater Than Or Equal To" and both empty boxes need to have a 1 in them.

- Click the blue FILTER button. This will now be a list of all invoices for which you are owed money.

- You can further filter down by a date range, or any other attribute on the job.

How to Resolve the Error

- Step 1: Limit the Date Range of your Report - Longer date ranges can cause your report to run slowly and timeout generating this error. To fix this, select the date range on the top of the page and narrow it to only a few days.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1355353244/15f79c985b69055c326e8cf486b7/qHUT4r-LBmW8B8cKwt-z2EADfy4wPn1W2jNrRr9QXebSe-YC8qH7iU0KinERhRwo0SNP-xSe-NKAsDS6jX9KKlE6t_04dyj1yozqbESmFLLQ5UwtPPTqCJUaRmRd2MJQG2AaRCDsQgmYsn9uqIEw_vE?expires=1763568000&signature=6e3dfbbfa3c427ab52fb54899281f12126f45729b8dbdb2ec947ec307f28d5f7&req=dSMiE8p7noNbXfMW1HO4zei3GhUHPvnlRgvHarD9LT86PiloneOokFfLC7ef%0AszRZsxYXHw8ibSIpfSQ%3D%0A

- Step 2: Remove any Global Search Terms - The "global search" in the top right search bar of the report can cause your report to run slowly and time out.
- If you are using a global search term, delete the text from the search bar.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1355353245/a365f66c094bc2700cf2a6fc99e3/srycFGMHoi5s_pglwAC_TD5cWtCnuKYicSLrmnzg4deK5Xgq-04HriLbIh7zSDeySsoHt95ww04e3UoaL7sViWhrQmn-9qGEZatn376FQEbe_9j-h6pNpFnbkDagFBvzU3TIGObKjcx9DAMQhxzkxSQ?expires=1763568000&signature=90a1f694545c6f0e6e932a89123aab916d3a1202ff214f2ce3323ad2e2cde773&req=dSMiE8p7noNbXPMW1HO4zUztxXPUSk%2F62v3rRWv6wfi4%2BpAlK4gQ%2BcIchFUK%0Aue%2FuI18RDFVNTOleHbk%3D%0A

- Step 3: Use One of the Columns to Apply the Search Term - Click on the filter icon next to the column name and type your search term there instead of using the global search.
 - You can select any column in the report to search and/or filter.
- Using this search functionality will help your report run faster and decrease the likelihood that it times out.
