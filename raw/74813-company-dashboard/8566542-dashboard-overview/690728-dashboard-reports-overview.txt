Estimates Dashboard Report

From your Dashboard, the Estimates dashboard report quickly tells you how many estimates you have created, how many have been won, and any that have been lost.

An estimate that is copied to a job is considered a won estimate that has gone from being an estimate performed to a job that will result in a paid invoice for your business.

This dashboard report can be filtered and sorted by date range and table columns. Click the eye icon to edit your report.

Filtering the Job Revenue Earned Dashboard Report

You can filter to a different time period on the chart by clicking on the filter icon in the top right corner of the card.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1355310734/1073d18801670948ed60652e80d5/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=436904c7d0aa9678ae326f7f40a0458d36a6e5290b3e12e9d7a15e7d92d42395&req=dSMiE8p%2FnYZcXfMW1HO4zfGhDzO1s%2B7XCcz72Pr77EgYpt4%2F%2FGnVFG9Okrnb%0ALzl2q148QafOmVKFsqM%3D%0A

After clicking on the filter icon, you can change the reported time frame by selecting Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.

- Daily view = data for the past 7 days.
- Weekly view = data for the past quarter (13 weeks).
- Monthly view = data for the past year (12 months).

In all views, the final metric will be your current job revenue, whether it's today, this week, or this month.

Jobs By Tag Dashboard

This report on your dashboard will show you at a glance which job tags are attached to the most jobs. It's a snapshot views of jobs by tag to gain insights into which Job tags you are using the most.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1355232938/f41c260bbfc821f80b5e9f27b872/1.png?expires=1763568000&signature=3a8d107323294d30bdef1fb1a953f01eec93c7694d3ca2fd80cbff5b5d5a39ee&req=dSMiE8t9n4hcUfMW1HO4zWuI1G0eJ0RrpkZblRaiAJYqFlCvZTAGKHdLtFQ7%0A6O%2BSa1OhtEM8%2BQGqGdU%3D%0A

Job tags can be used to keep track of different aspects of a job. For example, a job can be tagged with a certain product name to indicate that a product was used when performing this job. A job can be tagged with a status such as "awaiting part" to indicate why the job is in its current state. A job can be tagged with a specific tag to indicate that an upsell was used as well.

In order to see or filter jobs by tag and see which jobs have been tagged specifically, you will want to run and filter your Jobs Report.

Average Job Size Dashboard Report

The Average Job Size report appears on the main Dashboard of your Housecall Pro account and shows the total revenue earned divided by the total number of jobs completed.

This report defaults to a weekly view and includes a comparison average job size for the previous week and quarter.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1355344327/57297bb0267297518282a6677296/nofilter.png?expires=1763568000&signature=52363be2bc3314a269e6ddfb9ccb830757ee7b533bd088e86f54734299daec85&req=dSMiE8p6mYJdXvMW1HO4zTdPaLoVB%2FclU32rDxYhPqE9Z9VyZ23D1umE0wyS%0ApBQW0G%2FilN%2B1P9jQZo4%3D%0A

- To move the report, select the “Reorder dashboard reports” toggle in the bottom left corner of your dashboard.

How the Average Job Size Report is Calculated

- Average job size = (sum of $ value of completed jobs)/(count of completed jobs)

- Week = starts on Sunday and ends on Saturday. - For example, if it is Tuesday, this week's job size will consider any jobs completed on Sunday, Monday, or today.

Sales Leaderboard FAQs

How is this calculated?

- Both the number of jobs and the dollar amount tied to those jobs are based on a job in the system being marked as finished.

What if there are multiple employees assigned to the job?

- The entire dollar amount of the job will allocated to each of the employees assigned to the job. All employees will get credit for the job counting towards the all-time number of jobs they have been on.

If an employee no longer works for us will they still appear?

- Since this dashboard is all time it will show both active and removed employees.

Can this be customized?

- Since this is a pre-set all-time visual, you are unable to customize this report. However, you can get this same data and customize it by employee or date range or tags by creating a Jobs report.
