Business Coaching by Housecall Pro

When Conquer partnered with Housecall Pro in 2021, the goal was simple. Instill confidence and offer a proven playbook. Since then, thousands of Pros have gained valuable insights and strategies to elevate their businesses. Now, with a vision to bring the benefits of business coaching to our entire community of Pros, Conquer has evolved into Business Coaching by Housecall Pro. While we have a new name, our mission to help Pros grow and succeed stays the same.

---

About the Rebrand

What is Business Coaching by Housecall Pro?

- Business Coaching by Housecall Pro offers educational programs tailored to your learning style and business goals to help you confidently lead and grow your business.

How is Business Coaching different from Conquer?

- Business Coaching by Housecall Pro is the new name for Conquer. It includes the same great coaching programs, just offered within the Housecall Pro ecosystem.

Besides the name change, are any other changes coming?

- Yes! Beyond the change to Business Coaching by Housecall Pro, the team is working to create new offerings for you to take advantage of including on-demand webinars covering relevant topics to help your business grow and more resources to help you navigate Housecall Pro’s features with ease. We plan to share more details on these offerings mid-2025.

How do I learn about new Business Coaching offerings?

- Want to be the first to know about these new coaching offerings? Sign up for the waitlist

Accessing Existing Coaching Programs

I am part of a current Conquer course. Will I still be able to access my current coaching content and workshops?

- Yes, your Conquer account will continue to work so you can access coaching documents and videos. Additionally, there is no change to how you access your current workshops and coaching sessions today.

How do I access my coaching content?

- You will continue to use your existing username and password to access resources through the existing website portal. In addition, keep an eye on your email and calendar for content and invites from your coach.

About Business Coaching

What programs are offered on Business Coaching and how can I sign up?

- Along with webinars, Business Coaching has a robust curriculum to meet your needs including a workshop called “The Blueprint to Early Success”, Business Foundations program, Solo Coaching Plus, and Group Coaching. Learn more here.

Can I benefit from Business Coaching?

- Absolutely! Similar to how coaching positively impacts a professional athlete's career, any Pro can benefit from Business Coaching tailored to your industry and business to reach your full potential.

How much does Business Coaching cost?

- Given our programs and features, Business Coaching can be completely free, a one-time fee, or paid with a monthly subscription. Complete an interest form to talk with an Enrollment Specialist about what coaching options could be the best fit for you.

Can I sign up for Business Coaching if I’m not a Housecall Pro customer?

- We recommend subscribing to the Housecall Pro platform to get the full benefit of Business Coaching. If you do not have a Housecall Pro account, you can create one here.
