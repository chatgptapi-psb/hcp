Marketing Center Overview
Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/11057071-marketing-center-overview
Author: Nate H
Last updated: Updated over a month ago

Marketing Center helps you reach more customers, book more jobs, and build your reputation—all in one place. With email, text, and postcard campaigns, plus built-in review management and online booking, you can keep your schedule full and focus on what matters most—growing your business.

To view the Marketing Center in Housecall Pro, click on “Marketing” in the top navigation bar of your Housecall Pro account.
