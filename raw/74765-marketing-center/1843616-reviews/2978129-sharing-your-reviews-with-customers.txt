Share Your Reviews with Customers

Not all your customers have Gmail or Facebook accounts, but we still want them to have the ability to provide you feedback on your wonderful service!

These customers are directed to a page specific to your business to leave their reviews, which you can view and share with others. These reviews are great for employee management as they are tied to a specific job with that customer & make great content for your website.

To share your reviews with your customers, follow these steps:

- Click the gear icon in the top right corner of your HCP account to go to Settings.

- Select Marketing Center from the Settings menu on the left.

- Click the Reviews tab on the Marketing Center settings page.

How to Set Up Exclusion Tags in Your Reviews App

If there are specific customers or jobs you’d like to exclude from receiving review requests, you can use exclusion tags in your Reviews settings.

To prevent review requests from being sent:

- For a customer:
Add the exclusion tag to the customer's profile. This will prevent them from receiving review requests for any future jobs.

- For a specific job:
Add the exclusion tag directly to the job. This stops a request from being sent for that job only.

Then, complete the setup in your Reviews settings:

- Under Exclude specific tags, enter the exact customer or job tags you want to exclude.

- Press Enter after typing each tag to add it.
