Pointing Your Domain into Housecall Pro

If you currently own your domain through another registrar and would like to keep the domain there while still utilizing the Websites by Housecall Pro add-on, you can point your domain to us!

To point your domain in, you'll need to access your domain's DNS settings. This process can vary depending on where your domain is hosted, so you may need to contact your domain provider for assistance.

If you own your domain through Housecall Pro's registrar services and would like to keep it here, but want to use the domain on an External website, you can request that we point the domain to your new website host! Please reach out to the Blue Chat Bubble to be connected with our Website experts for assistance with this process.

DNS Record Configuration

Before you begin, make sure you take a screenshot or write down what your current DNS settings are in case you ever want to change them back. Then you can make changes to your DNS records so they include the following:

Record Types & Information

- CNAME Record: - Host: www
- Points to: s.multiscreensite.com
- TTL: Choose the lowest setting
- A Records: - First A Record:
 - Host/Alias: @
 - Target/Destination: 35.172.94.1
 - TTL: Choose the lowest setting
- Second A Record:
 - Host/Alias: @
 - Target/Destination: 100.24.208.97
 - TTL: Choose the lowest setting

When you've completed your repointing, please chat in so we can finish the process of getting your website set up for you!
