Section 1: Purchasing Your Domain through Google Domains

Step 1: Create a Google Domains account

- Open your web browser and go to the Google Domains website: https://domains.google/.
- Click on the "Get Started" button to create a new account.
- Follow the prompts to set up your account by providing the required information.

Step 2: Search for and select your desired domain

- Once you've created your account, sign in to Google Domains.
- Use the search bar on the homepage to enter the domain name you want (e.g., "yourdomainname.com").
- Google Domains will display a list of available domain extensions (e.g., .com, .net, .org). Select the extension that best suits your needs.
- If your desired domain is available, click the "Add to cart" button next to it.
​

Step 3: Fill out domain contact information

- In the cart, review your domain selection and ensure it is correct.
- Select the desired registration length (e.g., 1 year, 2 years) from the drop-down menu.
- Fill out the domain registration contact information.
​

Step 4: Complete the purchase

- After configuring your domain settings, review your cart to ensure all the details are accurate.
- Click the "Checkout" button to proceed with the purchase.
- Follow the on-screen instructions to provide the necessary payment information.
- Once the payment is successfully processed, you will receive an email confirmation from Google Domains.

Section 2: Emailing Your New Domain to Housecall Pro

After successfully obtaining your domain through Google Domains, it's important to email your new domain to us to complete the setup process for your new website.

Step 1: Compose an email

- Open your preferred email client or webmail service.
- Create a new email addressed to [email protected]

Step 2: Include the necessary information

- In the subject line, enter "New Google Domain: [Your Domain Name]."
- In the body of the email, include the following details: - Your name and contact information.
- The domain name you purchased through Google Domains.
- Any additional instructions or specific requirements you may have.

Step 3: Send the email

- Double-check the email for accuracy and completeness.
- Click the "Send" button to email your domain information to Housecall Pro.
