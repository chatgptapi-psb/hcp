Understanding Your Website Metrics

Once your website is live, it's essential to monitor its performance to make informed business decisions. Housecall Pro provides two categories of metrics to help you track your website's effectiveness:

Performance Metrics:

- Revenue: Total revenue generated from jobs booked through online booking in the past 30 days.

- Jobs Booked: Number of online booking jobs created in the last 30 days.

- Chat Leads: Number of customers created through the chat feature in the trailing 30 days.

Note: These metrics are displayed only after your website is published and will appear only if there is data from the last 30 days. If no data is available, the respective metric card will not be shown.

Traffic Metrics:

- Unique Visitors: The number of distinct individuals visiting your website in the last 30 days.

- Page Views: The total number of pages viewed on your website, including repeat visits, in the last 30 days.

Note: Traffic metrics charts are available immediately after publishing your website. Data will begin to populate as visitors access your site. Regularly reviewing these metrics can provide valuable insights into your website's performance and help identify areas for improvement.

Request Revisions to Your Unpublished Website Add-On

With our Commenting Feature Flow, you'll have Admin access so you can leave comments to request edits and suggest changes to your website during the build stage, ensuring your site meets your expectations before it's published.

How to Use the Commenting Feature Flow

Step 1: Log in to your Website Dashboard in your Housecall Pro account.

- Log in to your Housecall Pro account.

- Navigate to My Apps by clicking the 3x3 square icon in the top-right corner of your account.

- Click the 'Go to App Store' button.

- Scroll down until you find the "Websites" card and click on "Learn more" to explore further.

- Finally, click "Launch" to open your website's comprehensive dashboard.

Step 2: Make revision requests.

- Once logged into your website dashboard, click 'Edit' and your website will load.

- Click the '+' (plus) icon located on the right in the Comments bar.

- Click on any element in your website and a comment box will pop up.

- Enter your comment or edit request and click 'Submit.'

- The website team will receive your comment and, when the request has been resolved, you will receive an email notification that it's been completed.

Step 3: Leave the rest to us!

- After receiving your request, our team will make the changes for you so that your site is ready to be published.

- If you have any questions or additional edit requests once your site is live, chat with our Support team via the Blue Chat Bubble in your Housecall Pro account, and our team will take care of it for you!

- Please allow 1-3 business days for us to publish requests.
