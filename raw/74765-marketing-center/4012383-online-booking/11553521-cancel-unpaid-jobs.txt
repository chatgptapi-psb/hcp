Collect Payments When Customers Book Online

You are able to enable payments in your Online Booking flow to collect deposits or full upfront payments when a customer books a job. A customer has the option to not pay in the exact moment they book a job, but instead pay before the scheduled job has started. If a customer does not pay before the scheduled job, you are able to cancel these unpaid jobs.

---

Emails with Payment Links

When a customer fails to pay for the Job during the booking process, they will receive an email with a payment link. The customer can use this link to pay for the job at a later time.

Automatically Cancel Unpaid Bookings

To automatically cancel the unpaid jobs, follow these steps:

- Click the gear icon in the top right corner of your HCP account to go to Settings.

- Select Booking from the Settings menu on the left.

- Click the Online Booking tab in the Booking settings page.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1645661054/d6d771047c019f0de0297697b4a2/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=881f1bdd52bc3078d2a013f08d7de8f961cdb2be500930209d8e4996d5c74588&req=dSYjE894nIFaXfMW1HO4zcTU45kzJNE3bLSjOl6T1ZhIdX2SHb4KsWTofEla%0AcvOY5FJMicSGAkPVXyk%3D%0A

- Enable the toggle Automatically cancel unpaid bookings within 1 hour.

If that option is toggled on, the unpaid Jobs will be automatically canceled after one hour, and both you and the customer will receive a notification about the cancellation.
