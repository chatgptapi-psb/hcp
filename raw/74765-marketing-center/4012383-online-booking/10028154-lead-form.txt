Lead Forms Overview

Lead Form allows you to capture leads from your website. This simple form can be customized in Housecall Pro settings and embedded on your website.

The customers visiting your website can fill in this form, and a Lead will be created in Housecall Pro.

[Video - wistia] https://fast.wistia.net/embed/iframe/6p6moxyp38

---

Set Up Your Lead Form

To set up your Lead form, follow these steps:

- Click the gear icon in the top right corner of your HCP account to go to Settings.

- Select Booking from the Settings menu on the left.

- Click the Lead form tab in the Booking settings page.

Edit Lead Form Text

To change the text displayed in your Lead form:

- In your Lead form settings page, expand the Edit text section by clicking the arrow in the top right corner.

- Click the Edit button.

- When you edit the text, it will get updated in the live preview on the right-hand side.

- Once you are done with the updates, click Save.

Add Fields to Your Lead Form

You can pick which fields will be displayed in your Lead form.

To add fields to your Lead form:

- In your Lead form settings page, expand the Add field section by clicking the arrow in the top right corner.

- Click the Edit button.

- Click the toggle to the left of the fields you would like to add: - Last name
- Email
- Address
- Service Details
- How did you hear about us?
- Frequency
- Custom question

- Click Save and you're all set! Your Lead form will then be updated.

Publishing the Lead Form on Your Website

To publish the Lead form on your website:

- In your Lead form settings page, expand the Share lead form section by clicking the arrow in the top right corner.

- Copy the embed code by clicking the Copy code button and paste it into the right place in the code of your website.

- If you are not managing your website, copy the Lead form code and send it to the webmaster or the company that runs your website.

Receiving Leads with the Lead From

- Once a customer fills in the Lead form and submits it on your website, a Lead object will be created in Housecall Pro.

- You will receive a text notification about the new Lead. This lead will also show in the Job Inbox and in your Pipeline under the New Lead column.

- All the info provided in the form by your customer will be visible in the Lead object.
