What Is a Plumbing Service Plan?

A plumbing Service Plan, or maintenance membership, is a program that offers customers exclusive benefits in exchange for a recurring fee. These benefits might include regular maintenance, discounts, and priority service. Service Plans help customers prevent costly plumbing emergencies while giving you predictable income.

Why Plumbing Pros Should Offer Service Plans

- Steady Revenue: Secure recurring income with predictable monthly or annual fees.
- Customer Retention: Build loyalty by creating an ongoing relationship with your customers.
- Upselling Opportunities: Leverage routine checkups to recommend additional services.
- Less Downtime: Smooth out slow seasons by scheduling regular maintenance visits.
