Maintenance Cleaning Plan

Plan name: Maintenance Cleaning Plan

Plan description:

- Regular steam cleaning of high traffic areas
- Spot cleaning - up to 3 areas of stain removal
- 5% off additional treatments and odor control

Duration: 1 year

Included visits: 4 per year

Price: $500

VIP Cleaning Program Plan

Plan name: VIP Cleaning Program

Plan Description:

Includes unlimited full-service cleanings in a year (up to four areas). As a member of the VIP Program, you will receive a 20% discount on all cleanings, and priority scheduling.

Duration: Indefinite

Included visits: N/A

Price: $10/month
