Website Requirement for A2P Registration

Author: Olivia R
Last updated: Updated over 4 months ago

Summary: When registering for A2P, a website is required for the Approval process.

A2P Registration

When registering for A2P in order to use SMS, the section of the form asking for your business's website is required for certain registration types.

It is important to ensure you use a full URL starting with HTTP:// or HTTPS://

If you do not have an active website that is verifiable, please provide a public social media page that is accessible by anyone.

This can include:

- A Facebook page
- An Instagram page
- A Yelp listing
- A LinkedIn page

This list is not exhaustive, but these are the most common forms of public business pages.
