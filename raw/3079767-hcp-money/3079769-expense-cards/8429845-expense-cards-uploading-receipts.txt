Expense Cards: Uploading Receipts
Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/8429845-expense-cards-uploading-receipts
Author: Nate H
Last updated: Updated over 9 months ago

Summary: Expense Cards users have the option to upload receipts directly to transactions.

Upload an Expense Card Receipt (HCP Web Portal)

- Click on the 'My Money' tab in the navigation bar at the top of your Housecall Pro account.

- Select 'Transactions' from the menu on the left, under HCP Money.

- Click on an Expense Card purchase line item, and the transaction details card will appear on the right side of your screen.

- Upload the receipt image, and you're all set!*

Upload an Expense Card Receipt (HCP Mobile App)

- Open the HCP Mobile App.

- On iOS/Apple devices, tap 'More' in the bottom right corner of your screen.

- On Android devices, tap the three-line icon in the top left corner of your screen.

- Tap 'Expense History' then select an Expense Cards purchase line item.

- Tap 'Add receipt' and upload the receipt image by taking a new photo or uploading one from your photo library.

- You're all set!*

---

Expense Card Receipts in QuickBooks Online

Expense Card receipts will now transfer to your QuickBooks® Online account automatically. Just upload your receipts through the HCP Web Portal or Mobile App and we'll take care of the rest!
​
To view the receipt in your QuickBooks account, go to the Expense Cards transaction and it will be in the 'Attachments' section.
