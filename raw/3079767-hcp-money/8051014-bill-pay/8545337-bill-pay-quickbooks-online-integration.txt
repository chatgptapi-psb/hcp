How Housecall Pro Bill Pay Works with QuickBooks Online

Housecall Pro Bill Pay is now connected to QuickBooks Bill Pay! With this integration, bills created in Housecall Pro automatically sync to QuickBooks Online, saving you time by reducing manual entries and providing accurate reporting.

When you're connected to QuickBooks Online and pay a bill through Housecall Pro, the billing record is immediately sent from Housecall Pro to QuickBooks Online.

- Vendors: When a vendor is created in Housecall Pro and added to a bill, the vendor will be created in QuickBooks Online when the bill is saved.

Prerequisites for Using Bill Pay with QuickBooks Online

To use Bill Pay with the QuickBooks Online integration, the following requirements must be met:

- Your bank account has been connected to Housecall Pro.
- You have enrolled in Housecall Pro Bill Pay.
- Your Housecall Pro account has been connected to QuickBooks Online.
