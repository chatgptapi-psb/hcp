How to Create and Manage Vendors with Bill Pay

Every bill submitted through Bill Pay must be associated with a vendor. Luckily, Bill Pay allows you to easily create and store vendor information, eliminating the need to re-enter vendor details with each new bill.

You can also view past and future invoices associated with each vendor, along with the outstanding amount due to a given vendor.

The Vendor List in Housecall Pro allows you to add new vendors and access existing vendor information.

---

Create a Vendor in Housecall Pro

To create a vendor in Housecall Pro:

- Go to My money in the top navigation bar of your Housecall Pro account on web.

- Select Expenses > Vendors from the left menu.

- Click the blue New vendor button in the top right corner of the page.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1252335386/6db583ddcc9839a02142bc7345cd/NewVendor.png?expires=1763568000&signature=de3627540a6ef71fb05342954a015e5d7393927cd2a64682403b5fa4d4f14937&req=dSIiFMp9mIJXX%2FMW1HO4zexM%2FpgXUO9zf3IaiDTlwHpvBzK8NsZnjSVLgF2t%0A8KLeBmJv7tbLEjJPK3c%3D%0A

- Input the following vendor details: - Vendor name
- Address
- City
- State 
- Zip code
- Vendor phone number (optional)
- Vendor email address 
- Your account number with your vendor
- Notes (optional)
- Vendor bank account information (optional)

- Click Save, and your new vendor will be added to your Vendor list.

Review and Edit Vendor Information

To access your Vendor List and review your vendors in Housecall Pro:

- Go to My money in the top navigation bar of your Housecall Pro account on web.

- Select Expenses > Vendors from the left menu to view your Vendors list.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1252277142/af9a2060f0eb0bf745eb6d2518b6/Vendor-2BOverview.png?expires=1763568000&signature=60efab1fdf3bf03a770eb9dc31d76cc0a9a858c432044b82b62142d7b52a6dd4&req=dSIiFMt5moBbW%2FMW1HO4zSqtHLu7NiSfm8CSbB0BjN732AJgaTHhcYIsw7S7%0A8JIlQBjIspMs5INIqlU%3D%0A

- Select a vendor from the list to review their details.

- The Vendor details page provides an overview of the number of bills you've paid to this vendor, the status of the bills, and any relevant notes for that vendor.

- Click Edit to update the vendor's information as needed.

- Click Save when you're done, and that's it!
