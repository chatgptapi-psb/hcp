Templates for the Trades
Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/11970195-templates-for-the-trades
Author: Taylor L
Last updated: Updated over a month ago

Housecall Pro offers a variety of trade-specific templates and calculators designed to help your businesses save time and streamline operations. Whether you’re in HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical, or another trade, these ready-to-use resources help you spend less time on paperwork and more time getting the job done.

---

Access Trade Templates & Calculators

To access our available Trade Templates and Calculators, follow these steps:

- Click this link to open our Templates for the Trades webpage > Templates for the Trades.

- Scroll down on the webpage and find your industry.

- Under the industry, click the View all templates > button.

Download a Trade Template

To download a trade template, follow these steps:

- After selecting your industry’s templates, scroll down to the Try all our Calculators and Templates section.

- Find the template you’d like to download and select it.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1660822063/974a36bd9bd09f8470c7e778797f/ScreenRecording2025-08-08at10_17_10AM-ezgif_com-video-to-gif-converter.gif?expires=1763568000&signature=4fb9a9a909667b316ce3f3eb80b52245a81ef9d909f2c09fc53202373b4936f5&req=dSYhFsF8n4FZWvMW1HO4zfAOOmDlKJt%2B%2FnkI3cy5uj8vtwDjdjEAWAzpRoJZ%0ATb%2Ftx7SGxCmZfHfvgSw%3D%0A

- Once you've selected the template, click the blue Download Now button.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1660796015/66d95c85074199a2186cc98ae237/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=7a5a1bb6e0389c458db582a68ecd15f32c7a35cf1406e7b6780f6ee1f04f184f&req=dSYhFs53m4FeXPMW1HO4zW%2BR1mQvhwPJGLtyTgiGJ8BfkDgt0KlXen7e6a6F%0AGPPSDadOK24k1PWTJpQ%3D%0A

- Fill out the required form fields: - First Name, Last Name, Phone Number, Email, Company, Company Size, and Industry.

- Then, click the Download now button at the bottom of the form.

- The template will then be sent to the email address entered, and will provide template instructions once downloaded.

Download a Trade Calculator

To download or use a trade calculator, follow these steps:

- From the Try all our Calculators and Templates section, click on the calculator you'd like to use.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1660854688/96eb05e246acff6dc4dfaee2c185/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=04613bb038e6f1fb40d887b9d51e464d044bf0454c440c7e834a5de6464efbce&req=dSYhFsF7mYdXUfMW1HO4zea71xoj%2FJb6TrVOxVm4QGZCe4U4jlBoixitk4UY%0Acv3Vv63Vx5hrdSHgaJI%3D%0A

- You can use the Calculator directly on this webpage, or download the calculator by selecting the blue Download Now button and following the same steps mentioned above.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1660860050/d41e6eb34ae24a8da8a4a9ce6833/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=9d789a43864e045a82738df088ee9c77337f458ace53cd3135c70797f4c110c9&req=dSYhFsF4nYFaWfMW1HO4zctAOfJxp4uOD%2BerBpguxuLa8l6vKFdQwkz0iAai%0A6Gv2A3jxiNskeKa7oDw%3D%0A

- If choosing to download the Calculator, the calculator will be sent to the email address entered when filling out the form.

- Once the calculator has been downloaded, follow the template instructions to begin using your newly downloaded industry calculator.
