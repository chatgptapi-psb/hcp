Events in Housecall Pro

Events are a simple and effective way to block time off on your schedule. Whether you need to schedule a meeting or take time off, Events make it easy to keep track of your and your employees' time.

By following the steps outlined in this article, you can easily schedule and manage Events for you and your employees, making it easier to stay organized and on top of your schedule.

---

---

Creating an Event

There are two ways that you can create and schedule an Event in Housecall Pro. We'll go over both methods in this section.

---

- Click the New button in the top navigation bar of your Housecall Pro account.

Create a Recurring Event

Setting recurring Events such as weekly meetings, time off, or appointments in your calendar is simple: set it up once and you're good to go! Just follow the steps below.

To create a recurring event:

- Click the New button in your navigation bar and select Event.

- Input your Event details: date/time and employee (required), event name, notes, and location (optional).

- Click the pencil icon to open your Schedule.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1133057879/8b53cb0fdcbf4dbd4ee36ef0/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=88ea1a3bfb64747bfb214d067e587801bbd0a889721d52cb054ffeda6a0923d4&req=dSEkFcl7molYUPMW1HO4zYIah%2FxjWMptOeZzCBBKRFHNrf8V%2BY2kfUM6cODk%0AieA6KqGFRANFFpvOCkA%3D%0A

If you no longer need an Event, you can easily delete it from your calendar by following the steps below:

- Go to your Schedule page and click on the Event you'd like to delete.

Events FAQs

Can I convert an Event into a Job or Estimate?

- Events can't be converted to jobs or estimates at this time.

Do Employees get notified of Events?

- Employees assigned to an Event can view it on their Schedule, including all Event details, but they will not receive notifications for events.

Where can I view deleted Events?

- At this time, Events cannot be viewed or recovered in the system once they have been deleted.
