Viewing Your Schedule (Calendar) in the Field

The Housecall Pro mobile app makes it easy to have your schedule and job information on hand at all times, whether you use an iPhone or Android.

Clicking directly into a job or estimate on the schedule will bring you to the Job Details page where you will see the details for that specific job, including who your customer is, service address, and notes on what needs to be done.
​

---

View Your Schedule in the Field (HCP Mobile App - iOS)

View your company's schedule on the iPhone mobile app with just a couple of quick steps.

- Select the 'Schedule' icon in the navigation bar at the bottom of your screen.

- Tap the menu icon in the top left corner of your Schedule.

What are the schedule view options on Android?

- Tap the Schedule icon at the bottom of your screen. This icon is located second to the left and appears as a little calendar in gray. - Once selected, the icon will turn blue and you will see your schedule on the screen.

- The default view in Android is the List view, which will show the jobs assigned to you in a list format.

- Tapping the filter icon in the upper right-hand corner of the screen next to the schedule icon brings up your calendar filters.

- The calendar can be filtered by List View, 1-Day View, or 3-Day View.

- To change to a 1-Day or 3-Day view, click the stack of three lines in the top left-hand corner of the screen. A menu will open to allow you to choose between views. Simply select the view you prefer from the list and the calendar will adjust accordingly.

What are the schedule view options on my iPhone?

- Tap the Schedule icon at the bottom of your screen. This icon is located second to the left and appears as a little calendar in gray. Once selected, the icon will turn blue and you will see your schedule on the screen.

- On the iPhone app, the schedule displays a 2-week calendar overview at the top of the page, featuring all dates for the current 2-week period.

- The default view is the List view, which will show the jobs assigned to you in a list format. To change to a calendar or Day view, click the stack of three lines in the top-left corner of the screen.

- A menu will open to allow you to choose between views. Simply click on your preferred view and it will immediately be reflected in the schedule.

Key Reminders

If you've added a job, estimate or event on the web version of Housecall Pro, remember:

- Information about new jobs, estimates and events, refresh every minute in the app.
- You will have to reload the chosen view screen in order to see any new jobs, estimates and events.
