Save the web portal as an icon on iPad

[Video - youtube] https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_d3Z31qiSwk

To save the web portal as an icon on your iPad, follow these steps:

- Open your web browser and sign into your HCP account
- Click the folder icon to the left of the '+' button at the top right of the page
- Click the '+' button titled 'Add to Home Screen'
- Click 'Add'
- A new icon will be created on the home screen to access while in the field.

A few notes:

- This is for use by Admins right now. The employee permissions are not yet fully implemented.
- This is NOT an app that you download through the app store. It is our web portal working great on your tablet.
- Use the Safari browser to add the icon to your home screen. The web portal works on other browsers, but they don't have the 'add icon' feature.
- Watch the vid above to see how.
- Whatever page you are on when you 'Add to your Homescreen' will be the page the app opens to. So we recommend the Dashboard or Schedule page.
