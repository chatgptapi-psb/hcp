I am seeing a 'Network Error' message on my app. What do I do?
Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/1543163-i-am-seeing-a-network-error-message-on-my-app-what-do-i-do
Author: Dyamond W
Last updated: Updated over a year ago

Summary: Troubleshoot your mobile app to get back into the app quickly.

If a window showing 'Network Error' has popped up on your app, you can solve this issue on your own with ease!

The most common cause of this network error message is that a change was made to your employee information, such as your login email or password. Follow the troubleshooting steps below:

Step 1: Log out of the app and log back in.

To log out on an iOS device, click the 'More' icon in the bottom right and scroll to the bottom of the screen. Click the red 'Log Out' option, located at the bottom of your screen.

To log out on Android, click the three stacked lines in the top left corner of your app. Select the down arrow next to your email. Click ‘Logout’.
