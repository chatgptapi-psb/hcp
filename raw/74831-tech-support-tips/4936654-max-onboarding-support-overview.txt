Support Options: MAX Plan

We're committed to supporting you every step of the way on your MAX Plan. Here's a recap of all the ways we’ll be here for you:

- Chat Support: For immediate assistance, quick questions throughout the day, and tech-related issues, chat is your fastest option. - Available:
 - Monday to Friday: 5 AM - 5 PM PST
 - Saturday and Sunday: 8 AM - 4 PM PST
- Phone Support: If you prefer to speak with us directly, you can call our support line at 1-877-944-9010 (press 1 for customer support). - Available:
 - Monday to Friday: 6 AM - 5 PM PST
- Housecall Pro Help Center: Access an extensive library of videos, tutorials, and trainings anytime for self-paced learning.
- Email Support: Any inbound support emails will be routed to our chat team for real-time assistance.

---

Your 60-Day Onboarding Journey

- Our onboarding process is divided into two phases to ensure you’re set up for success: - Phase 1: You'll work closely with your Onboarding Specialist during weekly training calls to build your ideal workflow, understand available tools, and get ready to GO LIVE!
- Phase 2: After going live, this phase focuses on fine-tuning your processes and addressing any questions that arise in the field. You can schedule additional coaching sessions using the “Schedule a Coaching” link located in the chat bubble.
