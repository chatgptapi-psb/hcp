Resetting your Housecall Pro Password
Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/362412-resetting-your-housecall-pro-password
Author: Steve S
Last updated: Updated over a year ago

Summary: How to reset your password.

If you or a colleague have forgotten your password (don’t worry, it happens to everyone!), just follow the steps below to reset it on the HCP website or mobile app. 
​

Reset Your Password from the HCP Website

- Click 'Forgot Password?' from the From the Housecall Pro Sign in page

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1133302451/e2234ee9d494a4eb765b42a7/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=e90b7f8e587ed2735092629b51ef41647c92ba8a2ab92fec086aa1fd8bd367c1&req=dSEkFcp%2Bn4VaWPMW1HO4zc%2F3N%2FkKe2aVQEZ5RX9TiiokvymNY5x1Qs6L5YEq%0AI9xMoa%2BZJjozWTw7LKM%3D%0A

- Next, enter the email address associated with your HCP account. This should be the same email address you use to log in normally. 
​
- Finally, click Send, and then we will automatically send you a reset password email to the email address you entered.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1133308522/99616f252a8eab49cdd67fba/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=7833fe62cb9d70e0f0097b4095b16fecc1b55c3b4781b57533ed4a6ad61333dc&req=dSEkFcp%2BlYRdW%2FMW1HO4zZ7gTWeIdc6bKkGJ9e%2F7%2BiV3p7qTMsujwg9%2Fo5nk%0ABSRF3nqyBf2ACjKMB0Q%3D%0A

- Go into your email account and look for the email from Housecall Pro (support@housecallpro.com) with the subject "Housecall Password Reset"

- When you open the email, click on the blue 'Update Password' button

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/462501655/c755bb2985ad591e1b12b138/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=a98e49624caf136d1b3355f68a8ef9cdaa864a0f5a7741b0c2d3486c8673e90f&req=cCYlE8l%2Fm4RaFb4f3HP0gKrW4SnRRw2NVmM7MJWH6zY33EUAKyeSSR%2F7njHe%0AWkE5N6pCNwYQUbkfMA%3D%3D%0A

- After you click the 'Update Password' button, you will be brought to the 'Reset your password' webpage. You will need to type in a new password, and then click the blue 'Reset Password' button.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1133314800/4460308d1c79c3f7185a2fac/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=d1e886e671c8d71174dd4a959e7493c9e824e3744472fad3b473321c49f7c581&req=dSEkFcp%2FmYlfWfMW1HO4zWglbo7lGXQ8i8L%2FWHe3cQza8lkARGWZMA8W3Jpw%0AfVrmZlf6Vj8XhNb%2BGEQ%3D%0A

- You will then be automatically logged into your account!

Reset Your Password from the HCP Mobile App

- To reset your password from the HCP mobile app, first, tap the 'Sign in' button from the main screen.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1133326258/5c075f42c7304df887206dd7/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=f74c672873f6e2f96fddec60eadab766c60c09f06313a0568d3e44c64b403b0c&req=dSEkFcp8m4NaUfMW1HO4zdagcccHWpDNDsBtswET5tb41TMGAYNzUu0w0MSd%0AkaW0eGEtV1BXL2f1hX8%3D%0A

- Next, on the Sign in page, tap on the 'Reset password' button. 
​

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1133324771/bbc431d90eff812b7fdbcb5d/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=876acc8b9d291994ee9fb82dd6412375c7ab957bf54a6150f763ab25a74ebcb5&req=dSEkFcp8mYZYWPMW1HO4zZkoc4NhCsdWLL0UIxBRabO77BScpdr7lE%2Ba8rM0%0AAo6URxliGfq%2BuKPpPH8%3D%0A

- From here, enter the email address associated with your HCP account. This should be the same one you use to log in normally. 
​
- After entering your email address, tap the blue 'Send link' button, and we will automatically send a reset password email to the email address you entered. 
​

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1133327867/374bcd8fd211a6c8bb4e3081/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=fae13b2dd57794ed8fdf999f62d3602339bd214ff1d48cda43d641a2f7c7b305&req=dSEkFcp8molZXvMW1HO4zckv0YhbomubvSOGNy%2B9oEPrQceTMy7putRmsv2C%0Akic7Z%2B0ydWMHSnKLUXc%3D%0A

- Go into your email account and look for the email from Housecall Pro (support@housecallpro.com) with the subject "Housecall Password Reset"

- When you open the email, click on the blue 'Update Password' button

- After you click the 'Update Password' button, you will be brought to the 'Reset your password' webpage. You will need to type in a new password, and then click the blue 'Reset Password' button.
​

- You will then be automatically logged into your account!

---

​Password Requirements

Please be sure that your password meets our password requirements and contains:

- 8 or more characters
- At least one uppercase letter
- At least one number
- At least one special character (!@#$%^&*)
