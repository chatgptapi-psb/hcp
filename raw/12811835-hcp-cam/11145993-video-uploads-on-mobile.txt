Video Attachments on Mobile

Photos are great, but they don’t always tell the whole story. That’s why we’re excited to introduce video uploads for jobs and estimates on mobile! Now you can capture and share the full picture—whether it’s a broken part, an unusual sound, or anything else that needs a little extra explanation.

You can upload videos under a minute long to any job or estimate, and if it's longer, our in-app trimmer makes it easy to trim it down.

Skip the long descriptions and let your videos do the talking!

Recording & Uploading Videos to Jobs & Estimates on Mobile

Capturing and uploading videos directly from your mobile device is a powerful way to enhance documentation and communication.

In the following sections, we’ll walk you through the process of recording videos, trimming them when needed, and uploading them to a job or an estimate—all from within the app. Whether you’re showcasing a problem or explaining a solution, these steps will help you efficiently share important visual information with your team.

To record videos on a job or estimate from the HCP mobile app:

- Open a job or estimate and tap the "Attachments +" button.

- Then, tap the camera icon in the top right corner of your screen.

Trimming a Video

If you are uploading a video that is longer than one minute, we will prompt you to trim the video.

When prompted to trim your video:

- Tap "Manage" next to the video file.

- Then tap "Trim video" to confirm.

Attaching Videos to Invoices & Estimates on Mobile

After uploading a video to a job, you can share it with your customer when sending their invoice, or easily send it to others via email, text message, or any messaging app on your phone.

To attach a video to an invoice or estimate:

- When sending an invoice or estimate, tap the paperclip icon located just above the preview.

- Then, tap "Attachments" from the prompt at the bottom of your screen.

Sharing Videos with Your Customers on Mobile

To share a video with your customer:

- Open a job or estimate and tap the Attachments section to open it.

- Next, select a video you'd like to share with your customer.
