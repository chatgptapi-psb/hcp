Adding Annotations to Photos on Jobs & Estimates

Photo Annotations enable Pros to effectively communicate with Field Techs, Admins, and customers. After taking a photo through the app, Pros can add drawings, shapes, text, and time stamps before uploading the image to a job or an estimate.

To add an annotation to a photo:

- Open a job or an estimate in the HCP mobile app and tap the + Attachments button at the bottom of the page.

- Tap the camera icon in the top-right corner of the Attachments screen.

- Select Take photo.

- Capture the photo, then tap Use Photo in the bottom-right corner of your screen.

- You'll then be taken to the Edit screen, where you can add the following annotations: - Free draw editor (pencil icon)
- Shape editor (shapes icon)
- Text overlays (T icon)
- Timestamp (clock icon)

- Use the menu at the bottom of your screen for each option to further edit the annotations: - Drawing color and thickness
- Shape and shape color
- Text and background colors (for written text and timestamps)

- Tap the Undo icon to undo your last edit.

- Tap the Clear all icon to remove all annotations from the photo.

- To save the annotations and upload the annotated photo to the job, tap Save in the top right corner of your screen.
