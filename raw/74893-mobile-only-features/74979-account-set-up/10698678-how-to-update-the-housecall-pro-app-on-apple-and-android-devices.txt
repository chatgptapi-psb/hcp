How to Update the HCP Mobile App

Keeping your Housecall Pro app up to date ensures you have the latest features, security updates, and bug fixes.

---

Apple Devices

If you're using an iPhone or iPad, follow these steps to update the Housecall Pro app:

Step 1: Open the App Store

- Locate and tap the App Store icon on your home screen.

Step 2: Go to Your Account

- In the App Store, tap your profile icon in the top right corner.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1405445418/ba668bce44eb85a29228c60af41f/AD_4nXcJas7IJSX_o3uZDlZNOCnK2Q3z6pV2xBUUHafmTfdahA7MM2P69Gf26zgoG1bfQXSORWvqeb6UdAO2neZD_fVnok1kAoI0VbOvL4bfWbNmG-P5V3klpl7FMshUrx4QvgoE5L-2Wg?expires=1763568000&signature=0a977352da888839f8076f6de719f31b74f56534a371befdd93bc35385c7018b&req=dSQnE816mIVeUfMW1HO4zb8bWxr%2Bz4viskHxnFHcy%2FXvIJceKY1dSDZtistp%0ALqOHPUna7892tB6gE1Y%3D%0A

Step 3: Check for Updates

- Scroll down to the Available Updates section.
- If an update is available for the Housecall Pro app, you’ll see an Update button next to it.

Step 4: Update the Housecall Pro App

- Tap Update next to the Housecall Pro app.
- If prompted, enter your Apple ID password or use Face ID/Touch ID to confirm.

Step 5: Wait for the Update to Complete

- The Housecall Pro app will begin updating.
- Once finished, the Update button will change to Open—your app is now up to date!

Troubleshooting Tips

- If you don’t see an update, try pulling down on the screen to refresh the list.
- If the Update button is missing, the Housecall Pro app might already be up to date.
- If you're unable to update, check your Wi-Fi connection or free up storage space.
- Restart your iPhone if updates are not appearing.
- Still need help? Contact Support via the Blue Chat Bubble for further assistance.

---

Android Devices

If you're using an Android device, follow these steps to update the Housecall Pro app.

Step 1: Open the Google Play Store

- Locate and tap the Google Play Store icon on your home screen or app drawer.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1407079917/21e1ad95e248732691b4a0d59be7/Screenshot+2025-03-04+at+4_00_10%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=f8abde7fd3b9ce3df8c7c20d0b50ca33189b6873d7da98cd9acbb697a940d249&req=dSQnEcl5lIheXvMW1HO4zc%2F%2F60jySoxhTuApNfE871eAeExvWWrR9xDtX%2B1k%0ATDkYIpVGV0MYI%2BRMyk8%3D%0A

Step 2: Access Your Profile

- In the Google Play Store, tap your profile icon in the top right corner.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1407067558/948152d7fa2682c8b2ad98c9a729/Screenshot+2025-03-04+at+4_13_26%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=b112fe79bf42db0b7b4b6b3fd9b988ab4b41708365f8098cab0653e1d56eb0e4&req=dSQnEcl4moRaUfMW1HO4zbQf8DCjEyZG8o0VbG8LwVsWC01n1Bve9e437ZwN%0AXQ57H14lHJCj8jSz1Co%3D%0A

Step 3: Go to Manage Apps & Updates

- Tap Manage Apps & Device from the drop-down menu.
- Under Updates available, look for the Housecall Pro app.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1408465724/906b936e2bbc4c0aba682bb473d0/Screenshot+2025-03-05+at+12_39_54%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=5d9a2cf22957e8a821999b42ee0d635e492ea01b185f509a22e4bd129aa1dea8&req=dSQnHs14mIZdXfMW1HO4zWZWjMFrIteCw6kDNpZ5lTC56jndvQuEgis8Z%2By%2B%0AWuv6dYiPaIJTX%2BTvYjk%3D%0A

Step 4: Update the Housecall Pro App

- Tap the Update button next to the Housecall Pro app.
- The download and installation process will begin automatically.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1407061924/72c3e47691bb85b0ae59f9fcc4a4/Screenshot+2025-03-04+at+4_15_03%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=75e531f87c92526f14698df9ad4ba58f593fefae54ce33f0bd563c900a217599&req=dSQnEcl4nIhdXfMW1HO4zfdvUZQs2FkTRTm1mBnHewfhMRPTS9NBLneVRsNv%0AWFDmVAarw2vYlYeJKS4%3D%0A

Step 5: Wait for the Update to Complete

- Once the update is finished, the Update button will change to Open—your Housecall Pro app is now up to date!

Alternative Method: Update from the Housecall Pro App Page

- Open the Google Play Store.
- Tap the search bar and type Housecall Pro.
- If an update is available, you’ll see an Update button. Tap it to update the app.

Troubleshooting Tips

- If you don’t see an update, swipe down to refresh the updates list.
- If the Update button is missing, your Housecall Pro app might already be up to date.
- If the update won’t install, check your Wi-Fi connection and storage space.
- Restart your device if updates are not appearing.
- Still need help? Contact Support via the Blue Chat Bubble for further assistance.
