How to use Amazon Alexa with Housecall Pro

[Video - wistia] https://fast.wistia.net/embed/iframe/yol56c1kr5

Housecall Pro has released a new way for you to manage jobs on the go. Using Amazon’s Alexa app or any Alexa-enabled device you can:

- Hear the details of your next job, such as the time, customer name, and address.
- Text your customers to let them know you’re on the way with a single voice command.
- Get directions to your next job without taking your hands off the wheel.
