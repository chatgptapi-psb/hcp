Summary: Learn how the integration between Housecall Pro and Chiirp works.

How the integration works

Chiirp is a communication automation software. Communicate more effectively with your leads and customers using SMS, email, and Ringless Voicemail.

Chiirp focuses on creating the ability to blend automation and actual human interaction to create quality customer communication at scale.

When an event is triggered in Housecall Pro, this automates follow-up communication from Chiirp's side. You can choose which customized campaigns to use for each Housecall Pro event.

---

Follow these steps to build and assign campaigns to Housecall endpoints.

- Navigate to the Campaign Builder tab in Chiirp to create and edit campaigns. Connecting Housecall Pro and Chiirp

How to connect Housecall Pro to Chiirp and what the integration does.

Chiirp is a communication automation software. Communicate more effectively with leads and customers using SMS, email, and Ringless Voicemail.

To connect the integration

- Log into Chiirp
- Click on Integrations, then click on Housecall Pro [Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/418203353/fbd4053957291dad5fad4b35/step+1.png?expires=1763567100&signature=662f37dfc60a65bac1634e711e791da373683a007e013f8fe36f1a3aff70d6d8&req=cCEvFMl9noRcFb4f3HP0gA0f4kNTfqY0jbEYVpVsQiwCAbPPoAXayPI1OJ2f%0A%2B30%3D%0A
- Click Not Connected [Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/418203443/131aa5565a6bb997797bd319/step+2.png?expires=1763567100&signature=25bea8af4da47117f0d9f329089de8f5bc7ff664a038067ca24aa9c135d33563&req=cCEvFMl9mYVcFb4f3HP0gHchkOeZx%2BpLWAl41ZWWwMphyEZNRUsgdGSTxMsD%0A%2B2M%3D%0A
- You will be prompted to log into Housecall Pro to connect the integration [Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/418203492/090973169e5b897a5a305455/step+3.png?expires=1763567100&signature=8dee89382d5627d1ef890aaaae570ed0b17aae8c39c4746550b4f3a61cc75a69&req=cCEvFMl9mYhdFb4f3HP0gI3VCS0khQt02ctYK8OimUeYmJsTXCfcKlWdJNOT%0AxjI%3D%0A

*IMPORTANT: Adjust your automated SMS messages in HCP so that the customer is not getting multiple messages.
