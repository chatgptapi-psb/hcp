Service Line Items on Jobs Report

The Service Line Items on Jobs report gives you a detailed breakdown of every service performed on jobs — not just the first line item. This report provides full visibility into all services sold, including pricing, costs, profit, and revenue metrics for each item.

---

Why Use This Report?

This report is the only way to view a comprehensive list of all service line items on jobs. It’s particularly useful for tracking:

- Total quantity of services sold
- Profitability per service item
- Revenue contribution per service item
- Deleted job entries (which are also visible)
​

Fields in the Report

Each row in the report represents a single service line item sold on a job. The following key fields are calculated:

- Service Item Profit - Service Item Profit = (Unit price - Unit cost) x Quantity

- Service Item Revenue - Service Item Revenue = Unit price x Quantity
​

---

Customizing Your View

- Click Edit columns to add or remove data fields.

- Use Export to download the report as a CSV for external analysis. Exporting will enable you to sort data by any field in Excel or Google Sheets. 
​

---

Additional Notes

- Deleted jobs will still appear in this report and are marked with the "Deleted" job status.

- You can identify multiple line items from the same job by checking for duplicate Job # values.
