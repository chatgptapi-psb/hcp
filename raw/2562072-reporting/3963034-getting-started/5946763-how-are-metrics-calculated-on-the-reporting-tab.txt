How are metrics calculated on the reporting tab?
Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/5946763-how-are-metrics-calculated-on-the-reporting-tab
Author: James N
Last updated: Updated over a month ago

Summary: Learn how metrics on the reporting tab are calculated

Learn how metrics are calculated when using the Reporting tab in HCP.

Job metric calculations:

Table:
Row 1: Metric | Definition | Notes
Row 2: Average gross profit | The average gross profit of jobs, or the sum of all gross profit divided by number of jobs |
Row 3: Average job size | The average revenue of jobs, or the $ sum of all job revenue divided by number of jobs | Zero dollar jobs (callbacks) are included in this metric by default. If you want to exclude, use the callback job field to mark these jobs Use filters menu to deselect these jobs
Row 4: Avg rating | The average customer rating for any completed jobs | Includes only reviews sent to HCP
Row 5: Commission cost | Sum of commission cost | When viewing by employee, only shows costs associated with that employee Must be using commissions feature to view this metric
Row 6: Commission cost % of rev | Commission cost divided by job revenue | Includes all commission cost when viewing by employee Must be using commissions feature to view this metric
Row 7: Hours per tech per job | The average of on job time (hours) that a technician logged across selected jobs | If time was not tracked on a particular job, that job will be excluded in the average Must be using time tracking to see this metric
Row 8: Job count | The total number of jobs |
Row 9: Job revenue | Job subtotal minus discounts (Excludes taxes and tips) |
Row 10: Job total | The total invoice amount of the job (Includes taxes and tips) |
Row 11: Labor cost | Sum of labor cost. | When viewing by employee, only shows costs associated with that employee Must be using job costing to view this metric
Row 12: Labor cost % of rev | Labor cost divided by job revenue | Includes all labor cost when viewing by employee Must be using job costing to view this metric
Row 13: Material cost | Sum of material cost | Must be using job costing to view this metric
Row 14: Material cost % of rev | Material cost divided by job revenue | Must be using job costing to view this metric
Row 15: Miscellaneous cost | Sum of miscellaneous cost | Must be using job costing to view this metric
Row 16: Miscellaneous cost % of rev | Miscellaneous cost divided by job revenue | Must be using job costing to view this metric
Row 17: Profit margin | Gross profit divided by job revenue | Must be using job costing to view this metric
Row 18: Total cost | Sum of labor, material, commission and miscellaneous cost | Includes all labor and commissions costs when viewing by employee. . Must be using job costing to view this metric
Row 19: Total on job hours | The sum of on job time (hours) that a technician logged across selected jobs | Must be using time tracking to see this metric
Row 20: Total travel hours | The sum of travel time (hours) that a technician logged across selected jobs | Must be using time tracking to see this metric

You can view your job metrics according to three action dates:

Table:
Row 1: Action Date | Definition
Row 2: Job created date | Date and time the job was created in HouseCall Pro
Row 3: Job scheduled date | Date and time as the job appears on the calendar
Row 4: Job completed date | Date and time the job was marked finished

Estimate metric calculations:

Table:
Row 1: Metric | Definition | Notes
Row 2: Conversion rate | Percentage of estimates that were approved, out of all the estimates (won, lost and open) |
Row 3: Won value | Total value of approved estimates | Includes copied to job
Row 4: Won count | Total count of approved estimates | For estimates with multiple options, approved counts when one option is approved or copied to a job Includes copied to job
Row 5: Lost value | Total value of declined estimates | For estimates with multiple options, the maximum value is used
Row 6: Lost count | Total count of declined estimates | For estimates with multiple options, all options were declined
Row 7: Open count | Total count of undecided estimates | For estimates with multiple options, all options are open
Row 8: Open value | Total value of undecided estimates | For estimates with multiple options, the maximum value is used

Table:
Row 1: Action Date | Definition
Row 2: Estimate created date | Date and time the estimate was created in HouseCall Pro

Leads metric calculations:

Leads metrics are only available to Orgs using the Pipeline Feature.

Table:
Row 1: Metric | Definition | Notes
Row 2: Conversion rate | Percentage of leads that were approved, out of all leads (won, lost and open) |
Row 3: Open count | Total count of open leads |
Row 4: Won count | Total count of approved leads | Includes copied to job or copied to estimate
Row 5: Lost count | Total count of lost leads |

Table:
Row 1: Action Date | Definition
Row 2: Lead created date | Date and time the lead was created in HouseCall Pro
