Your HCP Assist Journey Begins: First 30 Days

Welcome to HCP Assist! Let's get you familiarized so you know what to expect within your first 30 days of service.

---

How will I be notified after each call?

Once our team of Virtual Assistants handles your calls, you will receive a full recap of the conversation in your call log.

To access your HCP Assist call log:

- First, click the Inbox tab from the main navigation bar at the top of your account.

- Next, select Call Log under HCP Assist from the Inbox menu on the left side of your screen.

Additionally, you and any other designated recipients will get an SMS alert for messages that require immediate attention and will receive the post-call messages under the employee section in your Housecall Pro inbox.

To access your post-call inbox and messages:

- Click the Inbox tab. This action will redirect you to your Inbox page, where you can manage your communications.

- Here, you'll find a sub-menu labeled Inbox with several options to choose from.

- Click on Employees to proceed. This action will open up the section of your inbox where you can view and manage messages

How do I alert HCP Assist agents to holidays or last-minute schedule changes?

To ensure HCP Assist agents don't accidentally book jobs or estimates when your team is unavailable, please keep us updated on any schedule changes, including holidays or emergencies. Follow the steps outlined in this help article to notify HCP Assist agents about your schedule changes.

---

How do I get assistance with HCP Assist?

We've got your back! To get assistance with HCP Assist, you have several options:

- Send a reply message via the HCP Assist call log.

- Go to the Blue Chat Bubble > HCP Support > General Support > HCPA Answering Service.

- Call us at (858) 842-5746 M-F 8 AM-8 PM EST, Sat-Sun 11 AM-7 PM EST.

---

What should I expect during the first four weeks?

To ensure your success, we will have weekly check-ins with you for the first four weeks. These personalized sessions will help us gather feedback on what’s working and discuss any adjustments you may need.

Your check-ins for week 1 and week 4 should already be on your calendar. During your first check-in, we will schedule the sessions for weeks 2 and 3. If you need to reschedule any of these appointments, please chat via the Blue Chat Bubble.
