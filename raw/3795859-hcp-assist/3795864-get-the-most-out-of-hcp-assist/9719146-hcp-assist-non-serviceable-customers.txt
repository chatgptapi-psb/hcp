How can I block a Do Not Service customer from forwarding to HCP Assist?

- Depending on how you forward your calls, you can block a Do Not Service customer or vendor's phone number directly from your device, carrier, or VOIP settings.

- When a number is blocked from your end, it will not transfer to our Virtual Assistants for service.

- If you are having trouble blocking the number through your phone or carrier, we recommend contacting your carrier's support team for further assistance.

---

If I block a phone number, will it still be forwarded to HCP Assist?

- If you have blocked a number from your device, carrier, or VOIP, then it will not transfer to our Virtual Assistant for service.
