Job and Estimate List Reporting

The Jobs List and Estimates List let you customize how your data is displayed. Each list can be filtered, sorted, and customized by columns. Choose from a wide range of column options covering customer details, scheduling, and financial information, and export your lists to build reports that fit your business needs.

---

Access your Job List

To access your Jobs List:

- Navigate to the Customers tab at the top of your navigation bar.

- In the left-hand menu, select Jobs to view your Jobs List.

View, Filter, and Sort your Job List

The Jobs List displays all jobs created in your account. You can filter, sort, customize visible columns, and export the list to fit your reporting needs.

To view and apply filters to your Job List:

- Click the blue Edit columns button to change the columns that are included in the current view of the Job list.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1723093650/0b8c7c78601ecf735770fe5f84b6/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=780d6b3b654a4bb4d8a635c7e9faecb381182667e64835580715efe509dd12cf&req=dSclFcl3nodaWfMW1HO4zTJSeGiFpnzWo49IbXroFcL8PhThvr4I0z2zJgN5%0AVNjGYS9YZCN3OTWPmLU%3D%0A

- In the Select columns to view window, check or uncheck the box next to the data you would like to add or remove as a column, then select the blue Done button in the bottom right corner.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/805062269/3cdb0036a9f884ac093422a2/Screenshot+2023-08-10+at+3.18.30+PM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=4afcdfd73f6c48d79b41bf998030580d94c4ffaf42b80b8568439abccae09dd6&req=fCAiFs98n4dWFb4f3HP0gL00umOAN0wsypmChzCBCj%2B3QHKVSszune6coDdZ%0Am%2F%2F9iVoMdgqVgrIovg%3D%3D%0A

- Click the blue Filter button to modify your view settings.
- You can apply filters based on any of the following criteria to your Job list: - Job created, scheduled, completed, and paid in full date 
- Last invoice sent date
- Job lead source
- Customer lead source
- Job Tags
- Customer tags
- Job Status: Unscheduled, Scheduled, In progress, Completed, and Canceled
- Employees assigned
- Customers
- Job type
- Job total amount 
- Job profit
- Job amount due
- Callback

- Once you've added your filters, select the X in the top left corner when finished.

- To export your Jobs List, click on the blue Actions button drop-down menu at the top right.
