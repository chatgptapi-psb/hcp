Summary: Keeping track of materials in Housecall Pro

How do I keep track of inventory?

Many of our pros use QuickBooks Online Plus as they have a built-in inventory tracking feature that works well for keeping track of your materials. If you have the material added as a price book item in Housecall Pro, you will be able to account for this in QuickBooks Online when the invoice with the material included is sent over.

If you would like to track what type of materials you use on each job, check out our article on how to run a Material Usage on Jobs Report!

You can also track and report on where material is coming from along with purchase details across jobs and employees. To learn more, check out Material Inventory Detail Tracking.
