Summary: How to connect Housecall Pro to Swell and what the integration does.

How the Integration Works

Swell is a customer experience platform that helps local businesses attract and keep more customers while spending less time doing it.

With the integration, you'll be able to automate texting when you mark a job as “Finished” and provide an easy way for your customers to leave reviews.

- Swell sends out automatic text and email messaging based off job statuses in Housecall Pro such as Job Start Time, Job End Time and Job Completed.

- These triggers will send customer messages such as: Appointment reminders, Review Invites, etc.
