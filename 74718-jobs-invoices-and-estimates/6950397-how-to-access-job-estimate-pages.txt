Accessing Jobs and Estimates

To access a Job or Estimate page:

- Jobs and Estimates can be accessed by clicking on the 'Customers' tab in the navigation bar at the top of your Housecall Pro account.

- From there, select 'Jobs' or 'Estimates' from the menu on the left.

Job and Estimate pages enable you to take actions on your list of jobs or estimates, including:

- Search all jobs/estimates by any field.

- Filter by common fields such as Status, Customer, Employee, Scheduled Date, and more.

- Edit Columns to adjust what fields are shown in your table.

- Create a job or estimate easily. - This is the same functionality as the 'New' button in the navigation bar.

- Export your list of jobs or estimates.

- Import your jobs.
