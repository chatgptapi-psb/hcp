How to Preview, Download, & Print Invoices

Whether you need to preview an invoice, download an invoice for your records, or print a hard copy, managing invoices is quick and efficient in Housecall Pro.

In this article, we'll guide you through the steps to view detailed invoice information, download invoices in PDF format, and print them directly from your account.

---

Preview an Invoice - HCP Web Portal

To preview an invoice through the HCP web portal:

- Click Customers in the navigation bar at the top of your Housecall Pro account.

- Select Jobs from the menu on the left.

- Select the job associated with the invoice you need to preview, and you'll be directed to the Job Details page.

- Click the Invoice icon (paper airplane) in the Action Bar at the top of the page, or click the blue Send Invoice button in the Invoice section.

- You'll then be directed to the Send Invoice page where you'll see the invoice preview.

Download/Print an Invoice - HCP Web Portal

To download or print an invoice from the HCP web portal:

- Click the Customers tab in the navigation bar of your HCP account.

- Select Jobs from the menu on the left.

- Select the job associated with the invoice you need to preview, and you'll be directed to the Job Details page.

- Click Print or Download PDF in the top-right corner of the Send Invoice page.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1278462773/75a4c5005f049ddf01ace763b050/DownloadPrintInvoice.png?expires=1763568000&signature=9c98ea1db7241e0ab9943cec3f6393de5f6b928f0507bbb7acd959b7172ad6df&req=dSIgHs14n4ZYWvMW1HO4zTwqxEx6ref4KSgoAifhK2aFNVTatuqPGcJ312MA%0A3WxgpNczxSBlbsosOOI%3D%0A

- Alternatively, click the printer icon in the Invoice section of the Job Details page, located to the right of the blue Send Invoice button.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1278445469/6bfffda34b9f9a3b4ba50b853a73/Screenshot%2B2024-10-25%2Bat%2B1_15_17-E2-80-AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=f0a0d7eca72719f3e8e1540a272193615d77f9c871646cbd37bece8bab650c3a&req=dSIgHs16mIVZUPMW1HO4zVMRp18k0xnztc8f9MNCpPn25qeQwnwxcE1LiUda%0AlldEMH2GXM4PTENyJOY%3D%0A

- A new tab and Print window will open automatically.

- Select your printing options. - Choose a printer to print a physical copy, or select PDF to download the invoice as a PDF document.

- Click Print in the bottom right corner of the Print window, and you're all set!

Preview, Download, & Print an Invoice - HCP Web Portal (Progress Invoicing Enabled)

To preview an invoice through the HCP web portal with Progress Invoicing enabled:

- Click the Customers tab in the navigation bar of your Housecall Pro account.

- Select Jobs from the menu on the left.

- Select the job associated with the invoice you need to print/download, and you'll be taken to the Job Details page.

- Click the Invoice icon (paper airplane) in the Action Bar at the top of the page. - Alternatively, you can click the + Invoice button in the Invoice Summary section.

- You'll then be directed to the invoice preview on the Add Invoice page.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1278402768/5b954095ee25a74a299ae262777d/ProgressInvoicePrint1.png?expires=1763568000&signature=bf38a8867c0c7d7babafb815533be7a8c0cca2b6f3349323a25d39739fcf4f11&req=dSIgHs1%2Bn4ZZUfMW1HO4zarxupkUeiaYOjJDdn0wYFdSA85YwzYNDYZGxJKf%0AVkJvs0eu6wj4gYsnyJQ%3D%0A

To download or print the invoice:

- Select Print or Download PDF in the top-right corner of the Add Invoice page.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1278276337/24676e5e41ed626521cb1b7c6e18/PrintDownload.png?expires=1763568000&signature=41ccafff9cf0baf6f4d19395d11ba8e4fdb1c3e550c91535aa7a5d439ae98780&req=dSIgHst5m4JcXvMW1HO4zT5YsRg0%2F2GKgVd1FfWnW7EgtC%2Bvd9K7B1oWFdTJ%0AylCuVI5iPQpMH6WMm5Y%3D%0A

- When either of these options is selected, a confirmation window will appear, asking if you would like to proceed with saving and printing the invoice.

- To make changes before saving the invoice, click 'Cancel.'

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1278279950/9bcd4d7cab6e4afc8ec88d90d041/PrintCancel.png?expires=1763568000&signature=c91752b902d15256888bafc6d3f3d5e5fc4bf690a7d2eec99eaeef38ab5ca1ad&req=dSIgHst5lIhaWfMW1HO4zTLVocl5wDHHrdFuEh83%2FJnuwN%2Bp4EuMhnDQfNI%2B%0A12buWraeVv%2FfXaZRmGk%3D%0A

- Click SAVE AND PRINT or SAVE AND DOWNLOAD to proceed after making any necessary changes (if applicable).

- When SAVE AND PRINT is selected, a Print window will open on your computer. - Choose a printer and adjust any additional settings (such as paper size or layout).
- Click Print to print a physical copy of the invoice.
 - Alternatively, select Save as PDF in the Destination drop-down and click Save to download the invoice.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1278281381/a8d21d5d4d93205504ca483303eb/SavePrint.png?expires=1763568000&signature=b141bf8cbaee010fb33a05b4b945fc2475d36bfe42e2f74df46616f5e8ddbc62&req=dSIgHst2nIJXWPMW1HO4zbU3JdhJyTUnB7kqmcQQ8SxlpcaOWsXf5Gz61e13%0ANR5OYB03%2F1obSo%2Buyg8%3D%0A

- When SAVE AND DOWNLOAD is selected, the invoice will be downloaded as a PDF file to your computer. - Open the PDF file from your downloads, then rename or print the invoice and you're all set!

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1278293482/cde9d012ceef06fd9cc5a47d8473/SaveDownload.png?expires=1763568000&signature=8be686e02ce00611b6609b22875e2642a401a8d0add0937e563dd70981ff2d0d&req=dSIgHst3noVXW%2FMW1HO4zYnfGzRCMf2NxVFJa5Vi9hY%2FpRDH4KpX3l37QVoM%0AR8a%2BuTPfTNEa2abv%2BKQ%3D%0A

Preview, Download, & Print an Invoice - HCP Mobile App (iOS)

To preview an invoice on mobile iOS devices (iPhone/iPad):

- Open a job in your HCP mobile app to access the Job Details page.

- Tap the Invoice icon (paper airplane) in the Action Bar at the top of your screen.

- You'll then be directed to the Invoice screen, where you can preview and edit the invoice before sending it to a customer.
