How to Copy Estimates

With Housecall Pro, you can easily duplicate existing estimates, saving time and reducing manual entry. Whether you need to resend an estimate with minor adjustments or create a similar one for another customer, duplication ensures accuracy and efficiency.

---

Copy Estimate Details to a New Estimate (Web)

Similar to copying job details to a new job/customer, you can copy line items and attachments from an existing estimate to a new one. You can also assign the estimate to a different customer, if necessary.

To copy an estimate to a new estimate:

- Open an estimate and hover over the option tab at the top of the page.

- Click the More icon (3 dots) that appear to the right.

- Select Copy to new estimate from the drop-down menu.

How to Convert (Copy) Estimates to Jobs

You can quickly convert an estimate into a job in Housecall Pro, carrying over all relevant details without re-entering information. This makes it seamless to move from pricing to scheduling work with just a few clicks.

---

Convert an Estimate to a Job (Web)

Once your customer has approved an estimate, you will need to create & schedule a new job/invoice for the work to be performed.

To convert the estimate and carry all the information over into a brand-new job, follow the steps below:

- Open an existing estimate or create a new estimate.

- Click Copy to Job in the Action Bar at the top of the page.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1226421620/d870d8571bf9dc0202ac49679781/Screenshot+2024-10-23+at+12_05_09%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=f6ba62d3ac2b7e0985fe182cc1e3784c078fc3c027ab2b1965c390d5a7cea435&req=dSIlEM18nIddWfMW1HO4zUCDEXiOSLWllAkRAg96QS9vUUddyfSMA6k44xmF%0AKR2%2FYaf3AWgpwcRt9pQ%3D%0A

- The "Copy to job" window will appear. If your estimate has multiple options, you can select the options you'd like to copy in this window.

- Click Copy (single-option estimates) or select the applicable options and click Approve and copy (multi-option estimates).

Convert an Estimate to a Job (iOS)

To copy an estimate to a job from the HCP mobile app on iOS:

- Open an estimate.

- Tap Copy to Job at top of your screen.

- Select Approve Estimate and Copy to New Job from the menu that appears at the bottom.

- You'll then be directed to the Job details page. All information from the estimate will be automatically implemented into the job fields, however you will need to schedule the new job itself.

How to Convert a Job to an Estimate

Once a job is created, you can convert it to an estimate without starting over and losing your hard work!

To convert a job to an estimate:

- Open a job and click the gear & wrench icon in the top right corner of the page.

- Select Convert to estimate from the drop-down menu.

I converted a job to an estimate. Where did the job go?

When you copy an estimate to a job, a copy of the estimate remains on file for you to reference in the future.

When you convert a job to an estimate, however, the job will no longer exist in the system. It has been changed from a job to an estimate with all line items, customer information, notes, and attachments still intact.

This estimate can always be copied back to a job when needed by following the steps listed above.

---

Why can't I convert my job to an estimate?

With Housecall Pro, you have the option to convert a job to an estimate at the click of a button. However, there are four reasons that a job may NOT be converted into an estimate within the system. So if you're looking for that option and don't see it, check the list below to make sure this job is eligible.

There are four reasons you can't convert a specific job into an estimate:

- The job already has transactions tied to it. - In the Housecall Pro system, payment can only be taken on a job and not on an estimate. So if a job already has a payment processed or recorded through it, it cannot be converted to an estimate in the system. If the payment was recorded by mistake, you can delete the payment and then convert the job to an estimate.

- The job has multiple segments. - Jobs that require multiple visits or multiple payments on a single project can be broken into segments. Once a job has been split into segments, it cannot be converted back to an estimate. If the job has multiple segments by mistake, you can delete the segments until only the single-segment job is left, then convert as usual.

- The job is part of a recurring series. - If a job is entered into the system as a part of a recurring series of jobs, it cannot be converted to an estimate. The system recognizes recurring jobs as pre-set appointments for work, such as a weekly clean or a 6-month system check. While the jobs can be moved, edited, and rescheduled, they can't be converted to an estimate.

- The job was already copied from an estimate and is linked to it. - When a job has been copied over from an original estimate, a copy of that estimate is kept in the system. The estimate and invoice will be linked together with an icon near the top right of each screen to give you easy access between the two. Since an estimate already exists for this job, the job can't be converted to a different estimate. This keeps duplicates from being created. You can always go back to edit the estimate as needed or delete the job that was created.

---

Can I change the customer on a job?

Yes, you can change the customer on an existing job by copying the job to a new one and assigning it to the correct customer. Please note that the initial job will still be active under the previous customer, but you can delete it if needed.
