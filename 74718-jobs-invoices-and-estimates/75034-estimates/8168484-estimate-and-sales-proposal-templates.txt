Estimate and Sales Proposal Templates
Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/8168484-estimate-and-sales-proposal-templates
Author: Nate H
Last updated: Updated over 2 months ago

Summary: Create templates for estimates and proposals on web to quickly and efficiently present details to customers with greater consistency.

This article provides a detailed overview of how to create templates for estimate and proposal options from the Housecall Pro web portal and mobile app.

---

Access Estimate and Sales Proposal Templates

To locate your Estimate & Sales Proposal Templates:

- Go to Price book in the top navigation bar of your HCP account on web. - Depending on the size of your browser window, you may need to click More and then select Price book from the drop-down.

- Select Estimate templates from the left menu.

Sales Proposal Templates

Creating Estimate Templates from Scratch

To create an estimate template from scratch:

- Select Price book from the navigation bar at the top of your account. - Depending on the size of your browser window, you may need to click More and then select Price book from the drop-down.

- Select Estimate templates from the left menu.

- Templates must be assigned to a category, so you'll need at least one category to begin creating templates.

- While default categories are provided, you can easily create a custom one by clicking the Add Category button in the upper-right corner of the page. - You can choose to keep or delete the default categories we created for you.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1061360571/b481818dfcf2cf9e66405f79/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=71404058973615e53ffbd046964982b199840b648520cf88c32e4fe0d0d87534&req=dSAhF8p4nYRYWPMW1HO4zSKcBkKA6K50jalsagZ8ZyNxVAX5qA6l793h8F6d%0AxNUsjLrKHolbGq1CxSY%3D%0A

- Once you've added a category (or choose to use a default one), you can add templates by clicking into the desired category and clicking ADD TEMPLATE. This will open up a form that you can use to define the template’s content.

- This includes the template name, Option details (Name, image, and description the customer sees when you send the estimate/proposal), and Line Items (Services & Materials).

Things to Note:

- You must be an Admin or an employee with the "Update company account info" permission to create Estimate Templates.

- Options details are visible to your customers; however, the template name is not.

- You can add a single image to the option details section in your Estimate Template, which is only applicable if you use the Sales Proposal Tool.

- Service and material line items - you can add as many service and material line items to your template as you want. You can either add line items manually in the template form, or you can pull the services and materials directly from your Price book.

- Line items pulled directly from your Price book will be synchronized unless you update the line items directly in the template:

Creating Templates from Existing Estimate Options

One of the most valuable aspects of templates is that you can quickly create them from options that you already have in your system.

If you have created estimate options in the past that you’d like to reuse for your future customers, you can do so by saving this option as a template by following the steps below.

To create Estimate Templates from existing estimate options:

- Open an estimate that you would like to use to create a template.

- Hover over the Option tab at the top of the Estimate Details page.

- Click the More icon (three dots) that appears to the right. This will open a menu with a few items to choose from.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/792288139/ce27ae8a1003ad087b154bdc/saveastemplate.gif?expires=1763568000&signature=f9295b205f4f21e124ba59ee282491f62211a1d6d382f0bfcbfbb40965dca065&req=cyklFMF2nIJWFb4f3HP0gGjHmgmjiSmVa3V6UxPgGtpoOY2jT48%2FNbiuKyun%0AZcg3k3zFt8hxIIpU%2FA%3D%3D%0A

- Select Save as template from the drop-down menu. This will open a window that you can use to fill out the details required to save the selected option as a template.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/792288582/95cd3e590abd170e569d88ac/saveastemplate2.png?expires=1763568000&signature=6135d7a9318a9ce1a8b2f708a0169387610f73291120d99317b7a1956eff23af&req=cyklFMF2mIldFb4f3HP0gPUlgqWW8n1StTtxf1nfszJVWzvZb%2BRyBy4tt8A2%0ATRwvlkNHLP9LW8T06g%3D%3D%0A

- Name the template and select the location (category) in the Price book you want to store your template in. - The name of the option will automatically pre-fill in the form. You can change the option name directly in this form.

- You will also be able to add the option’s description and cover image. This content will be prefilled if the option selected has the proposal presentation mode added to it.

Managing Templates in Your Price Book

You can manage templates similarly to how you manage other resources (i.e., service and material line items) in your Price book.

Specifically, the following actions can be performed:

- Categorizing templates - You can organize templates into categories. Each category can have an unlimited number of subcategories inside. - Additionally, categories can be updated, deleted, and reordered.

- Editing and removing templates - You can edit and remove templates freely and independently from the estimate/proposal creation flow.

- Reordering - Each template can be reordered within the category it’s located in.

- Moving location - You can change the location (i.e., the category) of the templates.

---

Templates and Price Book Line Items

When creating a template, you can create new service and material line items from scratch, but you can also pull line items directly from your Price book (similar to how you add line items from the Price book to an estimate or a job).

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/792291947/00953b288ac1d70165154223/synchronization+of+templates+and+pb.gif?expires=1763568000&signature=1b61eaa4510a2ebbc93be82de68a916eca8c18c758f1cb3fb50e6c4377457538&req=cyklFMB%2FlIVYFb4f3HP0gK36Op6TtK%2Fs9OjTw51lSoRRTjxeLolthze8U7sA%0AwKzFESw8I6We2sTDAQ%3D%3D%0A

If you pull the line item from the Price book to your template, and you don’t overwrite that line item afterward (i.e., you don’t edit that line item after adding it to the template), the line item will be synchronized with your Price book.

Synchronization means that if you update the line item in your Price book, this change will automatically apply to all templates that have the updated line items inside.

Other important aspects of the Price book synchronization with estimate/sales proposal templates:

- The synchronization works in one direction only ( i.e., changing the line item in the Price book applies to the templates, but changing the line item in a template does not synchronize back to the Price book). - Updating the line item in a template removes the synchronization.

- It’s not currently possible to add new line items to the Price book from a template.

- Overwriting anything about the line item pulled from the Price book on a template level removes the synchronization with the exception of quantity (that’s because the quantity field is not part of the Price book line items).
