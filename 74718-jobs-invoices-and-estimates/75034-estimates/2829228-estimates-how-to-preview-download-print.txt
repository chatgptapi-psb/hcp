How to Preview, Download, & Print Estimates

In Housecall Pro, managing estimates is simple and efficient. Whether you need to review an estimate you’ve created, download it for client sharing, or print a hard copy for documentation, the process is seamless.

This article will walk you through how to view the details of any estimate, download it as a PDF, and print it directly from your account. These easy steps ensure you can quickly provide estimates to clients and maintain accurate records for your business.

Preview, Download, & Print Estimates: HCP Web Portal

---

Preview an Estimate: HCP Web Portal

To preview an estimate in the HCP web portal:

- Open an estimate to view the Estimate Details page.

- Click the Send icon (paper airplane) in the Action bar at the top.

- And that's it! You'll then be directed to the Estimate preview.

Download an Estimate: HCP Web Portal

To download an estimate from the HCP web portal:

- Open an estimate to view the Estimate Details page.

- Click the Send icon (paper airplane) in the Action bar at the top of the Estimate Details page.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1319440563/edb99f532fae93e54cd103d3171b/PrintEstimate5.png?expires=1763568000&signature=340ba932b76731595ef84ac51fba60b0f7388019a62e030e521a76e27dee8016&req=dSMmH816nYRZWvMW1HO4zTFNbrdHmC8g7g9c1FX1r5%2FYff%2F46hKevMquYFR5%0AcLOgDHueqJPS2sNdwi0%3D%0A

- Click the gray download icon (downward-facing arrow over a line) at the top of the invoice.

- The file will be downloaded as a PDF to your computer.

Print an Estimate: HCP Web Portal

To print an estimate from the HCP web portal:

- Open an estimate to view the Estimate Details page.

- Click the Send button at the top of the Estimate details page.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1661231950/cef0ec6408264fadf93c5b5f5c5b/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=258c6c00f005a3d75deb6dee8c5eae1554206ec06638ee7de8082cc2da28ddfb&req=dSYhF8t9nIhaWfMW1HO4zdVaMxksZSgdbQBdOV8sOJ2ZoadFaqWPz2crj%2B%2Bk%0ASFGasqpl0qks8%2B4Mq8Y%3D%0A

- In the Estimate preview page, click the printer icon in the top right corner.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1661232472/dd6e6815d67c4e76e82426644e1b/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=4aef4893817d416be796a05e21200fd19322ec7138c2a7027b1b125669318b61&req=dSYhF8t9n4VYW%2FMW1HO4zYHbL2FGy6K2%2F9S8zORR8LwioUXtrpYJk10BNRwS%0AxltTLCVsk5oL%2B6x9yj4%3D%0A

- A new tab & Print window will open.

- Select your printing options in the Print window.

- Click Print in the bottom right corner. - You can choose a printer to print a physical copy of the invoice or select Save as PDF to download the invoice as a PDF document.

- Open an estimate to view the Estimate Details page.

- Tap the Send icon (paper airplane) at the top of your screen.

- Tap the Printer icon under the blue Next button.
