Multi-Option Estimates: HCP Web Portal

The multi-option estimate feature is great for presenting customers with different packages or service tiers. It lets you create and label the options to suit the way you operate your business.

---

Create Multi-Option Estimates (HCP Web Portal)

To create a multi-option estimate in the HCP web portal:

- Create a new estimate, or open an existing estimate to view the Estimate Details page.

- Click ADD OPTION at the top of the page.

- Select New option from the drop-down.

- Rename the option if you'd like, then click CREATE.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1304833664/4f28dcdcd692617eb8b8e3c2797e/NewOption2.png?expires=1763568000&signature=66389e65678061e704f91d35f91648a40f12d3227f323bb1a253c0871a176645&req=dSMnEsF9nodZXfMW1HO4zXbpRRoDO6djksoFdBieU8JqObfex4vPhMgbsAoy%0Aa0MgVUoBOrqjm1%2B%2FABw%3D%0A

Rename Estimate Options (HCP Web Portal)

To rename an estimate option in the HCP web portal:

- Open an estimate to view the Estimate Details page.

- Hover over the Option tab at the top of the page.

- Click the three dots that appear on the right side of the tab.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1306553369/2b5d538009cfd6137c41b1add097/HoverOption%28More%29.gif?expires=1763568000&signature=9fe265faefd89012526fe1ca9fa216855242ff1133e496e464aa61a316560980&req=dSMnEMx7noJZUPMW1HO4zQj7hveDcDYOPI3phOYYpmwzldHEW1bnRFdrd3QW%0A8azfhwOtKb35U%2FHoHRM%3D%0A

- Select Rename from the drop-down menu.

- Edit the Option name, then click Rename.

Cancel/Delete Estimate Options (HCP Web Portal)

To cancel an estimate option in the HCP web portal:

- Open an estimate to view the Estimate Details page.

- Hover over the Option tab for the option you wish to cancel.

- Click the three dots that appear to the right of the tab.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1319293361/5cbd0dfbefbf5ff9da4b0ed297eb/HoverOption-28More-29.gif?expires=1763568000&signature=c06439e38fce32378483673cc0672b1db9d011159fa17f6fb8e6a1d380e62cc2&req=dSMmH8t3noJZWPMW1HO4zXS%2BRvsquOa9Z%2BUuG4WLqftPHpUMd3wReQfoQsJI%0A4o%2FfMX%2B4OQPR5IFm60w%3D%0A

- Select Cancel (option name).

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1306570396/a12f2e58f69fe7b2c5d1737e91bf/CancelOption1.png?expires=1763568000&signature=48a085f84b32bfe4531e3dd149abafb453364730325f90d1d2ce174575d3f7c3&req=dSMnEMx5nYJWX%2FMW1HO4zXMMpxtnqk48EHkPXC2AQ56Sxayla7Ul9sAgYh6v%0AcXAJKxfGDiSd8ZjGw7E%3D%0A

Send Estimate Options to Customers (HCP Web Portal)

To send estimate options to customers in the HCP Web Portal:

- Open a multi-option estimate to view the Estimate Details page.

- On the multi-option estimate details page, click the Send icon to open the Estimate preview page.

- Under the Estimate Options section on the Estimate preview page, check or uncheck the boxes next to the options you would like to include or remove from the estimate before sending.

Multi-Option Estimates: HCP Mobile App (iOS)

Provide your customers with multiple estimate options of services to choose from with the HCP mobile app on iPhones and iPads.

The multi-option estimate feature is ideal for providing different packages or level-of-service options to a customer. You can create and name the options to fit the way you run your business.

---

Create Multi-Option Estimates: HCP Mobile App - iOS

To create estimate options in the HCP mobile app on iOS:

- Open an estimate to view the Estimate Details page.

- Tap the More icon (3 dots) in the top-right corner of your screen.

Multi-Option Approvals on Estimates

Multi-option approvals on estimates let you define whether your customer can approve just one option or multiple options within a single estimate.

In this section, you'll find information on how to:

- Adjust the approval settings on an individual estimate basis.
- Define the default approval settings for all your future estimates.

The approval settings allow you to specify how many options your customers can approve in multi-option estimates received by emails or texts.

By default, the approval settings for all your estimates will allow your customers to approve only one option. This is equivalent to the system's behavior before the multi-option approvals feature was introduced, which means that nothing will change for your customers until you decide to change the settings.

Changing Approval Settings for Individual Estimates

Changing the approval settings on the Estimate Details page of a given estimate will only affect that particular estimate.

- If you choose only one option, your customer will be able to approve only one option in the estimate. Once they approve the desired option, the remaining options will be declined automatically.

- On the other hand, if you choose multiple options, your customer will be able to approve as many options in the estimate as they wish.

- The approval settings can't be changed for an estimate if the approval settings are set to "multiple options" and two or more options are already approved (either by the customer or Pro-approved in HCP). - In such a case, the component to change the settings is disabled, and the appropriate information is displayed on hover.

To change the approval settings for an estimate:

- Click the Customer can approve component on the Estimates Details page. This will open a modal with two possible options:
​ - Only one option
 - When the customer approves one option, the rest will automatically be declined. You will still be able to Pro approve other options.
​ - Multiple options
 - When your customer approves one option, the rest won't be declined. Both you and the customer will still be able to approve other options.

- Click the radio button next to the approval settings you wish to apply to the estimate.

- Click Save to confirm.

- Select Estimates from the Settings menu on the left.

Important Considerations

- No matter what approval settings are set for your estimates, you will always be able to Pro approve options on your customer's behalf through the HCP web portal.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/897458547/ce2bef2c83b3801f7ae8d89b/Screenshot+2023-11-30+at+18.39.31.png?expires=1763568000&signature=e023a805e4de641813e55e5ab3dfc6a2bb103955aa4bc90a1bbe5032cbe390f5&req=fCkgEsx2mIVYFb4f3HP0gAmbJ2sjM3rWOIJteREg9VD9ud5DRhr6YFT2i%2FbG%0AZzl4EXLcqlsOLJVpLg%3D%3D%0A

- For now, this feature works on the web only, but we plan to add it to mobile soon. It's important to remember that the approval settings specified on the web will propagate to estimates created and/or sent from mobile apps. For example, if you set the default approval settings to "Customer can approve multiple options", and you create and send a multi-option estimate from a mobile app, your customer will be able to approve more than one option.

- The approval settings work for all estimates. They also work for the newly created Sales Proposal Tool 2.0. So, if you have access to SPT 2.0, you can use the same approval settings to adjust the approval behavior for all your estimates and proposals. The settings won't work for the proposals created with the old version of the tool, though. Click here to read more about the Sales Proposal Tool 2.0.
