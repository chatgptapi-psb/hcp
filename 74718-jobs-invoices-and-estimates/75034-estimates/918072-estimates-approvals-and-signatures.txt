Estimates: Approvals and Signatures
Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/918072-estimates-approvals-and-signatures
Author: Nate H
Last updated: Updated over 2 months ago

Summary: Mark your estimates as approved or let your customer do it for you!

There are multiple ways an estimate can be approved in Housecall Pro. A customer can approve it in person, online, or you can manually mark it as approved.

If you're collecting an in-person approval, you can capture the customer’s signature right on your phone!

---

Approve an Estimate in the Field (Mobile)

To approve an estimate in person using the HCP mobile app:

- Open an estimate.

- Click Approve in the action bar at the top of the page.

- Select Approve an estimate (iOS) or Approve estimate (Android) at the bottom of your screen.

Manually Approve or Decline an Estimate (Web)

If a customer verbally approves an estimate, or decides to decline after approving it:

- Open an estimate in your HCP account on web.

- Click the Approval button at the top of the estimate details page.

- Select Approve estimate or Decline estimate from the drop-down menu.

- After manually approving or declining an estimate, the Estimate status will be reflected as Pro Approved or Pro Declined.

Customer Approves or Declines an Estimate

After a customer receives their estimate via email or text, they'll have the option to approve or decline it.

Once they've made their selection(s), you will be notified via the support email on your Company Profile.

View Customer Signatures on Estimates

Mobile

To view a customer's signature on an estimate through the HCP mobile app:

- Open an estimate in the HCP mobile app.

- Click the Estimate button (paper plane icon) in the action bar at the top of your screen. This will direct you to the estimate preview.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1672271816/03415bd12310f91091e01e8b9f7a/IMAGE-2B4.png?expires=1763568000&signature=bdeea65c72e678c627de8404c4c85cae6e1faa8aea3849287f094f8de97558c9&req=dSYgFMt5nIleX%2FMW1HO4zQPCQnLCtPhkkxn%2BG8O6%2BmrSvevbFq1J2%2FbxEVdh%0AqkONDqNIEbinzcZjIB4%3D%0A

- Scroll down to the bottom of the estimate preview to view your customer's signature.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1672272027/83d676da7a8d516db092ff93b7d9/IMAGE-2B5.png?expires=1763568000&signature=ebfbc35e33b759e3e00f3ff48c1f54211bc84227f37f1b2f9f2ba47f5acd6a38&req=dSYgFMt5n4FdXvMW1HO4zddxobCzFmCThYTXCRdxscLAH41SLsNV%2FHTlOo1Q%0AhsBG0fjfdSWMz2wCmlw%3D%0A

Web

To view a customer's signature on an estimate through the HCP web portal:

- Open an estimate in your HCP account on web.

- Scroll down to the Approvals section on the left.

- Click View signed agreement.
