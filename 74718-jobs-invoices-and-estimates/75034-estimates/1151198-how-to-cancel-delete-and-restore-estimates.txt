How to Cancel, Delete, and Restore Estimates
Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/1151198-how-to-cancel-delete-and-restore-estimates
Author: Nate H
Last updated: Updated over 7 months ago

Summary: Cancel or delete an unneeded quote from your account, or restore an estimate that has been canceled/deleted.

Cancel an Estimate

To cancel an estimate:

- Open an estimate to view the Estimate Details page.

- At the top of the page, hover your mouse over the estimate option you need to cancel. - If there is only one option, this will most likely read "Option #1" unless the option name has been changed.

- Click the More icon (3 dots) that appears to the right of the option tab.

- Select the second to last option from the drop-down: "Cancel Option #1," or "Cancel (name of option)."

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1335177389/258a694b4265c6e9fa5bdcad96fb/Screenshot+2025-01-14+at+3_35_24%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=8c7a1fb987d0d56c9b3c769f22a0047a815e5865c1882e2aba2498b32d7b70af&req=dSMkE8h5moJXUPMW1HO4zeMWocilXu8RqnSCwSFaPh6pMKnuVo4ZnRaaV9nK%0AlpR0RSRci0iyBtA7WnQ%3D%0A

- A confirmation window will appear, showing you which option will be canceled.

- You can notify your customer of the cancellation via email and/or text by checking the 'Notify customer' option. - If you'd prefer not to send a notification, leave this box unchecked.

- When you're ready, select 'Yes, Cancel the Option' in the confirmation window to cancel the estimate.

Delete an Estimate

Since changes are always happening with your business, Housecall Pro makes it easy to delete estimates that have been created in your account.

To delete an estimate you no longer need:

- Open an estimate to view the Estimate Details page.

- At the top of the page, hover your mouse over the estimate option you need to delete. If there is only one option, this will most likely read Option #1 unless the option name has been changed.

- Click the More icon (3 dots) that appears on the right side of the option tab.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1335173640/9a343fb2ba40fa5580b7d17be5ae/Screenshot+2025-01-14+at+3_31_26%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=99cdf538a632812b74613b31c3a27aa2f1231d5384b79d28eb63f1634d257474&req=dSMkE8h5nodbWfMW1HO4zd5qiBMaWJcEtBS5N6qZu8fGvsho%2BQucpWtoDXe9%0A7bp05llE124xc9Oh5aU%3D%0A

- Select the last option in the drop-down menu: "Delete Option #1," or "Delete (name of option)."

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1335178070/695c2d1b259d3aa72ae9f88744ad/Screenshot+2025-01-14+at+3_36_04%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=aa2602846dad99a43658d08ae7ad703199a0efa61effaa27af24f1fd25c084f5&req=dSMkE8h5lYFYWfMW1HO4zXlCB3BQR5rXYHXIksu82r0FJ3zQFwCd4y4x4xXs%0A4NScZEAJMpgFQ%2Fskr30%3D%0A

- A confirmation window will appear, showing you which option will be deleted.

- Click 'Delete' in the confirmation window to delete the estimate.

Restore an Estimate

To restore an estimate that was canceled or deleted:

- Navigate to the customer's profile and select the 'Estimates' tab.

- In the Status column: - Canceled estimates will be listed as "Canceled" (in red text).
- Deleted estimates will be listed as "Pro canceled."

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1417112529/5ab01519ac71a2223d91d1465e55/Screenshot+2025-03-11+at+12_24_50%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=2e2b1466dcafe5aac5eda3eadc1d80847f4c570a4ddbfadec4557f8334911879&req=dSQmEch%2Fn4RdUPMW1HO4zcWdIAP8UJr5S6DmV%2Bm2N1YlM93gWwhX7f2ppwUR%0AV%2FGUzJYaB9wHBa44rKQ%3D%0A

- Select the estimate you need to restore, and you'll be taken to the Estimate details page.

- Click the "Restore estimate" button in the yellow bar at the top of the page, and that's it! The estimate will be restored instantly and can be edited as needed.
