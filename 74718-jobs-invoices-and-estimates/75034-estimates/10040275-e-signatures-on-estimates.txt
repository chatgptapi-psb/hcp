E-Signatures on Estimates
Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/10040275-e-signatures-on-estimates
Author: Nate H
Last updated: Updated over a month ago

Summary: E-Signatures protect your business from disputes and other liabilities by letting you gather proof of approval before work begins.

E-Signatures on Estimates enable you to collect a signature when you send estimates to your customers via email or SMS. This removes the need to print, physically sign, and scan documents, allowing you to close deals faster.

In this article, you will find detailed information about collecting e-signatures on your estimates on the web version of Housecall Pro.

---

Turning E-Signatures On/Off

To turn E-Signatures on Estimates on or off:

- Click the gear icon in the top right corner of your HCP account to go to Settings.

- Select Estimates from the Settings menu on the left.

- Click the Configuration tab on the Estimate settings page.

- Click the toggle to the right of Require e-signatures for approval to enable or disable e-signatures.

Signing the Estimate

- First, create an estimate and send it to your customer by email or SMS.

- When your customer opens your estimate and approves an option, they will be prompted to provide their signature.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1340205669/43156ce5d31197ac803766a32663/Screen-Recording-2025-01-17-at-1.gif?expires=1763567100&signature=3e1366110a392554d76c6667d1bba4c2cef671081c7fa10c8f1630a07daf8b6b&req=dSMjFst%2BmIdZUPMW1HO4zQonHrBrOAc8iqJ3Ioke5f%2BmlYyFG9Hp6iDlB%2BKr%0ANJbroNauPPgiQUmJiDw%3D%0A

- The customer has to first provide their name. This name will later be displayed next to the signature.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1227594630/6f7d5c1c2619d24f28797a9e1d56/Screenshot+2024-10-24+at+15_28_04.png?expires=1763567100&signature=32d314384de4a13cfc46732eb177c9d091dd656d3a5f47337cc793efd70c2926&req=dSIlEcx3mYdcWfMW1HO4zYUDbm43Ec7f7%2BQZ2lfDyFvNIoREMimGD7Sq98I5%0ArWuwYL1nwIqjRkjDTBU%3D%0A

- Next, they can decide whether they want to leave the signature typed:

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1227595180/63bac1ddccf13adb7354b31aee56/Screenshot+2024-10-24+at+15_28_45.png?expires=1763567100&signature=3419bc03fa159cf5eaad0a8af27aafee70c06250721f2c6897e86d252a392434&req=dSIlEcx3mIBXWfMW1HO4zfZYfH7KbsuGn0czLgENTe0WPLtwS%2FqNjBCq3jRF%0APVkvOFIgMiqPZpskwX8%3D%0A

- Or, if they prefer to draw it:

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1227595806/4ded5e3ed6ce0dbc92c2c1d96fe8/Screenshot+2024-10-24+at+15_29_39.png?expires=1763567100&signature=d2b5f6c03af380087f075e239300943500ce92241104d51dc28b1417ac2ab46b&req=dSIlEcx3mIlfX%2FMW1HO4zUvPdv4bmNpkoCWz7niwUbiZN%2B4PaROdi5LYPO%2Bc%0Akl7f%2BpbbzLhyfVdExBA%3D%0A

- Once the customer successfully provides their signature, the estimate is approved!

Accessing the Signed Copy of the Estimate

- Both you and your customer will receive a signed copy of the approved estimate via email.

- This copy is a PDF file containing your customer's signature, along with the name, IP address, and timestamp of the moment the signature was provided.

- The PDF also contains the approved estimate and the terms and conditions your customer has agreed to.
