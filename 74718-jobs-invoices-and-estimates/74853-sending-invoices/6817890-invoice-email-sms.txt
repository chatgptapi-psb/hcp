How to Edit Your Default Invoice Email & SMS

To edit your default Invoice Email and SMS:

- Click the gear icon in the top right corner of your HCP account to go to Settings.

- Select Invoices from the Settings menu on the left.

- Click the Email and SMS tab on the Invoices settings page.

- If you are navigating here for the first time, you will see the Housecall Pro default-generated content.

How to Create Default Invoice Messages on Emails Using Marketing AI

To create default invoice messages on emails with Marketing AI:

- Click the gear icon in the top right corner of your HCP account to go to Settings.

- Select Invoices from the Settings menu on the left.

- Click the Email and SMS tab on the Invoices settings page.

Key Call Outs

Email Content:

- HTML embedding is not permitted at the moment for this content.

- If your techs have the ability to invoice, they will see the last content you have saved when they are in the preview invoice flow on mobile, ahead of sending it out in the invoice email or text.

- If your techs do not have the permission to edit invoice messages, they will not be able to modify the default content you have set up in this section.

- The character counter is only an estimation of characters. For more info on what each variable means and the character counts associated with each variable, check for more info here.

SMS Content:

- We will automatically append the invoice preview link for your customer, and this will not count against your character limit.

- The character counter is only an estimation of characters. For more info on what each variable means and the character counts associated with each variable, check for more info here.
