Add/View Attachments on Jobs and Estimates (Web)

You can attach any files uploaded to a Job or Estimate to an Invoice or Estimate that’s sent to a customer.

To add/view attachments on a job or estimate:

- Open a job or estimate to view the details page and scroll down to the Attachments section on the left.

Sending Attachments with an Invoice or Estimate (Web)

To send attachments with an invoice or estimate:

- Open a job or estimate to view the details page.

- Click the Invoice or Send Estimate button in the action bar at the top of the page.

- On the Invoice preview page, click the pencil icon in the Attachments section to choose which attachments you'd like to send the customer along with the Invoice.

- Select the attachments and/or checklists you'd like to include. You can select attachments for the specific Job or Estimate, as well as attachments from previous Jobs or Estimates, or any attachments from the Customer or Property Profiles.

- Once all attachments have been added, click Save in the bottom right corner of the window.

- You can see the number of attachments in the Attachments section once you've finished adding them.

- Ensure your other invoice settings are correct and send!

- Follow the same steps above for Estimates.

Customer View

When your customer receives the Invoice or Estimate, any attachments will be visible in the body of the email and can be opened to view or download.

These attachments will also be shown on the Invoice and Estimate preview pages.

Downloading an Invoice with Attachments

You are able to print or download an Invoice directly. If you’d like to embed images in the downloaded/printed PDF, you can do so by enabling an Invoice Preference setting.

To enable the Invoice Preference setting:

- Go to your Settings by clicking the gear icon in the top right corner of your account.

- Select Invoices > Invoice Preferences tab from the Settings menu on the left.

- Click the toggle to the right of the setting, "Include images when printing or downloading invoices." Doing so will embed images in the downloaded/printed invoice PDF.

Downloading or Sharing Attachments Directly with Customers (Web)

Attachments can be downloaded or shared directly with your customers from any Job or Estimate.

To download or share Job/Estimate attachments with customers:

- Navigate to the Attachments card on the Job or Estimate page.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1615463350/7f4cf1f5f741189d5048955ac77b/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=b904e222308325f6fe65edf0e3e8fd27f3713a37cfd3c0f0cb18c1fb5a968e58&req=dSYmE814noJaWfMW1HO4zWcz3WqTAunZxHPUyipjVcVv%2FEXjS4h1zoCz7wzn%0Ax4TKzLYxf66wffNLLyU%3D%0A

- Select the attachments you'd like to share or download by clicking the checkbox on each one.

- Click the blue Share button. (If you’d prefer to download the attachments instead, click the Download icon.)

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1615472135/ceb4231742e0ed009f17f9eb667f/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=dfd7f155cdf97996ae97a4aa81944beeb5fc525fc1cab9808f42b35e47640793&req=dSYmE815n4BcXPMW1HO4zYkGuCbBHHPu%2B8Jj5%2FH%2BUQQAMhw3mOVB92Xv3dZQ%0Apxu3xNAvEnMwv66Iqwg%3D%0A

- Choose either Email or Text as your delivery method.

- Enter the recipient's email address or phone number, and adjust the message if needed. (You can send to multiple email addresses, but only one phone number at a time.)

- Click Share to send.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1615479670/4571757d95b45972c222a9bf7eb0/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=703d57768f3d33cc1c05b15a5062cbe72f03517d6c3f6887f7a69f214918ef53&req=dSYmE815lIdYWfMW1HO4zamLhNzgkTK%2FJ2mpWpS14Don%2B8vNXPJ5GPjiIpf5%0ADxDrKIKee8Es1dz5M9g%3D%0A

- The customer will then receive a link to view the attachments shared with them.
