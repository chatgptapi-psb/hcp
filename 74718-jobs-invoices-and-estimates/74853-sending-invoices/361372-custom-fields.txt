Custom Fields
Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/361372-custom-fields
Author: Ian H
Last updated: Updated over 2 months ago

Currently, Housecall Pro is not set up to allow for custom fields. This includes but is not limited to custom invoice display, custom fields on the customer profile, and custom messaging to the customer. We do hope to build these features into the system with our future updates. Please continue reading below for some great alternative features.

---

Message on Invoice, Receipt, and Estimates

In Settings > Company > Profile, you may include a custom message that will automatically appear on all invoices, receipts and estimates going forward.

Click here to learn more about this field.

---

Tags

Tags are used throughout Housecall Pro as unique identifiers and are a great way to better manage lead, customers, estimates, jobs and invoices. Click here to learn more about using Tags.
