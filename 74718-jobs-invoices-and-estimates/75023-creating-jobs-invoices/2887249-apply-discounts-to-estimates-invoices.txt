Apply Discounts to Estimates & Invoices

Author: Nate H

Summary: Include a percentage or dollar amount discount on estimates and or invoices.

How to Apply Discounts

Follow the steps below to apply discounts to invoices or estimates:

- Open a job or estimate to view the details page.

- Click Add discount under Subtotal in the Line items section.

- Select whether you'd like to apply the discount as a percentage or dollar amount.

- Use the Description field to include a brief note indicating the discount reason (Seasonal Promo, Military Discount, Loyalty Discount, etc.). Keep in mind, customers will see the description on their invoice.

- Multiple Dollar amount discounts can be added by clicking Add discount after you've added the first Dollar amount discount.

- Click Save to update your changes - and that's it!

How Discounts Affect Tax

When you apply a discount to a job that includes both taxable and non-taxable items, tax is calculated on the taxable amount after discounts are properly allocated, not on the original taxable amount. We apply the percentage discounts directly to taxable items, and we proportionally allocate fixed discounts.

We calculate two totals:

- Taxable items total (only items marked as taxable)
- All items total

Why we do this:

- Ensures discounts are fairly split between taxable and non-taxable items
- Complies with tax regulations that require tax on net amounts after discounts
- Prevents mathematical errors when mixing different item types
