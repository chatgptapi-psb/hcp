How to Set Up Auto Invoicing
Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/2786247-how-to-set-up-auto-invoicing
Author: Nate H
Last updated: Updated over 4 months ago

Summary: Send your customer their open/unpaid invoices automatically daily, weekly, monthly, or annually.

Set Up and Manage Auto Invoicing

Auto Invoicing can be set up on the Customer Profile or from the Job Details page of an existing job (one-time or recurring). Either method will apply to all unpaid invoices listed in the customer's profile.

After Auto Invoicing has been set up, you can manage the Auto Invoice settings from either a customer's profile or any job for that customer.

---

Set Up Auto Invoicing (Customer Profile)

- Click on 'Customers' in the navigation bar of your Housecall Pro account.

- Select a customer from your Customers List, and ensure that you are on the Profile tab of their Customer Profile.

- Scroll down and select the Auto Invoice box on the left.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1219861243/cf70e419fa99fb893495227f5c96/Screenshot+2024-10-17+at+12_31_42%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=243a80ae066c138e03ec8f6a7f5e710ec71c3182638972feaba88d66110430d7&req=dSImH8F4nINbWvMW1HO4zXc5wYSbfg6XkoecO9vxUQnZeb1xIwTcqxP7aLYI%0AoL5gKlNvfrXB8yXtZPY%3D%0A

- The Auto Invoice window will appear on your screen.

- Click on the Recurrence drop-down box and select how often you want the auto invoice to occur: - Never (select this option if you would like to turn off auto invoicing). 
- Daily
- Weekly
- Monthly
- Yearly

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1243868729/9044d51ea412caeff05bfddeeaf1/AutoInvoice1-2.png?expires=1763568000&signature=1925747af5c8a7e65ca43bb65f1afc02763b5df373c5527beb82351e8050c4f1&req=dSIjFcF4lYZdUPMW1HO4zdiQRwkIpVAUCuRHsqXe0wbsschqkz2eb6BxzQf4%0AWzE8gTyqhqftvOeSFD8%3D%0A

- After selecting the recurrence, you can also choose when the recurrence repeats. - Repeats every: 
 - (X) amount of days, weeks, months, or years. 
- Repeats on: 
 - Select the specific day(s) of the week, month, or year the auto invoice will be sent out.

- Once your options have been selected, click 'Save' in the Auto Invoice window.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1249202948/4360fcdb6db2d7717a7b33b56a9b/AutoInvoice11.png?expires=1763568000&signature=1916d707bf39d4aac49509fbe13f97469bee8551b8d329ca0d2d491df566c27c&req=dSIjH8t%2Bn4hbUfMW1HO4zW%2FTCNGR%2BEjboumb%2BCjBVzR05z4517DIArmGO801%0A9iPp4s6E0aK7T7e0FFw%3D%0A

- Your customer will receive auto invoices on the date selected for all unpaid invoices in their Customer Profile.

- The auto invoice selection will appear on the Customer Profile in the Auto Invoice box. - Click the drop-down arrow to view a list of the dates the auto invoices will be sent to your customer.

Set Up Auto Invoicing (Job Details Page)

After creating a job, you'll have the option to set up Auto Invoicing from the Job Details page.

To set up Auto Invoicing from the Job Details page:

- Open a job to view the Job Details page.

- Scroll down and click on the Auto Invoice box on the left, and the Auto Invoice window will appear on your screen.

Edit Auto Invoicing

- Click on 'Customers' in the navigation bar of your Housecall Pro account.

- Select a customer from your Customers List, and ensure that you are on the Profile tab of their Customer Profile.

- Scroll down and click on the pencil icon in the Auto Invoice box to edit.

- Make any changes necessary to the recurrence and click 'Save' in the Auto Invoice window.

Turn Off Auto Invoicing

- Click on 'Customers' in the navigation bar of your Housecall Pro account.

- Select a customer from your Customers List, and ensure that you are on the Profile tab of their Customer Profile.

- Scroll down and click the pencil icon in the Auto Invoice box to edit the recurrence.

- Select 'Never' from the Recurrence drop-down.

- Click 'Save' in the Auto Invoice window.
