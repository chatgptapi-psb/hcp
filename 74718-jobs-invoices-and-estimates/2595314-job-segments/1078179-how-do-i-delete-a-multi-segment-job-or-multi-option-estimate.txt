How do I delete a multi-segment job or multi-option estimate?
Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/1078179-how-do-i-delete-a-multi-segment-job-or-multi-option-estimate
Author: Alyssa Moretti
Last updated: Updated over a month ago

Summary: Delete a job or estimate with multiple options

You already know how to create jobs with multiple segments or estimates with more than one option. But things change! So let's learn how to delete these jobs/estimates.

The process is nearly the same for estimates and jobs, other than this: with jobs the menu will say 'segment,' with estimates the menu will say 'option.'

To delete a segmented job, you first need to delete any extra segments. After opening a job, from the job details page, hover over the segment you would like to delete at the top of the job, and click the 3 horizontal dots.

A drop-down menu will appear, you can then select the 'Delete Segment' option:
