Summary: Learn how to create, rename, invoice, cancel, and delete job segments to manage multi-day jobs or jobs that require more than one visit.

Segments on the HCP Web Portal

---

Add Segments to a Job (HCP Web Portal)

To add a segment to a job in the HCP web portal:

- Open the job you want to add a segment to.

- Click the gear and wrench icon in the top right corner of the Job Details page.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1228263887/f31f82b7484420973f060d5143e8/Screenshot+2024-10-24+at+7_46_10%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=f1419f42ddfab7bb89b46e58ad94b0f7d3d51e7b450be4cbf33353176945ca9c&req=dSIlHst4nolXXvMW1HO4zXCloyg3XUaXW84u4evKwmy1Mi4i%2B9Q%2BC%2FMHc4gy%0AyeBa16yKSzs10qbS9nc%3D%0A

- Select Add segment from the drop-down menu. The Create new segment window will appear.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1228265210/e1e739ff5d98d578983d46520223/Screenshot+2024-10-24+at+7_46_36%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=388542453536f227af6362534e3dfb961dd02d16e969d0a9f7c8799a1053577c&req=dSIlHst4mINeWfMW1HO4zW%2BXGs%2BPU%2BdOq%2BJBMRVsSjc0iWM1nPZbT1IZNM73%0A91f%2ByMhvK5Dk9vRdzQw%3D%0A

- (Optional) Rename the segment, then click Create in the bottom right corner of the window.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1228265680/0354bf5793c42eeb99f782bb23eb/Screenshot+2024-10-24+at+7_47_08%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=ae22c4387decee09b8ab1304af4a2bd53035f42993f039145d6703554112df01&req=dSIlHst4mIdXWfMW1HO4zc%2BBgO7TfQC0JrynBOTnP0bdJ6x3%2B3W3l4mG77zu%0AiBG8sOtRm0hvlZ%2B29pc%3D%0A

- This will create a new job segment, where you can enter separate invoice details, scheduling, and notes. - Segment #1 will keep all the existing information you've already entered. 
 
- Segment #2 will start blank, ready for details related to the next portion of the job.

- You can repeat this process to add as many segments as needed. The job will maintain a single invoice number, with sub-numbers (e.g., Invoice #6-2) assigned to each segment.

- To switch between segments, click the Segment tabs at the top left of the Job Details page.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1228266051/81927a433b2f049835e8c21e296a/Screenshot+2024-10-24+at+7_47_54%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=8d247babcd03cfca5852ab253ede3ee1aa595571fe101dd344f9e626df4a24ed&req=dSIlHst4m4FaWPMW1HO4zbZwV2hFuKzr0d9ObkM%2FthvpJvKEUVdDUUed2S0v%0AO%2Fng3lNj7XNDNaInAj4%3D%0A

Invoice Multiple Segments (HCP Web Portal)

You can choose to invoice segments individually or group multiple segments into a single invoice. Before sending the invoice, you have the option to add or remove segments as needed.

To invoice multiple segments in the HCP web portal:

- Open a job to view the details page.

- Click Invoice (paper plane icon) in the action bar at the top of the page.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1584289854/9db66ee0a5359104ad4454f33797/Screenshot+2025-06-23+at+8_30_01%E2%80%AFAM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=dbb992c4aa71708e8f72f9dfe9f2b0fe7efe5af9283e02a9f400ce10c5a6eb79&req=dSUvEst2lIlaXfMW1HO4zapUmtm2M%2Baw%2FqzxfAVqQ44%2B3YnkCG0TDjLLNkhq%0A9bH5kziZQXeAx90jtLQ%3D%0A

- In the Invoice preview, click the pencil icon under the Segments section.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1584292022/34e22a0cb096045524c27bef7fa1/x+Send+invoice.png?expires=1763568000&signature=364daf2a33b1a860b28c162d75bcf72dceead759143760a0e803c1c25fd2c7e3&req=dSUvEst3n4FdW%2FMW1HO4zZfNKcjDm9WKrDHT5jFdOt44PxTNEoO1Jk3lOYmX%0AeWam4yYlYo90T0uL7ok%3D%0A

- Then, click SAVE to apply your changes.

Cancel or Delete a Segment from a Job (HCP Web Portal)

To cancel or delete a segment from a job:

- First, open a job to view the details page.

- Select the segment you'd like to cancel/delete and click the More icon (three dots) to the right.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1349361024/a93f72e97dda2299280f656002f9/Screenshot+2025-01-23+at+5_49_02%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=747cdce6d39be4769051dcc172fb7b6d95ba3cb41e92914e80592f2e9bed0ee9&req=dSMjH8p4nIFdXfMW1HO4zZgEHl%2BOJNql3D8cEsLqtGMxpQdmPAS%2B78lyKosG%0AazFjFcYnlVHxo9CBUnw%3D%0A

- Click Cancel (Segment name) or Delete (Segment name) from the drop-down menu, and a confirmation window will appear.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1349374129/0ad9126220b1ad95e716f41bab4b/Screenshot+2025-01-23+at+5_53_29%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=3c2a4df3e112d82691433fb495532bead64336b93a2f2e8d0a35a06e9e9ddcc8&req=dSMjH8p5mYBdUPMW1HO4zSPekqhlyidnRyAit0X%2BzYA6csmUGU6m3nCT0UZt%0AXGcTnZeOh74fnQ3AtzU%3D%0A

- Canceling a segment works the same way as canceling a job. If the Notify customer box isn't already selected, you can choose to notify the customer by checking it. (This option is preselected based on the customer’s notification preferences.)

- When you're ready, click Yes, Cancel the Segment.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1349376635/d6b4f96dd9e9a6d4abd9f0814b51/Screenshot+2025-01-23+at+5_57_25%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=2ed7c9bdfcda17fe1060975cb1571f4c9774f8832cfed977ed737ecd923c0308&req=dSMjH8p5m4dcXPMW1HO4za3nZt%2FP6W%2BFoamNR49iZhqA0FWC1ocyUXrBHZeG%0AgRli1L9%2Fc6NG1eDd3ks%3D%0A

- To delete the segment, click Delete in the confirmation window to confirm.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1349378698/8ece70917d7952ecc51d9462bdd5/Screenshot+2025-01-23+at+5_56_09%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=09df6f70f789488a44ba33c7d01bcb856f279974cd12fa5f548115e68ddb94a6&req=dSMjH8p5lYdWUfMW1HO4zcUzgse2JVgQCohC41JwFwPyiBNaOOU3u5sPFq6t%0AYa6lAJDfWSzRDQNgcZg%3D%0A

Delete Segments (Android)

Delete one or more segments from a job. To completely delete a job, you must delete all segments.

To delete a segment on the HCP mobile app on Android:

- Open a job and select the segment you'd like to delete.

- Tap the More icon in the top right corner of your screen.

- Select Delete and confirm that you'd like to delete the segment.

Invoice Multiple Segments (HCP Mobile App - Android)

You can add or remove multiple segments to an invoice as long as they have line items and a due amount.

To invoice multiple segments in the HCP mobile app on Android:

- Open a job and tap Invoice (paper plane icon) at the top of your screen.

- In the Invoice preview, tap the wrench icon to add segments to an invoice.
