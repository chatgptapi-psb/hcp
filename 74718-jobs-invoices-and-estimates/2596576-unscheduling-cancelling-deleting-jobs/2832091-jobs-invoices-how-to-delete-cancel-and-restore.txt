Summary: Learn how to delete, cancel, or restore a job easily through the HCP web portal with these simple steps.

How to Delete a Job/Invoice

If you need to delete an invoice, follow these steps to delete a job. The invoice associated with that job will be deleted.

To delete a Job or Invoice:

- Open a job to access the Job Details page.

- Click the gear & wrench icon in the top-right corner of the page.

- Select Delete job from the drop-down.

- Click Delete in the confirmation window to delete the job.

How to Cancel a Job

When a job is canceled, an email and text notification will be sent to the customer, and the job will show as canceled in their Customer Profile.

To cancel a job, follow these steps:

- Open a job to access the Job Details page.

- Click the gear & wrench icon in the top-right corner of the page.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1203465749/9669588a7022c46193a41f477a4b/Screenshot+2024-10-03+at+7_40_23%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=013f3a855085f3bd458d950ec0249fd4a83843341227b55de3766cfbdbec983d&req=dSInFc14mIZbUPMW1HO4zXTvNdc8aB%2BY8PfYf%2B486swUPcop2u9ytqbt%2FbPe%0AnsrcBY5wKZacRwDE8M8%3D%0A

- Select "Cancel job" from the drop-down.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1203468057/0f2e528a93e5550744f9e224d397/Screenshot+2024-10-03+at+7_42_33%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=d478d1ee6629c8ce9834b7f842145a051387126ff3504639c38940e8cb101300&req=dSInFc14lYFaXvMW1HO4zTZQN2u39961nEbPcCa1VPyif0v0W4mqZWF19rUc%0AkcbqdrWud3q9vb0X2xk%3D%0A

- The Notify Customer checkbox will be pre-selected based on the customer's notification preferences. - If you want to notify the customer about the cancellation, ensure the checkbox is checked.
- To prevent the notification from being sent, simply uncheck the box.

- Click "Yes, Cancel the Job" to confirm.

- After canceling a job, your customer will automatically receive a notification via email/text (if applicable) to inform them of the cancellation.

How to Restore Jobs/Invoices

If a job was accidentally deleted or canceled, we've got you covered! You can quickly restore a job (and the invoice associated with it) by following these steps:

- Click the Customers tab in the navigation bar at the top of your account.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1203478067/0fb97ba7f2964267f1cf5d5fe7ba/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=9faf8f1e28566715c966b7d2822431beae2e98b664157ff4d06dd3e05c7e1c3e&req=dSInFc15lYFZXvMW1HO4zRV2B3S9lo4YCIw0NPO67AnfqAnHQeB%2BQ5kX1iPp%0A6ykybXW1qQUNviJC11Y%3D%0A

- Select Jobs from the menu on the left.

- Click the Actions button in the top-right corner of the page, then select "Restore jobs" from the drop-down.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1203478069/a9c127c7aaa5203c7719adfe3b9c/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=16dd105ae7cc2d0efacad7225579d7f49d0bd4dc983e77b5bbc4b9109d5c43c2&req=dSInFc15lYFZUPMW1HO4zVkrowT4gDojtkC5dcOVvlwf1d1puNjpPnLowX4O%0AGgfLErDkXp3cLUDNlFg%3D%0A

- Click Restore on the line of the job you need to restore.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1203478068/b4dd47f93d82b9b806fbc08fe203/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=520d5c0df0e0bad6306dc1016e4565cf1da80fb170d81a7c6c5fadeac1e04894&req=dSInFc15lYFZUfMW1HO4zazgZu6sg3%2F3QZl74L5roIYEgvUocjSCnsciW7cR%0AG%2Fn9xsquBTaMkZBRWpk%3D%0A

- Once the process is complete, both the job and its associated invoice will be successfully restored.

- You will be automatically redirected to the Job Details page, where you can now reschedule the job to a new time and date.
