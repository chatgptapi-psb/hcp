Scheduling, Invoicing, and Payments Training Overview

In this Scheduling, Dispatch, and Payments Training, we provide a comprehensive overview of the essential workflows for managing scheduling, dispatching, and payments in Housecall Pro. This training is ideal for office admins and owners who need a thorough understanding of how to schedule jobs and estimates effectively.

In this training, we will cover:

- Setting up estimates in Housecall Pro
- Converting estimates into jobs
- Dispatching your field tech team
- Best practices for using the mobile app, tailored for owner-operators and field techs
- Invoicing and payments
- A quick guide to setting up recurring jobs and multi-day visits

Step-by-Step Instructions

Step 1: Create, Schedule, and Dispatch a Job

To create, schedule, and dispatch a job:

- Click the New button in the top navigation bar of your HCP account.

- Select 'Job' from the drop-down.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1178294258/da6dc66f1769615af1afca26/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=cc81f6bd8fd0ab5ed8ff3928130e3ea7d42c07b475ad560e9874c4a333d6ea16&req=dSEgHst3mYNaUfMW1HO4zUBsKutV7GztrurgUktLmk3mhV%2FrjWgm%2FAqgYfFD%0AMyYOOAHCppMb%2F1g%2FnKU%3D%0A

- Box 1 - Customer Information: In the Customer field, type the name of an existing customer or click '+ NEW CUSTOMER' to add a new customer.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/276096620/4c7cb0acdc94f9355b96a586/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=fd8051319e0c90fbb80fe5dcde2b6453effb4bfa64a8aebc1755c750a657ad45&req=dichFsB4m4NfFb4f3HP0gOd1Q79a0UdyX9Q3Rlu3Hc5kOHsNf9ak50P7Ajlv%0AqGYm0IC41IFKxyo3eA%3D%3D%0A

- Box 2 - Schedule: Click the Schedule box to open the calendar and begin scheduling the job.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1334949415/71fcb8cc2eab73e97878efaf62c4/image+%2835%29.png?expires=1763568000&signature=52f7000e20d39dcd0cd035f824d8fa31ad276804bec0a25c70543ab580191ec2&req=dSMkEsB6lIVeXPMW1HO4ze9DuY8pcyAN1eX5X%2F9EYUtjsPvrk9OMwtlTCH13%0AM2MBWOr5xQZoz7JHIX8%3D%0A

- Click or drag & drop the job anywhere on the calendar to schedule the job for that date/time.

- Alternatively, you can click on the job (blue box) to set up the date, time, and recurrence options. - If you have any trouble finding the job, simply click the wrench icon in the top left corner of the calendar to jump to the job.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1328432158/e73c71cae963fa3975c64d2feef9/ezgif-1-aaeeda441d.gif?expires=1763568000&signature=16c40b2481cd72f49ed079e5b1442a4f341f8cfe124011fb21a0ce6defa65ed4&req=dSMlHs19n4BaUfMW1HO4zVRCqZjgXYS3dTxrPGbaWzrocGZAvCR7tK5DNhOt%0AIQzuzURG4Tv4At6JVjM%3D%0A

- Check the 'Notify Customer' option in the top right corner if you want your customer to receive an email and text notification about the scheduled job. - Leave unchecked if you'd like to prevent this notification from being sent.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1328433986/3ff05f785c6ce26595dbf26be43b/NotifyCustomer.png?expires=1763568000&signature=ff860c8df1166fa78482f983504b74fadf8a2f0f2a261c249b46da4a8723dbf0&req=dSMlHs19nohXX%2FMW1HO4zdGbclKpR3gXcnIjeZz8q6VUbumjSXiqjIgA7dgD%0Akzl04yh8oAjeiBpRJzQ%3D%0A

- Click 'SAVE' in the top right corner of the page to confirm your scheduling.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1328434904/abc9a40fbb5b14eb3f9c0c85581b/Save.png?expires=1763568000&signature=cbdd444404ebe8c53c13501c3871036512bf7e5cb381f6cfefeaa0b9621eae46&req=dSMlHs19mYhfXfMW1HO4ze%2BRiz3XZHgneyjmu5zwc%2FYeux3IsMgJ%2FI%2BzDCJI%0A8YfdM1dor5PN%2FZ82e9w%3D%0A

- Box 3 - Line Items: Add any services or materials to the job, which will be listed as line items on the invoice.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/276105142/a1e823edfa1d4fe9a3eca1ba/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=af5b0841b0cdc7b1e9d8fd8bd6b3b382cd14ecfebb30ef5a204f9035d0199b59&req=dichF8l7nIVdFb4f3HP0gIhz%2FozHALUxodnCgTzbOBnTtRn826uvHl19lhRr%0ANKd%2FAtnuiaZ9p6qseg%3D%3D%0A

- If you need to add more line items, click either '+ SERVICE ITEM' or '+ MATERIAL ITEM,' depending on the type of item you wish to include.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/276106287/12f96e997de90fdeb1315500/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=3a2c16abfe4cf1daf071d4183e4775828cbcbad6f2d6a48641b64ec11970a046&req=dichF8l4n4lYFb4f3HP0gFsRfio8skh9JI4LGCrcbNUCQJFUI08j3zMj0cc2%0AGolEDOfp7vSVe4wD4w%3D%3D%0A

- Optionally, you can include: - Private Notes
- Customer Tags
- Job Tags
- Lead Source
- Attachments

Table:
Row 1: 🛠️ Pro Tip: These are only visible to you and assigned employees, not the customer.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/276107120/4a26ba3583e1ebab82e7985a/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=6a3f54939f1cb0d4ee3d1a8461adfdc02ab73d7b231fb1937efe4479994c9b0a&req=dichF8l5nINfFb4f3HP0gIk7HWx9a1I0gLFHwpzFIUEXfk%2F3AMPh3i1sDebo%0A%2BnaaSjqauzdqd2962g%3D%3D%0A

- Click 'SAVE JOB' in the top right corner to finalize. - If you selected notification options earlier, your customer will receive their text/email notification at this time.

- You will then be taken to the Job Details page.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/276107355/622a69902ee96645e87134fa/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=9af0a14620a6103151b55873726fea36b8863ebfabd7c3064fd7536a6970d839&req=dichF8l5noRaFb4f3HP0gCrLDQ42%2FfFDyp2Aumnn2F9OqgPMWVtZkNgw3r%2Fs%0Ar2PHcrVBnlarzESrXw%3D%3D%0A

Step 2: Running the Job through Housecall Pro

- Access our Field Tech training checklist & videos by clicking here.

- Click On My Way, Start, & Finish for each stage of the job. - We will automatically capture the date and time when technicians click these buttons from their mobile app.

Step 3: Sending an Invoice and Taking Payment

- When you are ready to send the invoice to the customer, open the job to navigate to the Job Details page.

- Click the blue 'SEND INVOICE' button or select the Invoice icon (paper airplane) in the Action Bar.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1229250906/76a231a303e5ec4dcb9233077e8f/Screenshot+2024-10-25+at+2_36_39%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=2494956633fd17d45def9c072c1822c240d4f4ae7fc0478787b8eaa27b3a2a69&req=dSIlH8t7nYhfX%2FMW1HO4zW2fQJNwdhlwLeznQ5HUoyjLsVcoiBmjZaAYAgDS%0AATlupnryeVceTLczfRM%3D%0A

- Adjust the invoice details, settings, payment options, and any necessary attachments (photos, videos, or checklists) by clicking the pencil icon in each section. - You can adjust payment settings to allow customers to pay online directly from the invoice they receive using a credit or debit card or ACH.

- Click 'NEXT' in the upper-right-hand corner to proceed.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1178313885/4694da7158eb6bbd03bfc3b7/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=245f1023f6a4f6808e27269180c4955f9dea4cda2714c8b5958de58795a059ab&req=dSEgHsp%2FnolXXPMW1HO4ze9zsxLFCwmG%2BSdL3o4%2FoWVirzFNGzVYmG0E7RIs%0ASSTr%2FxpgTR4gmGgdg5A%3D%0A

- Confirm or update the email address for receiving the invoice, or choose to send the invoice via text message instead.

- When you're ready, click 'SEND.'

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1178314743/a2a3fc774ffcc483b336ca9f/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=d1fa7271a52cfe25b5020aa9f2f2b8a7a5b63910e40b869be8470012592ab306&req=dSEgHsp%2FmYZbWvMW1HO4zZ%2FhinRQjzwGQ4J9fH311vCcqXlw%2FvQaByPh8BNi%0AZ%2Bka9KUI%2F%2BdqpJCMq5g%3D%0A

- To record a payment from your customer, click the 'PAY' icon on the Job Details page.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/276138021/df33de5e282794fa619d89a9/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=d019b98f5faad14456220ddcc1aadc57299a82ef5d7a0378acc2fd2f8a45a43a&req=dichF8p2nYNeFb4f3HP0gHX0pE5dBc4vsBEfq1aaZzWNn0L47jE%2FCuboTb0A%0AMrJ9v18zertVP01C9w%3D%3D%0A

- Enter the amount collected and the payment method. - Depending on the payment method, you may be asked for additional information, such as credit card details or a check number as a payment note.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/276138554/b7ba6b547b1e0654fe3bacaa/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=ad17f2a9ab2c95b4c37d27221bd2270ef5de4de0f812aa3f69ab5db2adf318ee&req=dichF8p2mIRbFb4f3HP0gCSD7Lk1lxyrXXpdBP0%2F1GkaG6qSBtIXAt6WBSnQ%0Aeae3971TfLgRVPn6eg%3D%3D%0A
