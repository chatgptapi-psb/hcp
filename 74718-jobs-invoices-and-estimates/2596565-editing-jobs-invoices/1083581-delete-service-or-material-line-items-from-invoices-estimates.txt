Delete Service or Material Line Items from Invoices & Estimates
Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/1083581-delete-service-or-material-line-items-from-invoices-estimates
Author: Francisco C
Last updated: Updated over a month ago

Summary: Delete service or material items that no longer need to be included in an invoice.

Removing a Line Item

If you need to remove a line item from either the Services and/or Materials section of an Estimate or Job:​

- Open a job in your HCP account on web.

- In the Services or Materials section, click the X next to the item you need to remove.
