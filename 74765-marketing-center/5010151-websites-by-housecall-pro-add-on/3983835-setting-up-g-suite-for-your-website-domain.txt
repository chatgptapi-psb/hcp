Getting started

If you haven't already, you will first need to sign up for a G Suite account. You can do that here (note that G Suite accounts have a cost associated with them. We recommend starting out on their Basic plan). If you have any issues setting up a G Suite account we recommend reaching out to Googles support teams for further assistance as they will have the best insight and access to the product.

Once you have your G Suite account set up

Once you have your G Suite account set up and are ready to connect it to your domain, you can follow these steps below:

- From the Google Admin Console, click on the Click here to get started link on the banner near the top of the page

- Choose 'Activate' next to 'Activate Gmail for yourdomain' tile.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/205550356/112d9f24136c189509e572b9/Screen+Shot+2020-04-30+at+5.58.24+PM+copy.png?expires=1763568000&signature=9c34c099cab4cf575eeb5b7c01d9036b44181b6a74ff610f49fe9aada743ec7a&req=diAiE8x%2BnoRZFb4f3HP0gFZum1cFEc66zU8vjljKpkUu9JL1I8CTNouktJRh%0AbxVlJPJ9sndSXxN4Yw%3D%3D%0A

- Click on 'Continue' in the lower left corner of your screen.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/205550511/527902302435d4e91ffbe993/Screen+Shot+2020-04-30+at+5.58.31+PM+copy.png?expires=1763568000&signature=7b53ff4effa8913706ce5eeb2cda6f14bc2653dcfb678780bd14a24908fc0070&req=diAiE8x%2BmIBeFb4f3HP0gGAM1ioFsd7YkKoHK8D1JcBHc9x1vsOm9ZjzwHW6%0ANnDCEJiVRrYfSOLRDw%3D%3D%0A

- Scroll to the bottom of the next page and find the verification code associated with step 5 (you do not have to complete steps 1-4 on your own. We will take care of those for you on our end once you provide us the code from step 5!).

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/205809502/a4b87b1d748138023c7fb01c/Screen+Shot+2020-04-30+at+5.59.10+PM+copy.png?expires=1763568000&signature=825cef82a7fbdb1e1d8800610fd1335bbe5d4c64a7693ce4d63695ad42728d89&req=diAiHsl3mIFdFb4f3HP0gOJYLOm1dYQTwYNvAdh%2Fhcb%2FNhGZcs7aBuRvEsYr%0ACj%2B8gotxVzaBpTxcxg%3D%3D%0A

- Once you have that code, chat into the Blue Chat Bubble from your Housecall Pro account. Copy and paste the code into a chat and ask to get your Website domain hooked up for G Suite.
- After we are able to add the verification and other MX records to your domain, you'll be all set to come back to G Suite and choose 'Activate Gmail'.
