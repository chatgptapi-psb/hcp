Referral and Loyalty Campaigns Overview

Boost your customer base and retention with targeted referral and loyalty campaigns. Start by setting SMART goals for your referral campaign, such as acquiring new customers or increasing service calls. Identify and tag your target customers, then design eye-catching postcards using our free Canva templates. Launch your referral campaign through Housecall Pro and track its success to optimize future efforts. For loyalty, choose your top customers, tag them, and run a one-time postcard campaign to show appreciation.

How to Set Up a Customer Referral Campaign

92% of consumers trust suggestions from friends and family more than any other type of advertising. Leverage this trust and the trust you have built with your customers to acquire new customers by utilizing postcards to create a customer referral campaign.

[Video - wistia] https://fast.wistia.net/embed/iframe/rd414fgulb

Step 1: Set Goals

Before you create any marketing campaign you need to set a goal for the campaign. What is the result you are looking to drive? Remember these goals should be SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Realistic, and Timely)A few ideas to get you started:

- Specific number of new customers over a specific timeframe
- Specific number of service calls scheduled for new customers over a specific timeframe
- Specific number of jobs scheduled by existing customers that receive the postcard over a specific timeframe

Step 2: Determine What Customers You Are Targeting

You may not need or want to send this campaign to every customer in your database, so determine which customers you want to send the referral campaign to and tag these customers with a customer tag that is specific to this campaign. For more details on creating and using tags for customers, check out this help article.

PRO TIP: tag customers with the name of your campaign to keep things streamlined

Step 3: Create Your Postcard Design

You can use our postcard template as a starting place to design a custom postcard design that meets the necessary size requirements so that your design prints properly.

Step 4: Create Your Postcard Campaign and Go Live

Create your Customer Referral Campaign within Housecall Pro and order those postcards! For guidance on setting up postcard campaigns, visit this help article.

Remember, creating your referral campaign is only the first step. You need to track this campaign strategically so that you can determine success and gain insights on how to adjust your campaign to drive the results you are looking for so utilize custom reporting to track your goals and the effectiveness of this campaign.

How to Set Up a Customer Loyalty Campaign

How do you turn customers into lifelong loyal customers who will see you as their go-to brand for themselves and refer you to family and friends? Appreciation is one way to build this loyalty. In this video, we are going to share a strategy for utilizing your Reporting tab, customer tags, and postcards to drive a customer loyalty campaign that you can run biannually or annually.

[Video - wistia] https://fast.wistia.net/embed/iframe/73ul3e9n6a

Step 1: Decide your Cadence

In order to drive a successful customer loyalty campaign you need to decide how often you will run a campaign each year. We recommend once or twice a year.

Step 2: Determine Your Top 20 Customers

Access the Reporting tab by clicking on 'Reporting' in the main navigation bar at the top of your Housecall Pro account.

Step 3: Tag Customers as Top 20

Navigate to your customer list by clicking the 'Customers' tab in the top navigation bar of your Housecall Pro account. Select the customers you want to tag by checking the boxes next to each name listed in your exported report. Then, click the 'Actions' dropdown in the upper-right-hand corner and choose 'Add Tag.' In the pop-up window, enter 'Top 20' as the tag name and click 'Add' in the bottom right corner to apply the tag.
