Enabling Service Area for Online Booking

Tired of getting bookings in areas that you don't serve? Our new Online Booking Tool allows you to define your Service Area zip codes.

By enabling Service Area, the booking flow will ask your customers to provide their zip codes to ensure they're in an area you service!

Set your Service Area

To set your service area, follow the steps below:

- Click the gear icon in the top right corner of your HCP account to go to Settings.

- Select Company from the Settings menu on the left.

- Click the Service area tab in the Company settings page.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1645700690/a5370d79b6e905457f142c4cfb44/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=06844ad07f22e2ff8e1d08babb40638eff3d4314e1a702dbf5e73fef3c067d05&req=dSYjE85%2BnYdWWfMW1HO4zWn%2BAej45SGkqR16XSw6TCYax95%2FSDSnSCHc3p8d%0AXRNwjmarp56Df%2B0abtE%3D%0A

- Under Service Area, add one or more zip codes that define the area where you operate.

- After typing the zip code you wish to use, press Enter on the keyboard to save.

---

Enable Your Service Area for Online Booking

To enable your service area for Online Booking, follow the steps below:

- Click the gear icon in the top right corner of your HCP account to go to Settings.

- Select Booking from the Settings menu on the left.

- Click the General tab in the Booking settings page.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1644112026/00040dbfad48ef6d5c65e989e352/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=6d23f8dcb230e0d5149ea5fe58269aa6ca2f4c5125ead7ebfbd9f2f07a919819&req=dSYjEsh%2Fn4FdX%2FMW1HO4zbpIgiD5y3QINSOZ3B09TlB1%2FhWHFw99TtDXcfTz%0A%2Bk5N9J5HdK4C4GvJmHU%3D%0A

- Under Online Booking's Service Area, you'll find a toggle button that states "Only allow customers within your Service Area to book a service."

- Click the toggle next to Only allow customers within your Service Area to book a service. The toggle will turn blue when the setting is enabled.

Service Area in the Customer's Booking Flow

Once the Service Area is configured and enabled for online booking, an additional step is added to the booking flow, where the customer is asked to provide their zip code.

- If the Zip code matches your Service Area, the Customer will be allowed to book.

- If the Zip code does not match your Service Area, the customer will see a message saying "We're sorry we currently don't operate in [zip code], if you have any questions, please call: [phone number on your company profile]."
