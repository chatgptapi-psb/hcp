Why Accept Payments Upfront?

- Secure your bookings - Minimize no-shows and last-minute cancellations.

- Get paid faster - Eliminate unpaid invoices and improve cash flow.

- Simplify your payment process - Say goodbye to preparing and chasing down invoices.

- Impress your customers - Deliver a seamless, professional booking experience.

---

How to Enable Payments for Online Booking

To enable payments in your online booking flow, follow these steps:

- Click the gear icon in the top right corner of your HCP account to go to Settings.

- Select Booking from the Settings menu on the left.

- Click the Online Booking tab in the Booking settings page.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1644138502/01f610f40c1c1bf0c0e87565328a/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=ba6504edaffcddbfd99ee0d6cf303bdfda288bc103231b1192f397ab58559492&req=dSYjEsh9lYRfW%2FMW1HO4zY4avTQZMoI3t1UrrzBFRfVkMJJV0sXkECEv%2FtWk%0AqkXtZd8xRwpA%2FVi3oiM%3D%0A

Once your bank account is connected, you will see the options to Require full payments and Require deposits.

- If you toggle on Require full payments, then your customers will be asked to pay the total amount of the booking upfront at the time of booking.

- If you toggle on Require deposits, you will set the fixed amount (ex: $20 out of the $100 total cost) or percentage deposits (ex, 50% of the total cost) that you want your customers to pay upfront at the time of booking.

Edit Your Online Booking Payment Options

To edit your payment options for Online Booking:

- Navigate to your online booking Payment options settings.

- Click Edit in the bottom right corner of the Payments & deposits section.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1644145485/f41d5b2990e496f72473188d2acd/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=f74847880b212bf4cd7dc03b8e2cbe03d0a4c7c3535e274329d5c89dd764d146&req=dSYjEsh6mIVXXPMW1HO4zdjX7g0UicBIbdA8CksWt4dFNAg5NsrrQQmwYjgA%0ANtGS%2BFtXchACEM2gMWk%3D%0A

- Select your payment option: - Require full payments
- Require deposits
- No payment required

- Click Save in the bottom right corner of the window to confirm.

Customer View

- Once you update your settings, you can preview your booking flow here.

- Customers will see the payment step to input a dollar amount or percentage (depending on your configuration) after selecting their service and date.

- If the payment is successful, the customer will receive an email with a receipt.
