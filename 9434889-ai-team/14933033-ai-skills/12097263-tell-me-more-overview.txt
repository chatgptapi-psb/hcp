What is Tell me more?

“Tell me more” is an AI Skill that quickly summarizes information using context from the page that it is on. Just tap the “Tell me more” button, and an AI Team Member will generate a clear, concise summary located on the right-hand side chat.

Right now, “Tell me more” is powered by CSR AI, providing instant customer context on the profile page. But this is just the beginning. Soon, the entire AI Team will expand its capabilities—bringing rich, real-time summaries to more pages across Housecall Pro, so you can get the context you need wherever you are.
