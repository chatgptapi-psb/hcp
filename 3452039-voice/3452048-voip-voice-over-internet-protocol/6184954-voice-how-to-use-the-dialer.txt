Voice Dialer Overview

VoIP allows you to make and receive calls directly from HCP on the web. Our VoIP solution can be used in conjunction with call tracking numbers.

Configure Your Status

When you are in the dialer, you can set your status as:

- Unavailable: This means the agent is not available to take calls, so they will NOT receive an incoming call, even if they are a part of a call flow. If the agent does not have their dialer open, then they will be considered unavailable.

- Available: This means you are available to take a call. If your agent is available AND they are part of a call flow through a ring group or directly, then they will receive incoming calls.

- On a call: This will occur automatically if you are on a call. We will automatically set you back to your status once the call is complete. While on a call, you will not be able to update the status.

To set your status:

- Click on the arrow directly to the right of your profile picture or initials.

- If you are available, the option will be to "Set to unavailable."

- If you are unavailable, you will be able to "Set to available."

Receiving an Inbound Call

- When you receive an incoming VoIP call, you will receive a desktop notification. Your dialer will also start to ring and show the incoming call.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/584358438/d80f0a0b26b79ec2ae8f4d91/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=5b95aab777dca2f093ba5b6fdcfcf32b0613084a91bc7c1710d3738847af66a6&req=cSgjFcx2mYJXFb4f3HP0gHNfoVPTdwd4RpsBBaeli0yCxw4ElLEz5J7O7EOp%0AoKgpMcjkVw3Yt5C11A%3D%3D%0A

- If it is an existing customer, you will see the customer name, number, and the lead source associated with the call tracking number they called into.

- If it is an unknown customer, you will see the phone number and lead source only.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/584356371/2a4bbd4f395d7599cfcabbd6/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=d7688f393e1c7a40aff9400d85574d4c79046211f7e8ada4b1aa3516f48f65ac&req=cSgjFcx4noZeFb4f3HP0gMZ7eQvDvCMeL%2BwJNZeclGXX2ZgtMV7FXk8Ayr7R%0AOomxe3skDPCL6EHw2Q%3D%3D%0A

- If the customer you are communicating with has the Do Not Service label, you can see it for incoming calls and active calls.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1547133183/3732ff042b7e530a9d5432ba517a/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=2dca0f699b0970383d691130c5c97ac0263443a199210cebf90571c646805afd&req=dSUjEch9noBXWvMW1HO4zWSTV7m%2BRoI5s56aAETQvaoncjIVA0w2JwmKXvUn%0AwYVsUyPdTyiCSVTgzBI%3D%0A

- When you answer the incoming call, it will open up the Customer Intake flow (Request Guide, Reminders, and FAQs) automatically in a new tab.

- After doing this, the customer profile, phone number, and address will have all been populated for you. Simultaneously, you will be connected to the customer.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/584358816/0d446ec2d5f65901e89c5593/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=ad350ccd096608ebdb514c86a902569c373e17efe2d08e0c23a1824499329f01&req=cSgjFcx2lYBZFb4f3HP0gFMZ9C1cM7ncjnhpjBFIVLT4%2BQTIlNHuQ8qaxzmj%0AZHR46vgL2f2bhMOzuw%3D%3D%0A

- You can also add another person to the call if you need. This feature applies to outbound calls, too.

- When you click Add call, you will be able to see a list of your employees and their phone numbers on the employee profile. - Note: You will only be able to add external numbers to your live phone call.

Making an Outbound Call

To make an outbound call, you have a few options. You can either call from the Call details on the global call log or the customer call log. You can also call directly from the dialer.

When calling from the Global call log:

- First, click on the customer's name, then click on the phone icon to the right of the customer's name.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/506525624/8d6bd29785cb27e9c37992f1/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=9716f23e41b71a4b48d261b51a3b00028550a4bbb80a1cb8d4e6b26d75f23e39&req=cSAhE8t7m4NbFb4f3HP0gGiyNWAD0fj1TMS1rXMCZymE1NaNofGpbx7tQHUb%0AqtHk0xRsGoTJthKgcA%3D%3D%0A

When calling from the Customer call log:

- Click on the blue Call button in the top right corner of the page.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/506525475/f64f83a57ceb7cdb10386568/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=6afd6f09b4f6e3728a23b96ccf2be9d6c07cdbc2df34d4f529db27459ac680c5&req=cSAhE8t7mYZaFb4f3HP0gGhD12YgqS4VWpU%2FMWcU2924425N%2FlmsZYVl0XQ0%0AJ9xXa9Olb13sJTE%2BGg%3D%3D%0A

When calling from the Dialer:

- Enter the customer's phone number and click the blue phone icon to initiate the call.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/584379778/d1375ce479e0f19686b0dd1f/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=3cff3595beeeb76cd136ba2e89097bed8db7a96f9905591db93f3094fca384b5&req=cSgjFc53moZXFb4f3HP0gKCkD7qh%2FxW5Gvaqs5F0KlvzyIcm%2Bg%2Br9N5Wa5S8%0ABoFrdzQlymg3w6tu4Q%3D%3D%0A

- Once you click the call button, the Customer Intake flow (Request Guide, Reminders, and FAQs) will open automatically in a new tab with the outbound call initiated on your dialer.

- Just like with an inbound call, you will be able to add notes, edit the job, etc., and then that data will appear on the call log.

Call Queue

Our call queueing feature allows you to:

- Handle multiple calls at the same time
- Understand what your agents are doing at a given time
- Place calls on hold

When you open up the dialer, you will see your call queue now instead of the keypad.

- If you need access to your keypad, you can select the blue floating action button to use the keypad.

- All active and queued calls will show up here.

Accessing the Call Log

You will have access to a call log within the dialer. This call log will provide the most real time data. It should match the call log within HCP web but there may be a slight delay with what you see in HCP web.

To access the dialer call log:

- Click on the Call log button to the left of the Keypad icon.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/507039034/c530d6c8e9dcae084ad5c617/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=c7e81fed5fd3ddcca5f66a005badcc560f7606507c15cc210858d4c75e272c78&req=cSAgFsp3nYJbFb4f3HP0gIEZgfj0bMh3cHd71y4A0C4VIqPGK1k5Y7Lhhl2K%0AEGs9FPz3LOLe7m%2B4Ig%3D%3D%0A

- In the call log, you can see All calls or Missed calls.

- You will also see notification badging for any missed calls. In order to clear the notification badge, you will need to click on that call row.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/507040163/ca2411413a1433d6107dd46c/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=d927850ae0b75d1cf4c18b67a1d08d7f22c8e0b40133771558330a501f95adac&req=cSAgFs1%2BnIdcFb4f3HP0gLLrSGyHIZdGhK%2FaN45mMtfJp3HUJrB8cbuFjn4q%0A3M7h0uSl%2Bo4wjl54%2BQ%3D%3D%0A

- Clicking on a call will open up the Call details, where you can see what type of call it was, the number, name, lead source, and call recording.

- You can also make an outbound call through the Call details.
