Make Referrals Easy: Share Housecall Pro with Your Network

If you know of companies that aren’t using Housecall Pro but could benefit from it or have shown interest in the platform, referring them is simple. Use your referral link, available on both the website and the mobile app, to easily share Housecall Pro with your network.

Housecall Pro’s referral program allows you to earn rewards when a company you refer becomes a customer. For updated details on our referral program and to view the offerings, visit Housecall Pro's referral page.

---

How to Refer Companies to Housecall Pro (Web)

To refer companies using the HCP web portal:

- Log in to your Housecall Pro account and click on your profile picture or initials in the top right corner.

- From the drop-down menu, select Refer a Friend.

Alternatively, you can access the referral section through your account settings:

- Click the gear icon in the top right corner of your HCP account to go to Settings.

- Select Refer a Friend from the left menu, under Global Settings

- You’ll see two tabs at the top of the page: - Referral Link - Referrals

In the Refer Link settings, you’ll find several options for sharing your referral link:

- Copy and Paste URL: Copy the URL to share directly with interested companies.

- Google Contacts: Share the link with contacts from your Google account.

- Facebook: Post the link to your Facebook page.

- Email: Enter the email address of the interested company to send them a referral link directly

- Referrals: Send referral links to contacts recommended from within your Housecall Pro account.

---

How to Refer Companies to Housecall Pro (Mobile)

To refer companies using the Housecall Pro mobile app:

- Go to More in the bottom navigation bar.

- Select About > Share the love.

- On the next screen, tap Share link to access various options for sharing your referral link.

- You can copy the link to share it directly or use the available options to send it via email or social media. This makes it easy to refer companies that could benefit from using Housecall Pro. Thank you for spreading the word! 😁
