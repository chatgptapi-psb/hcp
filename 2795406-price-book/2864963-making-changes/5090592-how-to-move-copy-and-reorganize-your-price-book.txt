How to Move, Copy, and Reorganize Your Price Book
Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/5090592-how-to-move-copy-and-reorganize-your-price-book
Author: Layla K
Last updated: Updated over a month ago

Summary: Streamline your Price Book: learn to move, reorder, and copy items efficiently for better organization and smoother operations.

Benefits of Efficient Price Book Organization

Keeping your Price Book organized is key to smooth operations and accurate pricing. Whether you need to move items to new categories, reorder services, or copy them across industries, knowing how to manage these tasks can make your work easier and more efficient. This article will show you:

- How to Reorganize: Quickly move and reorder items to keep everything in the right place.

- How to Copy Items: Easily duplicate services and materials for different categories and/or industries.

- How to Save Time: Use practical tips to streamline your Price Book and focus on other important tasks.

Learn these skills to make your Price Book management simpler and more effective.

Moving Services and Materials in Your Price Book

If you need a simple and efficient way of reorganizing your price book, you can use the bulk move tool.
​
​This allows you to do the following actions:

- Move up to 100 services and/or materials to an existing location in your price book
- Move up to 100 services to the 'Materials' section of your price book
