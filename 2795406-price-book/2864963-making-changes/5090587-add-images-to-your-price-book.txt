How to Add Images to Services & Materials

To add images to services and materials in your Price Book:

- Navigate to your Price Book and select Services or Materials from the menu on the left.

- Hover over the image and click "Replace image."

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1479997915/e95f71d15de047f80052663c3a7b/ReplaceImage.gif?expires=1763568000&signature=a069613fd474ffbe41870a6b9e6bc566647086fa3f9c9b7259ccdba30264bd8c&req=dSQgH8B3moheXPMW1HO4zQCs48ShNZD%2FBF2CshniwKrp5bYqI0Zjz5x%2FXM43%0A8l3ZqgTMmEpzDEYEHm8%3D%0A

- For service items with no image, click the "Add image" button under the image placeholder at the top of the page.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1482777975/a0377e0f8a2cbef71f9e3b54c93d/Screenshot+2025-04-18+at+11_03_38%E2%80%AFAM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=2cfeed8cec54f344ee212ebf1ef07a877400f693033f04c987698e4b2092ac12&req=dSQvFM55mohYXPMW1HO4zfng%2BzdDaMpM0%2BCbKDh%2F4VFg0R5IUukFyujDFoeC%0A58aXdr8%2BPFX5bFIlVR0%3D%0A

- If you'd like, you can select a stock image from your Image Library and click "Use selected image" in the bottom right corner of the window.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1482788935/299f58786329380834079e40f984/Screenshot+2025-04-18+at+11_08_00%E2%80%AFAM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=11f6c1e353d2f4f0d69d8b845213d71c954e32943a7ae4a2363ab8663330b1d2&req=dSQvFM52lYhcXPMW1HO4zVZ2rlMNQOc1IigPpkIG1hb0lLMLe9QRfv4rtyOB%0AUJq3DuDg53SYShUrK2g%3D%0A

- Alternatively, click "Upload image" in the top right corner of the Image Library window and select an image from your device.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1482788752/91f96042ef298ce3eef46a936e4c/Screenshot+2025-04-18+at+11_10_55%E2%80%AFAM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=b423ac00e7448aae80ead4f2920cce0207137b5bd903ae0c3c7efd76e2f24ab0&req=dSQvFM52lYZaW%2FMW1HO4zc8oh4RuNopNaWd3UreeGshrRi9lGezVTcE2e%2Fgo%0AoBTRHuQ9ncnSMpOasuQ%3D%0A

When uploading an image:

- Select an image file from your device and click Open.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1482855633/3075cd41fd5fa4047752ed1cc1cb/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=cb04a6de1886e088a7a3e4d5067c4a3f72519eac57cdf16cf354bced264b49a6&req=dSQvFMF7mIdcWvMW1HO4zWakfLz2niQvH1%2Ffa5C5SxVScr2%2Bq%2FP2vwB3cXIk%0At4TnJCaoIv%2Fc82098gs%3D%0A

- You will then have the option to crop the image.

- Adjust the image by resizing the blue box and/or using the + or - magnify icons to zoom in or out until the image looks the way you want it for that category.

- To adjust the image orientation, use the left/right rotate icons as needed until the image is positioned correctly.

- Click Save to apply your changes.
