Getting Started with Your Materials Price Book

Effectively managing your Price Book is crucial for maintaining a well-organized and efficient business. By keeping your materials and categories up-to-date and accurately priced, you streamline the process of generating invoices and estimates. This not only helps reduce errors and save time but also ensures you provide clear and precise quotes to your customers. A well-maintained Price Book supports smoother daily operations and enhances your ability to adapt quickly to changes in your materials or pricing.

Setting up materials is a critical first step for implementing flat-rate pricing. However, even if you're not using flat rate pricing, having your materials well-organized is beneficial for easily adding them to jobs, invoices, and estimates.

This article will guide you through manually adding and managing materials and categories. For instructions on importing data, please refer to our articles on importing materials using a file or CSV.

Add a Material Sub-Category

Sub-categories help you organize materials more effectively by allowing for better management and clearer differentiation. For example, you might create sub-categories such as "Electrical Supplies" and "Plumbing Parts" to classify materials based on their use or type, making it easier to manage and track different materials within your Price Book.

Keep in mind that once you add a material to a category, you will no longer be able to add sub-categories to that category. To maintain better organization and structure, consider adding subcategories before you add any materials.

Add a Material to Your Price Book

Adding materials to your Price Book is essential for accurate quotes and streamlined operations. Keeping your materials updated helps ensure your pricing is current, reduces mistakes, and facilitates efficient management. Materials are particularly crucial for flat rate pricing, as they provide the detailed cost information needed to set consistent and transparent rates.

By regularly updating your materials, you maintain accurate cost information, improve quote precision, and enable quicker responses to changes. Learn how to add and update materials to enhance your business operations and support effective flat-rate pricing.

To add materials to your Price Book:

- Go to the Price book tab in the top navigation bar of your Housecall Pro account. - If you have a lot of features in the top navigation bar, your Price Book may be nested under the More drop-down.

- Once you have navigated to your price book, ensure that Materials is selected from the left-hand menu, located directly beneath the Price Book heading.

- If you have multiple categories, find and click on the category where you want to add materials.
