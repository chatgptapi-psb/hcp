Flat Rate Pricing: Recorded Webinar Series

Every Wednesday at 11am PST / 2pm EST, tune in to our Housecall Pros Facebook Community for live sessions where the HCP team tackles essential business topics. Our powerful community of home service Pros, united by a common purpose, is always there to lend a hand and share their expertise.

We were excited to collaborate with Profit Rhino for an exceptional 3-part webinar series featuring industry expert Jeremy Lowe. Discover his valuable insights on Flat Rate Pricing and how they can elevate your business.

Profit Rhino Webinar Part 1: Pricing for Profit

In Part 1 of our Profit Rhino webinar series, "Pricing for Profit," dive into effective pricing strategies that boost your bottom line. Learn how to set prices that not only cover your costs but also maximize profitability. This session provides actionable insights and practical tips to refine your pricing approach and ensure your business thrives. Don't miss out on these essential strategies for achieving financial success.

Profit Rhino Webinar Part 2: Should I be on Flat Rate?

In Part 2 of our Profit Rhino webinar series, "Should I Be on Flat Rate?" explore the advantages and considerations of adopting a flat rate pricing model. This session delves into whether flat rate pricing is the right fit for your business, how it compares to other pricing methods, and how it can impact your profitability and customer satisfaction. Gain valuable insights to make an informed decision about transitioning to flat-rate pricing

Profit Rhino Webinar Part 3: Are you charging what you're worth?

In Part 3 of our Profit Rhino webinar series, "Are You Charging What You're Worth?" discover how to evaluate and adjust your pricing to reflect the true value of your services. This session focuses on identifying pricing gaps, assessing your market value, and implementing strategies to ensure you’re compensated fairly. Learn how to align your rates with your expertise and boost your business’s profitability.
