Checklist Improvements Overview

You may not be at every job with your employees, but you can ensure every job goes as well as it would if you were there by leveraging custom checklists.

Be sure to take advantage of the newest improvements to checklists, including:

- Adding notes to each checklist item

- Marking certain items required rather than the entire checklist

- Adding sections

- Downloading/printing to PDF

- Uploading photos (Advanced functionality)

- Using single and multi-select fields (Advanced functionality)

---

Creating and Editing Checklist Templates

To create and update checklist templates on the HCP web portal, follow these steps.

- Navigate to Settings > Checklists.

- Depending on your industry, you may already have some default templates in your account that you can customize further by clicking the pencil icon.

- Otherwise, click + Checklist to create a new checklist.

- You can also duplicate an existing checklist by clicking the More icon to the right of the checklist and selecting Duplicate from the drop-down.

- Add a name for the checklist (this is the name you and your employees will see when adding and viewing checklists to jobs and estimates).

- Add sections to organize different checklist items.

- Choose from the following checklist item types: - Checkbox
- Text field
- Pass/flag/fail
- Photo upload (Advanced functionality)
- Single-select (Advanced functionality)
- Multi-select (Advanced functionality)

- Mark any checklist items as required if you'd like them to be completed before the estimate or job can be finished.

- You can enable the setting to auto-add checklists to jobs and estimates, which will automatically add the checklist to any new job or estimate you create.

- You can also create job templates to which checklists can be added to facilitate auto-adding checklists to different types of jobs.

To delete a checklist:

- Click the More icon to the right of the checklist and select Delete from the drop-down.

- This will only prevent the checklist from being used moving forward, it will not delete it from any jobs or estimates it's been previously applied to.

Using and Completing Checklists on Jobs and Estimates

Checklists can be added to jobs and estimates, as well as removed from the web or mobile apps within the checklist card. If they're set up to be auto-added, they'll show up within the checklist section of the job after the job is created and saved.

- Once added, the checklist can be viewed and updated by Admins, Office Staff, and Field Techs assigned to the job or estimate.

- Within each section, users can check items off, add text, select field values, and upload photos. Be sure to save your changes before closing the checklist.

- Checklists with required items (as indicated by a red asterisk *) must be completed before the job or estimate can be marked as finished.

- Comments can be added to any checklist item for additional context.

- Photos uploaded to a checklist will be stored and viewed on the checklist; they will not appear in the attachments section on jobs or estimates.
