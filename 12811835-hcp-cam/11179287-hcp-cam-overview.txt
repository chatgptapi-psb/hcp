What is HCP Cam?

HCP Cam allows you to document more on every job. With Photo Annotations, Video Uploads, and Checklists Advanced, you can gain complete control over what's going on in the field and enhance your quality of work while proactively protecting your business. Use checklists for clear client communication and guided workflows, and stay connected to the field with visual updates via photo annotations and video uploads.

Photo Annotations

Turn every photo into a clear visual record with photo annotations. Add notes, shapes, or timestamps to explain what’s done. After taking a photo through the app, you can add drawings, shapes, text, and time stamps before uploading the image to a job or an estimate.

Video Uploads

Record and upload videos without leaving your app. You can control exactly what you share by trimming clips directly in the app and sending them to customers or your team. Capturing and uploading videos directly from your mobile device is a powerful way to enhance documentation and communication.
