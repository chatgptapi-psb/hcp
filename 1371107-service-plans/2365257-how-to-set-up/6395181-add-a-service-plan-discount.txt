Service Plan Discounts

Service Plan discounts allow you to add a discount to the service plan templates you offer and then automatically apply the discount to all future jobs (including segments) and estimates for those customers.

---

Add a Discount to a New Service Plan Template

To add a Discount when creating a new Service Plan Template:

- Click the My Apps icon in your navigation bar and select Service Plans.
- On your Service Plans dashboard, in the top right-hand corner, click on + SERVICE PLAN.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/552002462/aeca9349dbfecccac84656ff/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=f68ee1c359a90c9ce4a354d921062987836314b10ecddf6ba44b268c494d830c&req=cSUlFsl8mYddFb4f3HP0gJDNu9xrNZXYq2R%2BUboDcA%2BNBSAixKI2e5cB4UaK%0AL2DwM2PDD6Uxsqyf1Q%3D%3D%0A

Set up the service plan as outlined in the steps here.

In the discounts section:

- Toggle Apply discount to jobs on.
- Enter a description
- Enter a percentage amount

- If you would like to remove or not include a discount, the Apply discount to jobs toggle must be off.

Add a Discount to an Existing Service Plan Template

To add a discount to an existing Service Plan template:

- First, navigate to the Service Plan Dashboard.

- Next, under the Plan Templates section, select the service plan template you want to update.

- Then, click the three vertical dots in the top right corner of the template and choose Edit Service Plan.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1610057737/28e413373d3d0de2a35be374bade/image.png?expires=1763568000&signature=7359b500f671a0d5d75ce158e5706113adc5b50e7fb1f2f899d488de20a500d1&req=dSYmFsl7moZcXvMW1HO4zYwialMtAQg4rT917%2FJNkNjZoP1%2FDAF4Ylq64Wo%2B%0AIvdnsB6S8%2F0HWPdWZD4%3D%0A

- Under the Discount section, turn on the toggle to Apply Discount to Jobs.

- Once you've added the discount, click Next in the top right corner to continue, then select Save Plan to confirm your changes.
