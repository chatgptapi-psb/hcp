Lead Source Overview

Our Lead Source field allows you to track where your customers found your business without having to use tags.

Lead source tracking enables better reporting so that you can make smart marketing decisions by investing in the sources that bring you good jobs. Leads can be tracked on customers, jobs, and estimates.

Adding Lead Sources

Lead sources can be assigned to customers, jobs, and estimates, allowing you to easily track where each lead originated.

Additionally, you have the flexibility to bulk-add lead sources, streamlining the process and ensuring that your lead data is efficiently categorized across multiple records at once.

---

Add a Lead Source to a Customer Profile

To add a Lead Source to a new Customer Profile:

- When creating a new customer, you will see a field to add a lead source in the bottom right corner of the Notes section.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1326846734/84065ea73776fb5895515ca6ecd4/LeadSource10.png?expires=1763567100&signature=af16033b8bb27ab76a7f58b59273044419b1615aeb322a27ce28d1b5d4cf07e9&req=dSMlEMF6m4ZcXfMW1HO4zX7UZoJ1xFlbmKlm3xukzTLVjU%2BDaLFmzCE%2BeiWk%0AtwT5Gh1RMAO7MjitkkE%3D%0A

To add a lead source to an existing Customer Profile:

- Open a Customer Profile and ensure that you're on the Profile tab.

- Scroll down and click the Lead source option on the left.

- Use the Lead source field to select a lead source from your list.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1328144770/e6a4e64ae29f9685c914184230cc/LeadSourceCustomerProfile.gif?expires=1763567100&signature=39ec4cbb6a6b75403ca80036a0122097500e1dd42a78d5fe62d675e835d9aaf9&req=dSMlHsh6mYZYWfMW1HO4zX3dkPDi6TJCmW5ys8nSzLP0yfqPomVOVMyFFvRH%0Af0vpja8kVvpm%2BsG2fj8%3D%0A

Add a Lead source to an existing Job or Estimate

- Navigate to the Job or Estimate details Page.

- Scroll down to the left-hand side and click the Lead source + button.

- Type the lead source name, then select it from the matching options in your lead source list.

Bulk Add Lead Sources to Customers

On the Customers tab, you can select multiple customers and bulk-add lead sources to those customers:

- Navigate to the Customers tab.

- Select the customers to add a lead source to by using the checkboxes on the left.

- Click the Bulk Actions button in the top right corner of the page.

- Select "Add lead source" from the drop-down.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1328207137/a08c81b71fc5f34c7defaf98cbf0/BulkLeadSource.png?expires=1763567100&signature=6797b230c7514f0ee82a110474c0f28e98a272dec9cd0dcc27d9ba1e1f9b6033&req=dSMlHst%2BmoBcXvMW1HO4zdJfbeOqluMFPZPCl7IZKc3UUxu5mNOkVxWde%2FCo%0AsQJ3w6qYtvNMoCAOd%2B4%3D%0A

- Click into the Lead source field and choose a lead source from your list.

Reporting on Lead Sources

You can report on lead sources using the filter functionality on the reporting tab. After applying filters, you will be able to view summary metrics for any lead source(s) and drill into job details to see the lead source for each job.

---

Jobs & Customers Lead Source Reports

To report on job & customer lead sources:

- First, click Reporting in the top navigation bar of your HCP account.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1326860749/dd385e1dcf2d49dd7906d57e8926/ReportingTab.png?expires=1763567100&signature=012bd3a7c7bed29ef95991277e64d2434125d27dbb0141d2e230bdbde8603841&req=dSMlEMF4nYZbUPMW1HO4zYdFtQSZaIyclHl2fzDCu%2FIEtZmrWxW98YCGhdSa%0Amqeivkg6SdDQR0TDV5A%3D%0A

- Next, select Jobs from the left-hand menu under the All Reports section.

- Then, choose one of the following reports depending on what you want to see: - For customer lead source data, open the Customer lead source Report under the Customer section.
- For job lead source data, open the Job lead source Report under the Type section.

- Once in the report, filter as needed.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1326860930/55add439ba99b92215aa906bd949/LeadSourceReporting.png?expires=1763567100&signature=41a37313bbe3c7e0d33e8ab3ffcd012437881f78485967559fe59001b9d686f3&req=dSMlEMF4nYhcWfMW1HO4zS7T%2BiDK9pilM6zsb%2Fuq2%2FV4C6G9hPS0qRfQrjoY%0AY7HLLHl5JiiVnSTytd8%3D%0A

Export Lead Source Reports

To export lead source reports:

- Click Reporting in the top navigation bar of your HCP account.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1326866855/5488b22412656dd2100e1a3db510/ReportingTab.png?expires=1763567100&signature=b8647d6954d973785620e0f7e9e6ed3b1ffc424ef5183c13d339d75584af31e4&req=dSMlEMF4m4laXPMW1HO4zW8K3q4Ns8k7l%2F22kBQGlkPKkZg6jQPqw8kWM8tK%0ABEUIV9VnrqYX55GV3Kk%3D%0A

- Select Jobs or Estimates from the menu on the left, depending on the report you need to export.

- Select the lead source report you'd like to export and filter as needed.

- Click EXPORT under the chart, on the right side of the page.

- The report will then be downloaded to your computer.
