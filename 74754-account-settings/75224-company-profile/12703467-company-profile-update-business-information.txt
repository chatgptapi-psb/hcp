Update Your Business Info in Housecall Pro

To update your business information in Housecall Pro via the web portal:

- Access Settings by clicking the gear ⚙️ in the top right corner of your screen.

- Select Company from the Settings menu on the left and ensure the Profile tab is selected at the top of the page.

- Click Edit to the right of the Business information section.

- You can then update the following information as needed: - Business name
- Address
- Time zone
- Business phone
- Support email
- Website
- Legal entity name
- Industry
- License number
