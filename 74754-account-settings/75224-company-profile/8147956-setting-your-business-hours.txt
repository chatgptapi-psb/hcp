How to Set Your Business Hours

Set your business hours to be displayed on your Housecall Pro calendar as well as your online booking page (if applicable). If you have GPS tracking for your account, you will be able to track your employees within your set business hours.

To set your business hours:

- Click the gear icon in the top right corner of your HCP account to go to Settings.

- Select Company from the left menu, under Global Settings.

- Click the Business hours tab on the Company settings page.

- Click Edit in the Operating hours section at the top of the page.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1645413057/3643f94dba34049113d577bff92d/image.png?expires=1763567100&signature=0cb05ca1ec5f65a251fb76d64dcc739b02041acda812655a7f6cdfa0d29ea272&req=dSYjE81%2FnoFaXvMW1HO4zctdO3IbS3UBWdccKHGrDTfzOQWkIj2WqGVAqk9O%0A6zDqPotLyfGHSMPBHmc%3D%0A
