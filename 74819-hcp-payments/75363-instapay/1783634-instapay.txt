What is Instapay?

⚡️ Make Every Day Payday with Instapay! ⚡️

We've introduced Instapay so you can get paid even faster than standard payment processing. You’ll be able to charge your customer's card or bank (ACH) and see your money in your account, typically within 30 minutes. All you have to do is enter your debit card and set Instapay as your default payout time. It's that easy!
