Taking Card Payments on the HCP Mobile App

One of the best parts of this technology is that you can take credit cards right through the app without any additional downloads!

To take payments on the HCP mobile app:

- Navigate to the Job Details page and tap the 'Pay' icon at the top of your screen.

- Next, select the payment type by tapping on the form of payment you are receiving:
​ - Credit Card (select only if you use our credit card processor).
- Cash
- Check
- Other (select if you are using any other credit card processor).

- When you select the Credit Card payment option, you can: 
​ - Manually enter the card number.
​
- Tap 'Scan Card' to scan the card with your device's camera. 
 - This will automatically input the card number. 
 - The expiration, zip code, and CVV must be entered manually. 
 - The app will need access to your camera, so make sure to allow it permission. 
​
- Tap 'Card Reader' if you have a Housecall Pro Card Reader and would like to swipe, tap, or chip the card. 
 - If the 'Save card on file' option is enabled, you will not be able to use the Card Reader.
