Summary: Learn how to refund a credit card payment from the Housecall Pro web portal.

Refunding Credit Card Payments

To refund a credit card payment:

- Open a job associated with the payment you need to refund to view the Job Details page.

- Scroll down to the Payment history section.

- Click 'Refund' to the right of the payment information.

- Enter the dollar amount to be refunded in the 'Refund amount' field. You can choose to enter the full payment amount or a partial amount.

- Click 'Issue Refund' to process the refund for your customer.

How do refunds work for ACH payments?

- ACH refunds must be initiated within 90 days after the original payment, and can only be processed after the payment process has been completed. 
​ - For example, if a refund is initiated immediately after a payment is made, there will be a 4-5 business day delay for the charge to process first, then another 4-5 business days for the refund transfer process. ACH refunds cannot be issued after 90 days from the payment date.

---

Can I cancel a payment before it goes through?

- Once a card payment has been made, it cannot be canceled. The payment will need to be refunded to the customer from the Job details page.
