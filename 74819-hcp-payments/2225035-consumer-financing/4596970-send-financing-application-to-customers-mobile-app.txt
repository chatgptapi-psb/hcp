Summary: Learn how to send Wisetack financing applications to customers from the HCP Mobile App.

Overview

With Housecall Pro’s consumer financing option turned on, customers will automatically have the option to finance their jobs when you send estimates and/or invoices.

You now will also have the ability to easily send your customer an application to finance their job with Wisetack directly from your job details page on the mobile app.

---

How to Send Financing Applications to Customers from the HCP Mobile App

With Housecall Pro’s consumer financing option, you and your employees will be able to offer financing while in the field on your mobile devices via the HCP mobile app.

If your job falls within the approved lending range with Wisetack*, you will see a banner on the Job Details page alerting you of the potential monthly payment.

To send financing applications via the HCP mobile app:

- Navigate to the Job Details page for your customer's job and tap 'Pay' from the menu at the top.

- If the total cost of your job falls within the consumer financing range, the option for your customer to check out with financing options will appear.

- To initiate the financing process, tap the 'Qualify Now' button.

- You can also tap the ‘View Details’ hyperlink to show the customer more information on their financing options.

- From here, your customer will need to fill out the application on their own device using the link they were provided.
