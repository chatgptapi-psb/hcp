How to Sign Up For Wisetack Consumer Financing

To sign up for Wisetack consumer financing:

- Go to My money in the top navigation bar of your HCP account on web.

- Select the Consumer financing tab from the menu on the left.

- Click the blue Signup Now button on this page to be redirected to Wisetack's merchant application.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1186332970/659aa7820aac138e18b84edc/My-money-HCP%2B-281-29.png?expires=1763568000&signature=7631066724794e3411537f38845683aa8b47429f9dcf6aaaaf5042dfeccf40d0&req=dSEvEMp9n4hYWfMW1HO4zbPJljAatJCQ2RZcgKqqhBwNXAi6CBTuiQ0j1iog%0ApZJE3hLF2hr%2FXh3DPmw%3D%0A

- Enter your email address, then click the Start button to proceed with your application.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1186332973/6dd1c9c08ab45116aa269010/Wisetack.png?expires=1763568000&signature=d06ce5e35eda6b6c40aff64624f0575e94e6339e972ab0d84793a43b69a6b089&req=dSEvEMp9n4hYWvMW1HO4zY7oyilJ4Wkm0hAGmeQUWmgt%2F4c2PKhlHEDj5bhs%0AYDQEGGAicNb2Xg6d9Rk%3D%0A

- Complete Wisetack's merchant application. Wisetack will reach out in 2-3 business days to collect any remaining information or let you know that you are approved.
