How to Connect Your Bank Account Manually

If you can't find your bank using the search bar or prefer not to connect your bank account using Plaid, you can manually add and verify your bank account by entering your account and routing numbers.

To add your bank account information manually:

- Select 'My money' from the Housecall Pro navigation bar at the top of your screen, and you will automatically be directed to the 'Payouts' tab.

- Click the blue 'Set Up Payments' button.

- Connect Bank on the main page in this tab.

- The Plaid window will then appear on your screen. Click 'Continue' to proceed to search for your bank.

- If you are unable to find your bank or would like to connect your account manually, click the 'X' in the top right corner of the window.

- Since you are doing this flow manually, be sure to double-check that you are using the correct account and routing numbers for your bank account. It is recommended to take a $1 test charge after this setup to ensure you have connected to the right account.
