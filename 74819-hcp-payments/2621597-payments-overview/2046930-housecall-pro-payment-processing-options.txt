Summary: Review payment processing rates, timeframes, and FAQs.

Standard Card Processing Rates & Timeframes

As of March 18, 2020, our standard card processing rates are as follows:

- 2.59% when the card is swiped, tapped, or chipped with our card reader (flat rate).

- 2.99% when the card is entered online by the customer via an invoice emailed to them.

- 3.49% when the card details are manually entered or scanned with the app or entered by your staff in the web portal.
​

Standard processing times for card payouts:

- 5-7 business days for your first payout.
- 2 business days for all subsequent payouts.

---

Instapay Processing Rates & Timeframes

Instapay Processing Rates are an additional 1% fee on top of any of the Standard Card or Bank (ACH) Processing fees:

- 2% when you take a bank (ACH) payment (only available in the U.S.).

- 3.59% when the card is swiped, tapped, or chipped with our card reader (flat rate).

- 3.99% when the card is entered online by the customer via an invoice emailed to them.

- 4.49% when the card details are manually entered or scanned with the app or entered by your staff in the web portal.

Instapay Processing Time: Typically within 30 minutes, up to 24 hours. 
​

---

ACH Processing Rates & Timeframes

Standard Processing Rate for ACH payments: 1% per transaction

Standard processing time for ACH: ACH payments typically take 1-2 business days to complete. Some ACH payments are subject to review, which may prolong their processing time to up to 7 business days.
