Summary: Learn how to disconnect Google Calendar from Housecall Pro.

How to Disconnect Your Google Calendar

If you no longer wish to have your Housecall Pro calendar integrated with your Google calendar, please follow these steps below to disconnect.

---

Disconnect Google Calendar through Housecall Pro

To disconnect your Google Calendar through Housecall Pro:

- Navigate to My Apps by clicking the 3x3 square icon in the top right corner of your HCP account.

- Locate and select the Google Calendar app card.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1095678194/b358e41d55cb5ae635590e9a/Screenshot+2024-06-27+at+10_38_48%E2%80%AFAM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=cc4956686509b2bd5e79d714bad2fe3f552fe624b4f4e78741e6872e7ffe32eb&req=dSAuE895lYBWXfMW1HO4zSxpqeucf8a0quFP2IIWCgRCUef0qayiRy0MEG%2BU%0AWZ1oeDf%2FLCF4%2FHKOAeM%3D%0A

- Click the toggle in the top right section of your screen, and a confirmation window will appear.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1458809952/b9dd0341a307c3eff2c279a0dfda/Screenshot+2025-04-04+at+11_41_22%E2%80%AFAM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=ab012aebfe0578eecd50e2c9b7bc4cfc8b0f9bb840861520a43dffffbae1d772&req=dSQiHsF%2BlIhaW%2FMW1HO4zeyhd0NXS4mBbDcHZd5J0d6%2BIJnc%2B5vg8v3R6c5v%0A49cAHaG4IlRKBZMjA%2Bo%3D%0A

- Click 'Disconnect' in the window to proceed, and your Google Calendar will be disconnected from Housecall Pro. - The toggle should turn grey and show "Disabled" to the right.

Disconnect through Google Calendar

To disconnect the integration through your Google Calendar:

- View your list of calendars under "My calendars" on the left side of your screen and find the calendar titled "Housecall Pro."

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1095689242/1e075beb0d4f6545295144c5/Screenshot+2024-06-27+at+10_53_53%E2%80%AFAM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=003bbd76b13dec60d750652bf1d71235e607445ef0c48f1cbad779f28841c686&req=dSAuE892lINbW%2FMW1HO4zeEXfkk6nJxzsX7X8yRnl8CbaUVarFk9tgJckTtY%0AAR300Dzb9oF9XcpstbA%3D%0A

- Hover over the Housecall Pro calendar and click the More icon (3 dots) that appears to the right.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1095691020/8cdf939f5e79a7a1c677df24/Screenshot+2024-06-27+at+10_55_28%E2%80%AFAM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=c06e00b823302f3445d003502cc2ffec578562eae16290ab2636fe9b400a9aac&req=dSAuE893nIFdWfMW1HO4zenjrsmngHD6k9LovNQtTnKq1e5WOFralWthMB6S%0A4LwJkZ%2FYcm3YRRtfsAc%3D%0A

- Select 'Settings and sharing' from the pop-up menu.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/66143993/267595496335b61e4858c052/Screen+Shot+2018-07-03+at+1.45.40+PM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=25614a5a32d39192c0f24f5bf5f7104542b073b844d9064567be8fd1367f1b08&req=ciYmEsp3lIITWLcX3D%2B5hvLSSu%2B3AEGwdxkeDP5zxtYIrxxSdM6x4nTjNqoZ%0AMcdHnbNV0rMUED9H%0A

- Scroll down to the bottom of the page to the box titled "Remove calendar." Click 'Delete,' and a confirmation window will appear.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1095695174/5d3853fcf316ebc531a27887/Screenshot+2024-06-27+at+10_58_38%E2%80%AFAM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=6860c1c7e1881590b2e01650f12aa8f0820a53f2581ba6dce0856d0a5c0e0cac&req=dSAuE893mIBYXfMW1HO4zUu9LVLPhiTp7Kbhc%2FOO3eAk%2BLh%2BoskutzV3%2FSHo%0AZt0OPgh0UcgSoNIVSMA%3D%0A

- Select 'Permanently delete' to confirm you would like to permanently erase this calendar from your Google Calendar view.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1095696969/aecd7984d9ab4050ca34e237/Screenshot+2024-06-27+at+10_58_59%E2%80%AFAM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=1fb3c3be026595a9e1f6ab55cfe0aa610ef4dd5c940667088889e9c0d2db372c&req=dSAuE893m4hZUPMW1HO4zQ0wJG29qylEWaV4ELAOiTZfsJtcarQviP7AmBCh%0AKYYcJmf8b0PbDtOIBFM%3D%0A
