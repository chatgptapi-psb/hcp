How to Integrate with Google Calendar

Integrating with Google Calendar is easy! If you are logged into Google, please be sure it is the Google Calendar you want to integrate with Housecall Pro.

​
​To integrate with Google Calendar:

- Go to My Apps by clicking the 3x3 square icon in the top right of your Housecall Pro account.

- Click 'Go to App store' from the apps drop-down.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/659517975/d8d964ee004f89ebc1d5762d/Screenshot+2023-01-25+at+6.09.29+PM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=e9614777bd2e3151ebfae7d64c6d729a08a04a7e8f1a9befcad9ca85af206407&req=ciUuE8h5lIZaFb4f3HP0gJiY9GQIYZfkPpyjfWlxKaHMVH7IfE9i6iqtW9cW%0A8mTJ3O5%2B0HPm0bsFTA%3D%3D%0A

- Scroll until you find the Google Calendar app, or use the search bar on this page to search for it. Once you've located the app, click on the View Details button.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h6f82l2e/1232577669/bfe754ce2fb1f4ce24a26ddf5f5d/Housecall_Pro.png?expires=1763568000&signature=27c1e2611082fd4ec99d7ccb161efbfcf56aec2f6b3669c45a9ad30fcc56f570&req=dSIkFMx5modZUPMW1HO4zaGKeUC1b7RGHsNCWR0XKoZfR9QON26gvVmyKvU%2F%0APhKQjxf1aDEczYWryyw%3D%0A

- Once Google Calendar is clicked, a new screen is presented. Click the toggle in the top right corner of the app details page to enable the app.

[Image] https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/659520699/9f84f71a4a4be0944cdd9278/Screenshot+2023-01-25+at+6.15.34+PM.png?expires=1763568000&signature=6e5e7c2d14286a3a55ebb7b14df64f46bed01742fe517656eb88b8fb3aaa361a&req=ciUuE8t%2Bm4hWFb4f3HP0gNKkDesliOgXki%2FfYcc%2BkxbJlpgEBgN1nhmerItl%0AodD4KA6Nlj08Pzz1%2Fg%3D%3D%0A

- Click Connect in the bottom right corner of the window, and that's it! Your jobs, estimates, and events will then be pushed to your Google Calendar.
